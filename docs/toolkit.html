What LocalRIM runs on

The models, software and data sources used to develop and evaluate LocalRIM, with their versions, licences and roles. Document reading, classification and language-model inference all ran locally on a Windows desktop, so no record or its extracted text was sent to an outside model service.

Models

The principal OCR, embedding and language models used in LocalRIM, with their versions, licences and roles. All ran locally.

ModelVersionLicenceUsed forStage
Tesseract5.4.0Apache 2.0Baseline OCR, run twice: on 150-dpi page images and at the PDFs' original resolution.02 · Capture
PaddleOCR (PP-OCRv6 medium)paddleocr 3.7.0; PaddlePaddle GPU 3.2.0; PaddleX 3.7.2Apache 2.0The selected reading engine: page text with word positions and confidence. Its orientation models propose rotations, which the pipeline checks by reading both ways (D-30).02 · Capture, Preparation
GLM-OCRglm-ocr through OllamaMITVision-language OCR, run through Ollama, that re-read the 60 pages in the three difficult scan groups.02 · Capture
Qwen3-Embedding-0.6BQwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6BApache 2.0Represents pages and questions as vectors for search and for classification with logistic regression.03 · Classification, Routing
Qwen3 8Bqwen3:8b through Ollama; temperature 0, fixed seed, thinking offApache 2.0Writes the archive assistant's answers and the review suggestions from retrieved records and filing rules, served locally by Ollama.03 · Routing

Data

Two sources: the client's archive, Philip Morris USA records from the UCSF Industry Documents Library, and the FUNSD benchmark for OCR evaluation.

SourceLicence or termsUsed for
Philip Morris USA records, UCSF Industry Documents LibraryPublic through the 1998 Master Settlement Agreement; UCSF copyright and fair-use policyThe sampled pages and the client's own retention schedules
FUNSD (nielsr/funsd)Non-commercial, research and educational use only; its images (from RVL-CDIP) keep their own copyrightReference transcriptions for measuring OCR word recall and word error rate (Figure 2.4). No FUNSD image is reproduced on this site.
