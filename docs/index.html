When can a local AI system be trusted to file records, and when should it defer to a person?

Salome RamirezM.S. Data Science capstoneResults as of —

LocalRIM investigates how a local AI system can classify records and identify cases requiring human review. Using documents from the Philip Morris USA archive, the project develops and evaluates a pipeline that extracts text, classifies records within a records hierarchy, and retrieves supporting evidence to answer questions and inform review suggestions.

Deciding where a record belongs takes judgment. A CV, for example, may belong to Human Resources or to Research and Development, depending on why it was kept. The document's contents alone may not establish its place in the records hierarchy. This ambiguity is central to the classification problem examined in LocalRIM.
