Dear Bernie:

Yesterday, Amir, Mike and I further discussed how we could go about providing Potential Indices (PIs) and providing Market Share Adjusted (MSA) PIs in response to Jeanne Lincoln’s request made to you earlier this month. As you are aware from our meeting at Market Metrics last week, we thought we had worked out a means to accomplish this by the first week in April. During that discussion we did, however, present two reservations:

• First, although much data validation could be completed by this time, we were all concerned about delivering production/business ready (certified) data in the midst of system development.
• Second, we discussed the fact that Market Metrics had redeveloped the CTS profiles in January/February and that we were planning to insert them into IRDD during the 1995 Demographic update in April. These profiles were reprocessed to take advantage of Charlie Getchell’s newest approach to regional profile development. Without this modification, brands of lower penetration would not be properly demographically represented. Please note the profiles existing in RDM I are not impacted by this because that set is not regionalized.

It is this last concern which prevents us from being able to certify PIs and MSA PIs calculated on the current IRDD CTS profiles.

In summary, the data requested by PM could be available at the end of April when the pre-generated data will be based on the new profiles. Or, if PM approves an interruption in the current IRDD schedule, we could take approximately three weeks to load the new profiles and generate all the PIs and MSA PIs. Then in April, we would turn around and re-do this work after the 1995 Demography is placed in IRDD. The exact impact to the schedule would need to be modeled through the project management software before we could advise PM.

I hope this helps to clarify our concerns.

Sincerely,

Ann P. Raezer
