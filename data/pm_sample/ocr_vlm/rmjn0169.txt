PROGRESS AGAINST PRIORITIES

Marlboro Unlimited/Fall Fest
Continues to be #1 focus with participation in the November elements of sweepstakes eligible participants at 90%, 89% participating in Retailer Rewards and 66% participating in Unlimited Heat. Heat participation was low due to the previous utilization of flex dollars with October's Marlboro flex. Basic utilization of MTC budget is at 50% vs. national average of 30%. Unlimited POS is staying up and we are "refreshing" as needed; current utilization is at 92%. Utilization of OPM headers was low at 73% and this element is receiving additional emphasis.

Retail Masters
Resignings are on track for the most part. We have aggressive action on the part of RJR to knock out exclusive contracts in both Wawa and Store 24. Up-front payments (bonuses?) of significant amounts have been offered. We are meeting with these two accounts on Monday to ascertain details. Both Wawa and Store 24 have seen significant volume increases since signing exclusivity agreements but the magnitude of RJR's offers are large enough that they feel obligated to re-examine their category merchandising.

Coverage/Span
The first phase of DCM offers have been made and acceptance has been good. Of the 15 UM position needed to be replaced, we have 9 DCM acceptances and 1 offer pending. As we move to Phase #2, we expect that the remaining 5 positions will be accommodated within the agreed upon January 1 timeframe.

We had a Region DM meeting this week to discuss increased productivity in 1996. As the units are redeployed, we are examining call frequencies on a territory-by-territory basis to gain TSM and lower management ownership of the process.

AAA
We have solicited and received feedback/input from our retail key account list (25 accounts) and there is almost unanimous support to the direction we're taking. Accounts are appreciative of this collaborative approach to this issue.

Tone of Marketplace
We continue to see 30-50¢ pack on major premium brands of RJR and Lorillard. Buydowns on GPC have been reduced in the South Boston market to 10¢. Will watch to see if this spreads across the Region.
