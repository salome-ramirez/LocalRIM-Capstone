SUBJECT: Indy Lights Sponsorship

Please be advised, we reviewed all of the Marlboro sponsored cars at the Indy Car race this past weekend in Long Beach. Through this review we discovered an additional Indy Lights sponsorship from PM Brazil. The sponsorship is with the following driver/team.

Driver: Gaulter Salles
Team: Brian Steward Racing
Trademarks: Kibbon and Tang on the car.
Marlboro chest patch and helmet ID.

Per the attached correspondence, I thought we had the understanding that there would be four sponsored drivers in the Indy Lights series. Would you please investigate this sponsorship and let me know how/when it was determined that there would be a fifth Marlboro sponsored driver/team.

In addition, per your correspondence dated March 14, it is imperative that PM USA review and approve all driver and team sponsorship prior to any agreements taking place.

I look forward to hearing from you on this matter.
