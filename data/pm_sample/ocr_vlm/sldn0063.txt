The attached are the latest and greatest versions of the Managing FDA Inspection SOP's. They have been through a number of reviews and are now consistent with each other and the training provided by Art Levine, et.al.

The largest change is that the Work Instructions detailing responsibilities for members of the inspection teams have been folded into the procedure and therefore have been eliminated. Use these to prepare your plans and to assist in training. As always, input is accepted and any questions are invited.
