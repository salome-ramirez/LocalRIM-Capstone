SUBJECT: Virginia Slims “Dueling Divas” Music Program

The Virginia Slims “Dueling Divas” music program is continuing its success this year in 11 markets. There are two new markets in 1999, San Francisco and Denver, and we are anticipating conducting a Hispanic test market in Houston that will feature Tejano or Salsa bands.

Attached is a list of program dates for all 11 markets and the bar night program schedule for the four Wave I markets. Please note that all schedules are subject to change. In case of a cancellation or change, you will be notified via e-mail by Donna Hingst.

This program is designed to build awareness of Virginia Slims among young adult female smokers (legal age to 24), while providing opportunities to aspiring female singers/musicians, which is consistent with Virginia Slims’ heritage.

Local female bands will compete for a $5,000 prize. There will be a “Dueling Divas” listening booth where bar patrons, 21 years of age and older, can vote for their favorite band each night of the program. There will also be name generation activities and pack sales during the entire evening. Virginia Slims will once again be partnering the “Dueling Divas” music program with a local AIDS/Hunger charity in each market. Each vote represents a $5.00 donation (up to $10,000) by Virginia Slims to the designated local charity.

The 1999 Virginia Slims Dueling Divas program is scheduled for 5 weeks in each market. There will be 4 weeks of competition (3 bar nights per week, including 1 live performance by a band each week) and a final “Showcase” featuring condensed performances by all of the competing bands. The winning band will be announced at the end of the Showcase event. A formal presentation of our charity donation will also be done at the Showcase.

No special activities are required by the field sales force; however, we welcome your attendance at any of the events. If you would like to use a showcase event as an opportunity to entertain key retailers, please call Donna Hingst at (917) 663-2461 or send an e-mail no later than 3 weeks before the event.

If you have any questions, please contact Donna as well.
