From: J. E. Wickham

Subject: Macdonald Tobacco Inc. - Canada

Macdonald Tobacco Inc., the recently acquired Canadian subsidiary of R. J. Reynolds Tobacco Company, has begun to distribute Canadian cigarettes in the United States. Export 'A', Macdonald Menthol, and Macdonalds' Export are the brands obtained from a local jobber in Richmond, Virginia.

The Export 'A' and Macdonald Menthol 85's are packaged in flip-top boxes with a U. S. Health warning label pasted over the Canadian health warning. The Macdonalds' Export cigarettes are 72 mm long and packaged in a slide & shell type package and have the Canadian health warnings on the side.

The blend of these cigarettes contains no expanded tobacco; however, the blend does contain stems at the 8% level and the stems appear to have been expanded to some degree.

The analytical data given in the attached table were taken from the Canadian Cigarette Intelligence Report Number 18 dated May 15, 1973.
