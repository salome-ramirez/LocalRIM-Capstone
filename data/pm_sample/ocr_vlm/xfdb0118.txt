EXPERIMENT

Effects causal by smoke from cigarettes of different composition on the respiratory tract of rats. The response of the laryngeal epithelium and changes of number and function of free lung cells was of special interest.

Animals: male CD-rats, 60 per group; age on start of inhalation approx. 53 d

Smoke: cigarettes 2R1, Xj, ADN and ADX; dilutions o.5 : 145, o.5 : 135, 1 : 145 and 1 : 50; TPM between o.21 and o.31 mg/l; nicotine between o.2 and 10 microgram/l; CO between 156 and 464 ppm

Exposure: 21o and 42o min/d; 42 consecutive days with 3 days training; dissections after 7, 14, 21, 35 and 42 d and after additional 21 d of recovery
