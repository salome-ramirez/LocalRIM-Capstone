The Dependence of the mutagenic effect of Cigarette Condensate on the Age of the Condensate

One test was carried out for each of the condensates of 2R1F, SHIM-5 and 2R1 cigarettes.

Results of the 3 Tests

For each of the 3 cigarettes the following statement is valid: "With a type I error probability less than 0.005 we can say: during the 2nd through to the 8th week the number of revertants decreases with increasing age of the condensate."

Remarks

The following statement is also valid for each of the cigarettes: "With a type I error probability less than 0.005 we can say: during the 2nd through to the 6th week the number of revertants decreases with increasing age of the condensate."

During the 2nd through to the 6th week all values fall strictly monotonously. From the 6th to the 8th week this trend is interrupted. 6 of the 9 values increase. One can assume that the mutagenic effect of the condensate will, at least for a time, not decrease further from about the 8th week.
