Comments to Hausmann's comments:

1) We found no citation about nicotine concentrations in the nasal cavity during smoking. I would like to go only into this discussion if it is the desire of the refreses.

2) The citation refers to both, olfcatory and trigeminal responses could be mediated by nicotinic Ach receptors.

3) We add 'burning sensation' some lines later in the new version of the manuscript, defining olfcatory and trigeminal concentrations.

4) Nicotinic Ach receptors, desensitization, tolerance...: Interesting point, I thought long about it. Generally the lack of differences in thersholds and discrimination between smokers and non-smokers does not point to the expression of ACh receptors induced by smoking history. I decided not to go into this discussion because we have no peripheral functional and biochemical data to lead this discussion. This means we need PCR analysis about Ach receptor expression and functional NMP recordings in smokers and non-smokers. Nice idea for a new study. Two years ago somebody told me at Achems something about a PCR-study comparing Ach receptor expression in the nasal cavity between smokers and non-smokers. I think he found no differences between smokers and non-smokers (unpublished results).
