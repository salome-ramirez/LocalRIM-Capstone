Subject: B&H PaperSelect Launch Update

Flash
Ongoing detail analysis of existing B&H finish goods inventory levels reveals critically low volumes of various packings at the planned production start-up date of March 26, 2001. In order to prevent major hardships on Production Planning, Manufacturing and Inventory Deployment areas, it is the recommendation and approval of the DRB to move the production start date to April 2, 2001.

All other milestones up-stream remain the same. Please inform others in your area.
