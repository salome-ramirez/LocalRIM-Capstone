EXPOSURE TO ETS AND HEART DISEASE

In their review of the data on exposure to ETS and coronary heart disease the NHMRC review panel concluded in 1997 that:

Passive smoking may increase the risk of IHD but other, non-causal, explanations are possible.

In the NHMRC's executive summary the conclusion stated was:

On the available evidence, however, it was not possible to conclude with certainty that passive smoking is a cause of heart attack or death from coronary heart disease, or any other type of cardiovascular disease.

I enclose a copy of a paper published in the latest edition of the British Medical Journal which presents the results of a meta-analysis of the available data on exposure to ETS and coronary heart disease, and a copy of a BMJ editorial commenting on the meta-analysis.
