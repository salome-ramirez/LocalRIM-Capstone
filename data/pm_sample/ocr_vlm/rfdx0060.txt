SYNOPSIS:

AN ACT CONCERNING DISCLOSURE OF INGREDIENTS IN TOBACCO PRODUCTS.

TEXT: Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Assembly convened:

(NEW) (a) Not later than December 1, 1999, and annually thereafter, each manufacturer of a tobacco product sold or offered for sale in this state shall file with the Commissioner of Public Health a report that lists, for each brand of such product sold, the following information: (1) The name or identity of any chemical known to cause cancer or reproductive toxicity as listed in the California Health and Safety Code section 25249.8 and such other chemicals, including neurotoxins or addictive substances, as the commissioner may list in regulations which the commissioner may adopt in accordance with the provisions of chapter 54 of the general statutes; and (2) the nicotine yield ratings that accurately predict nicotine intake for the average consumer of such product. The manufacturer shall also provide such information within sixty days of any change in the ingredients of such tobacco product.

(b) The commissioner shall publish such information at least annually.

(c) Any manufacturer that violates any provision of this section shall be lined not more than ten thousand dollars and each day that such violation continues shall be a separate offense.

Statement of Purpose: To require tobacco companies to provide a list of all hazardous ingredients contained in their products to the Commissioner of Public Health who shall compile such information and disseminate it to the general public.
