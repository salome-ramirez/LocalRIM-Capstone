Cooling the water for for instance the chilled beams, is possible by using an earthpump. From -1 m down to -100 m in the earth it is always about +10 °C. When we are drilling a pipe until -75 m deep we have a tubelength to cool the water of about 150 m, which is enough to cool water of about 18°C down to 12°C (pic. 5). One earthpump can go up to 7 kw. This can cover +/- 140 m2 of chilled ceiling (supposed that the ceiling covers 2/3 of the total local cooling demand).

For this project we need about 5 boreholes diam. 100 mm.
