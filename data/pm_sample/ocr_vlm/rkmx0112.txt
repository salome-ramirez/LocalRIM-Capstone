March 17, 1978

Mr. James C. Bowling
Senior Vice President
Philip Morris Inc.
100 Park Avenue
New York, New York 10017

Dear Jim:

Status of "Facts About the Smoking Controversy" text of which you provided to us:

We have made a very few minor editorial changes and have obtained our usual legal clearances. We are now going to typesetting.

Our first press run will cover our earlier needs plus 150,000 for your company plus samples with which to solicit orders from the other member companies and other organizations.

Needless to say, this kind of preparation work by your people is terribly helpful to all of us.

One other note -- we are changing the title to avoid confusion with certain other publications we have. The pamphlet will probably appear under the title "On Smoking."

Cordially,

William Kloepfer, Jr.
