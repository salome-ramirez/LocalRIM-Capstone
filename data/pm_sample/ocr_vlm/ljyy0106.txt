TEST PROCEDURE

The expanded tobacco being discharged from the expansion tower had the flow split into two streams with one going to the Proctor and Schwartz reordering machine and the second stream going to the conditioning cylinder. This allowed for a side-by-side comparison of the reordering machine and conditioning cylinder on essentially the same expanded tobacco. Listed below are the samples obtained during the test.

I. R & D

A. A sample is to be obtained at the following locations every 30 minutes for a sieve test.

(1) After vibrating conveyor
(2) Before conditioning cylinder
(3) After conditioning cylinder
(4) Before reordering machine
(5) After reordering machine

B. After the sieve samples have been obtained, they are to be equilibrated to approximately 11.0% O.V.

II. Q. C.

A. The location, sample frequency, and tests to be performed are listed below:

LOCATION SAMPLE FREQUENCY TESTS TO BE PERFORMED

(1) Before cond. cyl. one sample every 15 min. O.V., S.V., NH$_3$
(2) Before reo. mach. one sample every 15 min. O.V., S.V., NH$_3$
(3) After cond. cyl. one sample every 15 min. O.V., C.V., S.V., NH$_3$, Siev
(4) After reo. mach. one sample every 15 min. O.V., C.V., S.V., NH$_3$, Siev

B. All samples are to be tested as soon as possible.
