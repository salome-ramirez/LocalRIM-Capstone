BASIC JOB PROCEDURE

I. Potential Hazards

A. Unconscious and/or death could result from working in an oxygen deficient/toxic atmosphere without respiratory protection.

B. Low overhead obstructions and/or congested work area could result in lacerations, concussion, and/or contusions.

C. Slips and falls could result due to wet or uneven working surfaces.

D. Fire and/or explosion will result when gases are present in their flammable ranges and an ignition source is present.

E. Bodily injury could result from slips and falls from elevated surfaces (ascending/descending).

F. Laceration, fractures, and/or amputation could result if jewelry becomes caught on stationary object.
