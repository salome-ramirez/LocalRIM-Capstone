Subject: Evaluation of Cigarettes Containing WS-14 as an Additive

Two cigarettes as per your request have been evaluated for pyrolysis products arising from the addition of WS-14. Each cigarette employed in the study is described below:

1. Merit 85 with WS-14 7244-119B added to the filler at a level of 3.1mg WS-14/cigarette.
   IBM Code DOBMQ

2. Merit 85 with WS-14 7565-11 added to the paper at a level of 5.5mg WS-14/cigarette.
   IBM Code DOBMR

3. Merit 85 with no WS-14 added to either filler or paper.
   IBM Code DOFQM

No. 3 above or the control cigarette was used to establish the background which was later subtracted from samples Nos. 1 and 2. All smokings were performed by Karen Barlow of Project 2525, and mass spectrometry services were provided by Project 0108. Analytical parameters, previously established by 2501 in the 14C-WS-14 studies, were used to obtain the following results.
