Marketing

Accord Consumer Service and Marketing Research:

• Accord Consumer Service has received 699 incoming phone calls. Of the 96 calls in the past two weeks, nearly 1/2 continue to be for retail location; this is in addition to the 169 hits we received during this two-week period on the voice prompter for retail location. The total number of owners requesting Money Back Guarantee is 81—10 requests in the past two weeks. Of the reasons for requesting their money back given by adult smokers of brands greater than 6mg tar, about half were specifically taste/flavor-related and about a fourth were inconvenience issues; for smokers of ultra low tar brands, about 60% were inconvenience-related and less than a fourth were taste/flavor issues. We received 9 additional Warranty Cards for a total of 216, of which 170 are active.

• In the past two weeks, fulfillment has shipped 16 spare items to RST20 purchasers, primarily lighter cartridges (5 orders totaling 10 cartridges). Two replacement chargers were sent to purchasers.

• From 8/17/98 to 1/19/99, the Accord Complaint Handling Unit processed 46 alleged product failures (and one mailing list escalation): 20 relating to chargers, 13 to lighters, 5 batteries, 5 lighter cartridges, 2 cigarettes, and 1 lighter casing. To date, 37 of these allegedly defective components have been returned by consumers and sent to MDF for diagnostics.

• Adult smokers who have owned an Accord kit for at least 8 weeks and who have not contacted ACS for free lighter cartridges or components are being recruited for in-person and telephone interviews. Similarly “inactive” transfer warranty smokers also are being interviewed. We are interested in gaining insights into these smokers’ issues, challenges and overall perspectives of smoking Accord.
