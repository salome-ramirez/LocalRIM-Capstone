CONSUMER ORDERS MISSING INFORMATION

Goal: To address concern of consumers who call regarding something they left out of their order (i.e. check, miles, no signature, etc.)

“If you omitted something, your order will be returned to you. Please follow the instructions in the letter you will receive.

If consumer is concerned that they missed the deadline:

“If your order was postmarked by December 31, 1994, you did not miss the deadline.”
