PROJECTS 1705, 3101, 3102
MEDICAL PRODUCTS DEVELOPMENT

PERIOD COVERED BY REPORT: March 26 - April 25, 1970
DATE OF REPORT: April 28, 1970
PROJECT LEADER: David J. Taylor

I. Exploratory Research (1705)

A. Unit Antiseptic

By the addition of 0.75% titanium dioxide, the polypropylene tube has been converted from translucent to opaque white. In the view of project personnel, the white tube in conjunction with the two-color label has an excellent "antiseptic" appearance.

Labels for the POL samples are now in-house so the remaining barrier to production of test units is control of the score depth within acceptable limits. Variations in "hot knife" temperature and exposure are being evaluated microscopically in addition to weight loss and tensile strength measurements.

Results from the "closed patch" skin test are awaited from Woodard Research.

II. Sutures (3101)

A. 3rd Generation Package

The six month shelf life evaluation of the experimental packages has been completed and a complete report will be issued by May 8, 1970.

Needle corrosion test samples were received from ASR MI - Lukens but the use of an incorrect tubing solution has necessitated a remake.

B. Suture Specifications

Method development for tubing fluids with the Lukens' gas chromatograph is in the final stages and the draft specification is 90% completed. An analytical method for diethylaminoethanol in tubing solution has been completed. The overall tubing solution program is scheduled for completion by May 18, 1970.
