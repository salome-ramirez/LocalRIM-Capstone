And Philip Morris has announced plans to conduct research on a new battery powered smoking device that burns only when puffed. The new system, consisting of an ultra-low tar cigarette causing no side stream smoke, no ashes, nor lingering odor (sic). It’s designed to be smoked while partially inside what Philip Morris calls an "electronic puff activated lighter."
