INTRODUCTION AND SUMMARY

Certain Defendants seek summary judgment as to the State's antitrust claims in Count 5 of the Second Amended Complaint. The State alleges that Defendants conspired to restrain the trade of tobacco products in violation of ORS 646.725. Defendants are entitled to summary judgment on that claim because the State cannot prove the necessary antitrust standing.
