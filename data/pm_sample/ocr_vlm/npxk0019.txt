RACE SUMMARY
NEW ENGLAND 200
NEW HAMPSHIRE INTERNATIONAL SPEEDWAY
LOUDON, NEW HAMPSHIRE
August 17-20, 1995
Submitted by: Susan Bradshaw
Chris Mears

Marlboro Team Ponske drivers Emerson Fittipaldi and Al Unser, Jr. qualified sixteenth and seventeenth respectively. Andre Ribiero took his first Indy car career pole and went on to win his first race and the $75,000 Marlboro Pole Award "win from the pole" bonus. Michael Andretti finished second followed by Al Unser, Jr.

Al Unser, Jr. moved from tenth position to sixth after his first pit stop and moved into third on lap 127 where he remained to take his third consecutive podium finish and his fourth podium finish of the season. He is currently in fourth place in the PPG Cup Point Standings with 111 points.

Emerson Fittipaldi worked his way through the field to fifth position by lap 155 where he finished and scored 10 PPG Cup points. He is currently in twelfth position with 61 points.

Since Andre Ribiero won from the pole, the Marlboro Pole Award bonus for a "win from the pole" will be worth $15,000 in Vancouver.

TIME OF RACE: 1 hour; 34 min.; 36.192 sec.
AVERAGE SPEED: 134.203 mph
MARGIN OF VICTORY: 14.482 sec.
