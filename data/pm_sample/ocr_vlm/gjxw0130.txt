DEAR MR. CULLMAN:

YOUR COMPANY AND THE CIGARETTE INDUSTRY ITSELF, CONTINUE TO OVERLOOK A PRIME WEAPON IN THE CANCER-SMOKING CONTROVERSE WITH THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT.

NOWHERE IN THE WELTER OF WORDS SINCE THE INCEPTION OF THIS DISPUTE HAVE YOU OR YOUR INDUSTRY EMPHASIZED THAT IF THERE ARE HEALTH HAZARDS, THEY ARE DUE TO INHALING—NOT TO THE ACT OF CIGARETTE SMOKING PER SE. COUNTLESS PIPE AND CIGAR SMOKERS DO, OR DID, LIKE MYSELF, INHALE THOSE FORMS OF TOBACCO. THEREFORE ALL FORMS OF TOBACCO, IF INHALED, MIGHT BE HAZARDOUS TO HEALTH, NOT ONLY CIGARETTES.

CONSEQUENTLY, IT IS UNFALR TO SHRILL CRITICISM AT CIGARETTES ALONE. SIMILAR POINTS OF ATTACK SHOULD BE MADE IN RESISTING FTC INSISTENCE ON LABELLING. IF YOU LABEL ONE TOBACCO FORM YOU SHOULD LABEL ALL FORMS. AND SUCH LABELS, IF ANY, SHOULD BE CONFINED TO THE METHOD OF USE—INHALING. IF YOU INHALE, IT MIGHT INJURE YOUR HEALTH. THERE ARE ANY NUMBER OF PRODUCTS SOLD DAILY IN SUPER MARKETS THAT ILLUSTRATE THIS POINT. IT CAN BE ARGUED—AS THEIR LABELS ATTEST—THAT THEY TOO ARE DANGEROUS TO HEALTH.

THE FOREGOING, WHILE GENERATING THE SEEDS OF A RESISTANCE CAMPAIGN, WILL NOT, OF COURSE, WIN YOUR BATTLE COMPLETELY. BUT IT IS A SPARK OF WISDOM TO GIVE AN INCH TODAY RATHER THAN A FOOT TOMORROW. THIS, YOU WILL RECALL, MANAGEMENT FOUND OUT SOME YEARS AGO WITH THE RESULT THAT THEY REFUSED TO ACT ON THAT KNOWLEDGE AND BIG LABOR NOW SITS IN OUR POWER ECONOMY WHERE BIG BUSINESS ONCE SAT.

CHEERFULLY,

DM/VA

Mr. Joseph F. Cullman
PRESIDENT
