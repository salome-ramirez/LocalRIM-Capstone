From: André Clodong

Subject: Great American Smoke Out

The 'Great American Smoke Out' received scant coverage in the Belgian press. We have identified one article with photo in regional Flemish daily Belang van Limburg, published on Saturday, 22 November (see copy).

(Please note we monitor Belgian national and major newspapers daily. We receive other clippings within a week of publication).

Following our distribution of the news release on the Great American Smoker Kits, Belgian state-owned radio stations Radio 21 and RTBF One aired an interview with Mike Horst (see transcripts).

Best regards,

André Clodong
