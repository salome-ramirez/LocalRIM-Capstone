REGION 1 SUGGESTED "SCRIPT" FOR CONSUMER SURVEY
COUPLED WITH SWITCH SELL

Rep - "Hello, I represent Philip Morris and I notice you are a smoker."

Consumer - "Yes I am"

Rep - "Would you like to be eligible to receive free cigarette samples, discount coupons, and other promotional offers mailed directly to your home such as these (show current materials such as Marlboro catalog, etc.) with no obligation on your part to ever purchase anything?"

Consumer - "yes, I would" [or they will have some objection - probably] "but I don’t smoke your brand."

Rep - "That’s fine with us. We would like to be able to send you the promotional materials anyway. That way if you do decide to try a different brand in the future, you may be able to save yourself some money or you can give the information to one of your friends who does smoke the brand. It only takes a minute or so to fill out the card."

Rep - [hands customer card and thanks them.]

Consumer - [takes card and walks away or looks at card and objects to the amount of information requested.]

Rep - [If customer objects to the amount of information requested, handle with] "The reason we are asking these questions is to insure that if we send you samples, coupons, etc., we send you items that would be of interest and use to you. We want to do our best to insure we don’t waste your time or ours. Please take it and fill it out. I think you will be happy with the results." [Hands customer the survey and thanks them.]

[If customer still does not want the survey, thank them for their time and attention and tell them to please come back and get one if they change their mind.]

If at any time the customer walks away do not pursue them, but thank them for taking the time to talk with you.

END OF CONSUMER SURVEY SCRIPT, BEGINNING OF SWITCH SELL.

IF AT ANY TIME DURING THE SWITCH SELL THE CONSUMER SHOWS ANY RESISTANCE, END THE ATTEMPT TO SWITCH SELL. The major objective is to get the information on the survey card.

[Have surveys on clip boards with pencil or pen on string available to fill out the survey. If the customer is in a hurry, allow them to take the survey with them, but the object is to switch sell them while they are filling out the form.]

REP - "I noticed you smoke (consumer’s brand)", or "What brand are you smoking now?"

Consumer - "I smoke - (brand.)"

REP - "That’s a good brand. Would you consider trying our (brand from brand selection card) today?" [Give benefit statements for brand and inform them of any brand promotions that may apply to their selection.]

Consumer - "Okay" [or some objection.]

Rep - [Handle the objection by agreeing with the consumer’s right to purchase whatever they want or agreeing with whatever the core of their objection was and close with], "but I really think you may prefer our brand. It is the (repeat major benefit statements)."

If customer still objects - thank him or her for taking the time to talk with you and collect the survey or make sure the consumer takes a blank one with them.

REP - [If consumer has no objection or when you have over come their objection - offer to exchange the pack or carton for them while they finish filling out the survey.] "If you like, I will put that (pack or carton) back on the shelf for you while you are filling out the survey and get you one of (brand we are switching them to.)" [Make the switch, thank the consumer, and collect the survey. Drop collected surveys in nearest mailbox.]

NOTE: The Background Information on PM Brands section in your ASK Manual contains benefit statements for our brands.
