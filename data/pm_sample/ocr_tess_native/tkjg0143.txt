PROJECT TITLE: Flush Fluted! Single Filter (FFM-4):
No change since last reporting period.?
PROJECT TITLE: Evaluation of Eastman 1830E Polyethylene
Regular Fluted (R.F.), square fluted (SF-18) and turbo-
fluted (TF-18) filter rods have been sucessfully extruded using
1830E. The RF and SF-18: rods were combined to produce a
Multifilter and a single filter cigaret respectively. With
all these tests complete the: evaluation report has been
issued. *
PROJECT TITLE: Foam Filters
The final demonstration of foam filter extrusion was
conducted on January 28, 1972. The efficiency achieved
during this. test was 92.6%.°
The project completion report will be ready in February
1972.
