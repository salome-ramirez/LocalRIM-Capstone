EXPERIMENT
Effects causal by smoke from cigarettes of different composition on the
respiratory tract of rats. The response of the laryngeal epithelium and
changes of number and function of free lung cells was of special interest.
Animals:: male CD-rats, 60 per group; age on start of inhalation approx.
53 d ;
Smoke: cigarettes 2R1, Xj, ADN and ADX; dilutions 0.5 : 145, 0.5: 135,
1: 145 and 1 : 507 TPM between 0.21 and 0.31 mg/l; nicotine
between 0.2 and to microgram/1; CO between 156 and 464 ppm
Exposure: 210 and 420 min/d; 42 consecutive days with 3 days training;
dissections after 7, 14, 21, 35 and 42 d and after additional
21 d of recovery eo — D a
