OBJECTIVE
To carry out an interlaboratory comparison of the determination of
MH-30 by HPLC [1] and to compare HPLC figures with results obtained
by the standard colorimetric method ISO-4876.
PARTICIPATING LABORATORIES
The following five laboratories took part to the study:
— PM-USA1 (CPA laboratory) : HPLC method
— PM-USA2 (S.S. Yang) : HPLC method
— PM-Europe : HPLC method
— Universal Leaf Tobacco : HPLC method
— INBIFO : ISO-4876
SAMPLES
Tobacce samples containing MH-30 at five different levels ranging
from 40 to 170 ppm were selected. For each level, 6 replicate
analyses were carried out. Tobaccos were analyzed over two days as
described in the note accompanying the samples [2].
