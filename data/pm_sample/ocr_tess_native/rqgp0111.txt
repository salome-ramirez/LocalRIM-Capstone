SCIENTIFIC COMMISSION
Report cof the meeting
5th - 6h May 1988
The meeting of the Scientific Commission was opened at 9 a.m. by Vice-
President Ruy de Villas Boas who pointed out that President T. Osdene
asked him by phone call to chair this meeting. T.Osdene is unfortunately
unable to be here because of an important law-suit he is obliged to
attend.
R. de Villas Boas proposed to handle the convening of the meeting to
J. Chouteau, considering his many years of experience, if he wished so.
J. Chouteau declined this invitation and associated himself with the wish
that R.de Villas Boas should chair the meeting.
R. de Villas Boas welcomed the participants, particularly the Chinese
delegation and Dr.T.C.Tso who actively collaborates to the preparation
of the Guangzhou Congress.
He thanked Philip Morris Germany and H. Reif for the excellent program
organized on the occasion of this meeting.
All Commission's members, except T. Osdene and Chatti, attended the meeting, . __
as well as the Smoke and Technology Group's Secretaries and the Coresta
General Secretary (attendance list in appendix 1). The Chinese delegation
and T.C.Tso, on one hand, D.T.Westcott on the other hand, participated to
a part of the meeting.
The President proposed an amendment to the Agenda : point 11 (Report on
ISO/TC 126) should be discussed immediately after point 8 (Report of the
task force Coresta n° 10) and point 4 (Prize and Grants) should be discussed
together with point 5 (Coresta Awards).
The Agenda, thus modified, is adopted.
