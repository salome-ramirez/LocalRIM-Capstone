Subject: Low Weight Cigarette Mainstream Smoke Analysis - Basic Components -
Activity 2A12
I, Intr ign:
Cigarette models with various tobacco filler weights were analyzed by gas
chromatography (GC) to ascertain differences in selected basic components of mainstream smoke
relative to the reduction in filler weight. The six sample codes, cigarette target weights, filler
target weights, and average puff counts are shown in Table I (1). The cigarette targeted filler
weights range from 650 mg to 850 mg, with 750 mg as the normal filler weight of a cigarette.
This range reflects a 200 mg difference between the lowest (DL 22) and highest weight model
DL 27) and includes a + 100 mg weight difference relative to the 750 mg cigarette model.
Analytical data pertaining to these cigarette models have been reported (1).

Il. Method:
All cigarette models were conditioned at 75°F and 60% relative humidity for at least 48
hours prior to use. Four runs, using ten cigarettes each, were made at random for each model.
Prior to smoking, all cigarettes were marked at tipping plus 3 mm. Mainstream smoke from these
models was generated and collected using a 20-port Borgwaldt smoking machine with an
electrostatic trap (2). Electrostatic tubes were weighed before and after each run to determine the
weight of smoke condensate. During smoke condensate collection, the output voltage was-set at
