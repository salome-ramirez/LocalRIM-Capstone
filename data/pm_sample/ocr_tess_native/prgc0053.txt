ATTORNEY-CLIENT COMMUNICATION
ORANDUM
RE: QBRI Report
We are forwarding the revised QBRI draft time line. Most of it reveals target
dates and in-process activities internal to QBRI. This is how we understand the time line as
it reflects QBRI "deliverables" for our external, legal review:
1. On January 26, QBRI will forward to us drafts of twelve portions of the report
- that have been verified/vetted with the PM subject area quality person. These are the five
"review process reports” (leaf, cigarette manufacturing, processing plants, primary and
distribution) and seven "stand-alone" reports on QSR elements (management respansibility,
internal audits, design control, document control, complaints, purchasing/incoming materials
and statistics). QBRI seeks our comments on these materials by February 3.
2. QBRI will not forward other portions of its report to us for review before it
forwards the entire draft report on February 16. That draft will contain, among other things,
nine "dependent reports” that cover the remaining QSR elements, the integrated analysis, and
the recommendations and rationale, Legal review of the draft report is scheduled to be
completed on February 26. QBRI plans to incorporate comments and otherwise revise its
draft during the period from February 26 through March 24. It will then provide a revised
draft report (which QBRI views as its final report as a prelude to the initiation of the PM
options development and implementation process).
3, We understand the Walt Gundaker will participate in the QBRI internal review
of the draft materials before they are sent for legal review. ,
4. We understand that EduQuest, which has been engaged in a similar preliminary
drafting and PM verification/vetting process, will also forward its draft report for legal
review on February 16. It is presumed that the target for that review is also February 26.
Given this schedule, we should agree on a process for coordinating legal reviews/
comments to the consultants in response to both the January 26 and the February 16 draft
