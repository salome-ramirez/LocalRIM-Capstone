ADVERTISING AGREEMENT
THIS AGREEMENT is made as of this day of August, 1990,
by and between PHILIP MORRIS U.S.A. ("PM"), a division of Philip
Morris Incorporated, a corporation of the Commonwealth of
Pennsylvania, with its principal office at 120 Park Avenue, and the
NEW JERSEY SPORTS AND ı EXPOSITION AUTHORITY ("Authority"), a body
corporate and politic of the State of New Jersey with its principal
office in East Rutherford, New Jersey 07073.
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, Authority. is responsible for the planning, design,
construction, operation and use of the Meadowlands Sports Complex
("Complex") located in the Hackensack Meadowlands and may make and
enter into all contracts, leases, and agreements for the use and
eccupancy of the complex or part thereof that are necessary or
incidental to the performance of its duties and exercise of its
powers;
WHEREAS, among the facilities which Authority owns and operates
at the Complex are Giants Stadium ("Stadium), Brendan Byrne
Meadowlands Arena ("Arena") and the Meadowlands Racetrack
("Racetrack"), and each of these facilities contains space which may
be used for advertising purposes;
WHEREAS, Authority has exclusive right to license the rights to
advertise on the space available at the Stadium, Arena and Racetrack
and it desires to grant such license to PM for fair and equitable
consideration; - - -
WHEREAS, PM desires to advertise its tobacco products at the
Stadium, Arena and Racetrack through and including September 30, 2001
and is willing and able to pay fair and equitable consideration for
the privilege of doing so; and
WHEREAS, the parties desire to enter into an agreement defining
their respective rights, duties and liabilities relating to the
licensing of certain advertising rights at the Stadium, Arena and
Racetrack;
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises and the
mutual covenants contained herein, and intending to be legally
bound, the parties agree as follows:
1. Term:
This Agreement shall become effective upon execution and,
unless sooner terminated in accordance with the provisions in Section
7 whereof, will end on September 30, 2001 (the "Contract Period").
Each October 1 - September 30 period during the Contract Period shall
hereinafter be referred to as a “Contract Year". ,
