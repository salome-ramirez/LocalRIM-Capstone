P.M. U.S.A. has purchased approximately 1.8 million pounds of pickings from the pool. These
will be converted to material suitable for processing into sheet at Park 500. The conversion is
expected to be conducted by U.L.T. at Wilson during February 1994:
The following ground rules are proposed and will'be finalized once processing has started:
The lamina material will be threshed down as small as possible with no more than 10%
larger than 1/2". Stem and fines content of this material is unimportant. This material will
be designated as PBTBL. Oven volatiles should be at 12%. Packing instructions and lot
. numbers are being issued to ULT.
Material smaller than 16 mesh will be packed as BVF with normal standards and packing
instructions.
Stem material willibe evaluated for size and will probably be classified into SVST and:
BDM with normal standards and packing instructions being used.
A tnal run will be conducted before processing is started. Results from the tnal and samples will
be made.available to Park 500 for evaluation.
