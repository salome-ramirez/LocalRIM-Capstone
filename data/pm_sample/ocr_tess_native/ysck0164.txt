SUBJECT: TRION TONIZER CONVERSION
The attached requisition for $271,876 is for the purchase of
Trion ionizer conversion kits for Trion electronic filters
plantwide.
The function of the Trions is to remove dust particles and
contaminates from the room air in the processing areas.
However, due to electrical failures the Trions operate at
only 46% efficiency, thus removing less than half the partic-
ulate they were designed to filtrate.
Broken ionization wires is the root cause of most Trion
electrical failures. These key components of the electronic
filter break at a rate of 400 per year, triggering approxi-
mately 100 transformer failures annually. Each year Park 500
spends more than $60,000 in replacement parts and over 1000
labor hours for ionizer wires and related failures.
Technical representatives of Trion Incorporated have recom-
mended replacing the old technology ionizer wires with newer
design long-lasting, spiked ionizer plates. Discussions with
Philip Morris personnel who have experience with spiked
ionizers indicate that they are essentially maintenance free.
If this requisition is approved by October 16, ionizer
conversion may begin early in November and all units could be
converted by the end of the first quarter of 1993.
