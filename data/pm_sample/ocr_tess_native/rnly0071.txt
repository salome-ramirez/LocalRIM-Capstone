43 OHIO: In Plea Bargain, Oaker Pleads Guilty On Two
Counts
Cleveland Plain Dealer's Diemer & Epstein report, ex-Rep. Mary Oaker (D) has
agreed to plead guilty 9/34 to two misdemeanor election law violations, “bringing to an
abrupt end a criminal case" involving her failed 92 reelection campaign “and her misuse of

charges for accepting her guilty plea, As part of the plea agreement, Oaker “admitted
pumping” $16K into her ‘92 campaign, using the names of others to avold legal
contribution limits. “She also acknowledged "contacting people to see whether those
people wauld be willing to allow their names, or the names of people they know, to be
