Smoker Survey
Data Requirements
Business Summary
The Smoker Survey was last updated in 1993. Since that time there have been several
attempts to change and modify the questions however none of the versions were approved.
In light of an initiative to recertify the direct marketing database, new emphasis in being
placed on creating a Smoker Survey that captures accurate, actionable and pertinent
information.
Data Requirements
The majority of the questions that are being asked in the new Smoker Survey can be
mapped directly to data fields used by the old Smoker Survey. Whenever possible, it is
recommended that new fields be created within the 700 Byte Record in order to reduce the
number of programming changes required by Richmond IS and the keying vendors.
Currently, there are approximately 15 bytes of “filler” (i.e. unused space) available within
the 700 Byte Record. (1 Byte = 1 Character) A break down of each question as well as
recommended modifications are listed below.
1. Please fill in exactly what you as for when buying your regular brand -- that is, when
buying the brand you smoke most often.
700 Byte Record, Field 41, Regular Brand
No Change.
2. Is your regular brand...? * Menthol * Non-Menthol
700 Byte Record, Field 43, Flavor - allowable values M (menthol), N (non-menthol). U
(unknown, multiple answers checked)
No Change.
3. Do you usually buy...? e Hard Pack/Box © Soft Pack
700 Byte Record, Field 46, Packaging - allowable values S (soft pack), H (hard pack/box).
B (both), U (unknown, multiple answers checked)
No Change.
4. Is your regular brand...? * Regular/King Size * 100’s e120’s
700 Byte Record, Field 42, Length - allowable values N (less than 85’s. nonfilter), K
(regular. king size, 85’s), L (100’s, Jong), X (120s, extra long), U (unknown, multiple
answers checked)
No Change.
