Founded in 1951, Suburban Associates has become one of the country’s ten largest privately
owned field services. Every year we field over 1,000 different research studies. Suburban
Associates owns and operates fully equipped enclosed mall research facilities and specially
designed focus group conference centers in several major markets.
] QUALITATIVE RESEARCH
SERVICE
Qualitative Research may be conducted at any of
our spacious, comfortable conference centers. All
Suburban Associates conference centers are
specially designed for client comfort and
convenience while watching focus groups or
individual interviews. We cover the Northeast
market with conference centers in Ridgewood,
New Jersey; Woodbridge, New Jersey; and East
Meadow, New York. We also have full qualitative
conference centers in Tampa, Florida and Los
Angeles, California. Our Tampa conference center
can transmit focus groups live to your office using
the Focus Vision network.
All recruiting is done from our own on-site
supervised and monitored phone centers or by
mall intercept. Using our national WATS centers
we can also recruit specialized focus group re-
spondents (medical, executive, etc.) for anywhere
in the country.
Our focus group suites are also ideal for
conducting mock jury testimonial research, and
screening for product and advertising testimonials.
M MALL TEST CENTERS
Random shopper intercept studies are conducted
at our permanent mall test centers located in New
York, New Jersey, and California.
All of our malls have one-way mirrors for
observing pre-tests and quality control monitoring
of studies. Each of our mall offices have PC’s to
conduct CRT interviews. Our mall centers are ideal
for testing video commercials, print ads, and
packaging, for taste tests, and for product placements.
H WATS
INTERVIEWING
Our WATS phone center in Ridgewood, New
Jersey is available for anything from local
telephone callbacks to large national tracking
studies. Several of our tracking studies require
over 50,000 screenings, Our staff of interviewers
is experienced in interviewing senior executives,
physicians, and other medical specialists. Your
project can be quickly programmed on our new
CATI network of CRT stations. We can give you
the respondent data file on disk or tape, or provide
complete laser printed tables.
E CODING
AND TABULATING
All coding and tabulating is done by our
experienced full time staff of coders and data
processing professionals. By coordinating
schedules with our WATS or mall centers we are
able to give you faster top line and finished tables.
Our tab department is also available to handle the
coding, keypunching, cleaning, and processing
needs of your own field department. We can
punch and clean your questionnaires or work from
your disk or tape data file.
@ OTHER SERVICES
Our field interviewing staff is ideal for customer
satisfaction studies, mystery shopping, executive
interviewing, store audits, and distribution checks.
Our project directors can lend their expertise to
your auxiliary needs such as questionnaire pre-
testing and design, field management, sampling,
and interviewer instructions.
