PHILIP MORRIS U.S.A. INTER-OFFICE CORRESPONDENCE
120 PARK AVENUE, NEW YORK, N.Y. 10017

TO: B. Hopkins
DATE: February 25, 1985
FROM: Arthur Goldfarb
SUBJECT: Rio Retail Audits

The attached tracking form has been designed for use with test panel stores selected for retail audits. A complete listing of the 30 test panel stores has also been included for your use.

- The form should be completed upon each contact to a test panel store.
- The quantity sold or exchanged-in of any Rio packing should be properly recorded. Gratis should be included in the quantity sold.
- In the case of an exchange the quantity and specific packings pulled should be noted.
- 2 for 1 promotional displays should reflect total product sold (include complimentary packs and gratis) and quantity listed on form should be noted as a 2 for 1 promotion.
- Forms should be mailed on a daily basis, whenever contact to panel stores occurs, to the address listed at the bottom of each form.
- Test panel stores need be contacted during the first couple of days of retail coverage, allowing the auditors to begin measuring product movement.

Should you have any questions please contact me.
