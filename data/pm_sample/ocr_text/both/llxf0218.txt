Studies to be performed by John Hearn Neal Nunnally and Dawn Anderson on 11/5/2003 at Ashland

Objective
To investigate smoking topography of K series EHCSS and Marlboro Lights (MLs) with CreSSmicro measurement device and to examine variability in measurement between dry air and particulate (smoke). Using 2 second puff.

Materials

Cigarettes
ML to be supplied by Sensory Research
K series EHCSS to be supplied by Ashland

CreSSmicro device Series 2
To be supplied by Sensory Research

Setting scale factor
Set scale factor using 50-80cc (1 sec 50cc square and ½ sine wave puffs on smoking machine puff), check with 30 and 80ml.

Variables
Puff Volume (Particulate=35, 60, 85 Air=20, 35, 40, 50, 60, 85, 100), Cigarette Smoke(CS, with or without), Puff Profile (sine, square of ½ triangle), Cigarette (ML or K), Number of CreSS devices (3), Number of Cigarettes, Threshold setting (factory setting=20).

Topography Parameters
Puff Volume (PV), Puff Duration (PD), Peak Flow (PF), Puff Number (PN), Inter-Puff-Interval (IPI)= 28 seconds (2scc puff).

Questions of interest
1. How accurately does the device measure topography parameters (PV, PD, PF, PN).
2. Below what point can puffs not be measured.
3. Do measurements among between devices.
4. Do measurements differ according to whether air or CS is used- is this linear or nonlinear with volume.
5. Does the type of smoke (EHCSS or MLs) make a difference.
6. Do measurements change with puff number.
