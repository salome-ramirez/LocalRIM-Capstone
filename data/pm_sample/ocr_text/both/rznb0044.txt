Dear Christine,

Thanks again for the opportunity to present some Parliament Program concepts. Field Marketing is looking forward to working with Parliament.

You requested a timeline for the 1999 programs. In reviewing the concepts that we presented, I believe the attached timeline would work for all the concepts with the special caveat on the Millennial Party. If that concept is selected, I cannot emphasize enough the need to start immediately. I realize that any program needs to be put out for bids and this requires some time. However, it is our view that as individuals begin confirming plans for this New Years Eve, it will only enhance their resolve to do something about their Year 2000 celebration plans. We are not as concerned about flights and party details as we are about hotel rooms. We believe the sooner that hotel rooms can be secured, the better chance we have of getting preferred locations.

With this in mind, if the Millennial Party is selected, Field Marketing is willing to research and reserve the appropriate number of rooms in the key markets now. Upon awarding of the bid, the details and contacts would be turned over to the company selected. The majority of the hotels are requesting a firm commitment with a penalty if the rooms are cancelled.

Please review the timeline. The timeline is based on starting the program the end of May. Obviously I have just listed the key milestones such as start dates, training dates, contract schedules and timing for major decisions.

I will talk with you soon. If we do not speak before Wednesday, have a Happy Thanksgiving.
