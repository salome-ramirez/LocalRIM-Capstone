METALS IN TOBACCO AND SMOKE

The metal data-base of published papers has been updated for 1976. As before, entries under each metal are divided into two subdivisions, tobacco and smoke. For papers to be included, they must pertain to tobacco chemistry, and they must discuss the determination of metals in the tobacco product or its smoke. The metals must be components of the tobacco or smoke, thus metal reagents are not indexed.
