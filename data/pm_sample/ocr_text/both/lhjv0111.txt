held in Lausanne on January 23, 1992.

I. Introduction

A Marlboro Classics taskforce meeting of Marlboro brand and market managers was held in November 1991. Key recommendations adopted at this meeting were:

- To establish a common commercial and print pool for Classics advertising from which all regions could draw
- This pool would be developed with input from all major Marlboro Classics markets to ensure that local needs would be met and at the same time, one a clear image of Marlboro would be projected worldwide
- Develop common logo, brand signature, emblem and labelling to ensure accurate projection of the Marlboro image in all markets and avoid wasteful production of specific materials for different markets
- Outline a program of consumer brand promotions to complement the above-the-line image advertising expenditure

To implement these recommendations, a one-day Marlboro Classics communications workshop of task force members was held in Lausanne on January 23, 1992.

II. Marlboro Classics Store Visitor/Purchaser Survey

A survey conducted in October 1991 covering store visitors in 6 markets: Brussels, Paris, Stockholm, Oslo, Munich and Zurich, concluded that:

1. The shops attract more men, over 25's and upper classes than do the cigarettes, and the purchasers are even more like this.
2. Chance passage was how most people learn of the shops, but cinema was important for Brussels.
3. Conversion of visitors to buyers ranged from 7% to 34%; those not buying found the clothes too expensive.
4. The jacket is the most popular item, followed by jeans and coats.
5. Marlboro Classics' image is of good quality original clothing, high priced, which give reasonable value for money. They are not very young. The variety of items and sizes available is weaker.
