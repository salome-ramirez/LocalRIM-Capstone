Dear Ken,

I understand from a conversation with Charlie Bates that the need for any current activity on the FML project within R&D is somewhat unclear to you. I will recap the position as follows:

Following from your presentation to B. Murray in which you expressed USA's view that the FML filter was unsuitable for PM USA projects, I have decided, in agreement with G. Bible, to relocate the plug-making activities to FML London with a target date of July 1. This will require the transfer of the KDF-2/AF-1, which is in the process of being modified, and the MK-6/Mini. In the meantime some sample plug-making commitments will be required in order to initiate the program of market tests overseas that has been decided upon by FMI.

Irrespective of PM USA's plans, the development program for FML for 1988, which was agreed and funded, requires the development of the rod-making activity to 400 meters/minute on a KDF-2. This work has been substantially completed and some limited re-engineering and speed trials remain to be conducted.

I would ask your cooperation in ensuring that this work continues with urgency until July 1. The mechanisms are already in place by which the charges for this assistance are transferred to FML, and I understand that currently W. Nichols and D. Laslie are charged in total.

You will be aware that Walt's involvement is at a low level, but I understand that D. Laslie's work does not conflict with other priorities. Since the FML project will be handled exclusively in London and through the TTG from the first of July onwards, I would request that the project records and files compiled by W. Nichols during his involvement be made available by July 1st for transfer to London in order to ensure continuity.
