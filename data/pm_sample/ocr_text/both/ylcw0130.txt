Dear Luddy:

I have your letter of October 23 regarding Polymer Industries as a potential member of TRI. Quite frankly, I know of no basis for justifying their membership. As you know, they are a specialty chemical company supplying sizing formulations and related ingredients to textile processors. This is a very proprietary business which would receive relatively little benefit from the basic research done at TRI.

I do hope to see you in the Princeton area in the near future.

With best regards.

Sincerely yours,

Helmut Wakeham, Vice President
Corporate Research and Development
