SUBJECT: A New Marlboro Idea

About six months ago, I read an article in the Morning Newsbriefs which spurred an idea that I've been tossing around ever since. As time went on, I became more convinced that a potential Marlboro opportunity exists which is worthy of a concept test. After all, many thought Medium was a "crazy" idea - and we proved otherwise.

What I have in mind is a cigar or line of cigars from Marlboro. Here's why:

• Cigars are the latest trend in smoking. Popularity is evidenced by growing sales in cigar shops, a new magazine (Cigar Aficionado) devoted exclusively to this form of smoking enjoyment, and cigar clubs where you can puff without hassle.

• It's counter cultural. Just like the new popularity of steak houses, its a trend which suggests a backlash against what the majority says is "the right thing to do".

• It's image reinforcing. The testosterone level on this can't be beat. The clubs I referred to earlier are male bastions. Notice the attitude of the next man you see smoking a cigar. It's machismo at its finest. This can be a special place in Marlboro Country that "a man can call his own".

• Its new news from Marlboro - perhaps even bigger than a line extension -- because it's so unexpected. The halo effect could benefit the entire trademark.

• I personally know of four ex-cigarette smokers who now puff cigars, of which three smoked Marlboro. It's perceived as a lower health risk -- and that's an opportunity.

Some may say "No" -- we are a cigarette company. Yes, we are a CIGARette company, but more importantly a tobacco company, that produces a product which satisfies a sensory desire for smoke. Pepsi was a cola company -- that is until the introduction of Pepsi Crystal.

Although 1991 domestic cigar sales of $705 million (+2%) is relatively small by our standards, it's the potential of the category where the real opportunity lies.

Obviously, I'm not in a position to have all the issues scoped out (feasibility, production, pricing, margins, etc.). However, a line of "MAVERICK" cigars is a conceptual idea which may have all the elements of the three B's that guide us.
