THREE OF WORLD'S TOP FOUR,
SIX NATIONAL CHAMPIONS,
TO COMPETE IN VIRGINIA SLIMS OF WASHINGTON, JANUARY 3-10, 1983
WASHINGTON, DC -- Three of the top four women tennis players in the world --
Martina Navratilova, Andrea Jaeger and Tracy Austin -- will compete in the Virginia
Slims of Washington, presented by 1st American Banks, January 3-10, 1983.
Many tennis experts, including the world's No. 6 player, Pam Shriver, feel that
the 32-player field for the $150,000 inaugural tournament of the 1983 Virginia Slims World
Championship Series is of "Grand Slam caliber."
"I guess you have to play on the tour week-in and week-out to appreciate how
amazing it is to have a field in which no one is ranked lower than 39th," Shriver ex-
claimed at a recent news conference here in Washington. "The only place you usually
find this kind of field is at the U.S. Open or at Wimbledon."
Here's a brief look at the trio which most likely will comprise the Nos. 1, 2 and 3
seeds in Washington:
MARTINA NAVRATILOVA (Mar-TEEN-a Na-VRA-tee-LO-va), 26, Dallas, Tex.
Defending champion here in Washington (winner of the 1982 Avon Championships of
Washington) ... currently ranked No. I in world on WTA computer ... owns staggering
87-3 record in '82 ... already has earned $1,224,055 in '82, not including year-ending
Toyota Championships ... also has clinched '82 bonus pool in both singles ($130,000) and
doubles ($30, 000), only player ever to win both in same year ... won third Wimbledon
singles title in '82 ... 1982 French Open champion ... has been ranked in world's top
four since computer's inception in 1975.
ANDREA JAEGER (AHN-dree-ah YAY-ger), 17, Lincolnshire, Ill.
A mainstay in world's top-10 for more than two years ... currently ranked No. 3
in world ... has '82 earnings of more than $350, 000 (only Navratilova and Chris Evert
Lloyd have more) ... a semifinalist this year at Australian and U.S. Open and finalist
at French Open ... won Avon Championships of Detroit and California earlier this year.
TRACY AUSTIN, 20, Rolling Hills, Cal.
Two-time U.S. Open champion (1979, '81) ... once pro sports' youngest millionaire,
surpassing $1 million mark in earnings at age 17 ... Quarterfinalist this year at French
and U.S. Opens and Wimbledon ... $128, 012 in earnings in '82, almost $1.8 million life-
time ... mainstay in world's top-10 since early 1978 ... currently ranked No. 4 in world.

The 32-player field for the Virginia Slims of Washington also includes the No. 1
ranked player from six different countries. Among them are:
MARTINA NAVRATILOVA (Mar-TEEN-a Na-VRA-tee-LO-va), 26, Dallas, Tex., U.S.A.
Although born in Prague, Czechoslovakia, Martina became a United States citizen
on July 21, 1981. Hence, her status as the No. 1 player in the world carries with it
the distinction of being the No. 1 American.
HANA MANDLIKOVA (HAH-nah Mond-lik-KO-va), 20, Prague, Czechoslovakia
Currently ranked No. 9 in the world ... No. 1 Czechoslovakian ... 1982 U.S. Open
finalist ... 1981 French Open champion and Wimbledon finalist ... 1980 Australian Open
champion and U.S. Open finalist.
