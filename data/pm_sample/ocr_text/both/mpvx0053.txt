Internal Communications Plan:

After New York is notified of the verdict, Han or his designee will begin revising the final draft of a news release – or language to be inserted in the verdict news release – addressing corporate and/or financial issues raised by the verdict. Final approval of the release (or inclusion in the verdict release) will be made by the appropriate PM executives.

Han or his designee also will begin revising the final draft of a corporate desk drop notifying employees of the verdict and possible ramifications of the decision. Again, final approval of the desk drop and whether it is to be executed will rest with the appropriate PM executives.

Because of the widespread employee interest in this verdict, we should explore the desirability of using PM/TV as a vehicle to deliver messages directly to employees in addition to a desk drop.

There are at least two options if PM/TV is available.

The first would be a live or taped address by Wall, Ohlemeyer or perhaps Geoff Bible, conducted either in studio or as an address to the employees of 120 Park in the cafeteria. This may be difficult to accomplish in a timely manner while efforts are under way to deal with media requests regarding the verdict. The timing of the verdict also could render an address to employees impractical or impossible.

A second alternative would be the use of a “mini news documentary” that provides employees with background on the case, the major appellate issues at stake and the potential impact of the verdict on the company. Once the verdict is known, new sound bites could be taped and the piece re-edited for broadcast over PM/TV, or to be shipped via videotape to the appropriate locations.
