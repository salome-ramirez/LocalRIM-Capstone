RESEARCH OBJECTIVES

• To improve the effectiveness of PM-USA’s youth smoking prevention efforts by developing information, analyses and interpretation of the pre-teen and teenage population.

• To support YSP Communications group by qualitatively and quantitatively testing each communication initiative to ensure clear communication of a no smoking message.

• To support the YSP Program group by providing research information that will aid in the development of programs aimed at reducing the incidence of youth smoking.
