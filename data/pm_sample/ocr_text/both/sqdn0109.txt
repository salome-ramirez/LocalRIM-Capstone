INTRODUCTION

Mass spectral patterns and fragmentation mechanisms have been studied in some detail for nicotine and nicotine metabolites$^{1-3}$. In addition, Glenn and Edwards$^1$ have examined the mass spectrometry of one type of structurally-related nicotinoids. This paper presents an extension of that work to other compounds structurally similar to nicotine.

The widespread occurrence of nicotine-like compounds in nature, as well as a continuing interest in understanding the nature of these types of compounds in tobacco and tobacco smoke provides cause for the present study. Also, mechanisms developed here may be important in the understanding of similar substituted pyridine systems.
