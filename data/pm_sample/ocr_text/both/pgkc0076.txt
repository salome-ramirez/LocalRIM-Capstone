SUBJECT: DIRECT MATERIAL QUALITY REPORT, THIRD QUARTER 1997
Attached is the Supplier Commodity Support group report for the third quarter 1997. This report is
now being generated out of the SAP database for direct materials.
SAP captures and handles data more effectively than in previous direct material and QA systems.
The data is now real-time and quantifiable. As a result, there have been several changes from the
1996 reporting format. These changes are summarized below:
Percent Acceptance and Severity results by factory have been replaced by Quality Rating
and # Of Mandatory Copy/Sensory headings on the Direct Materials Quality Summary page.
Percent Acceptance goals and first quarter values in Table I, Buying Plan Summary
Comparison, have been replaced by Quality Rating goals and values respectively.
Percent Acceptance results for the current quarter only are now displayed on Table II, Volume
Received vs. Volume Rejected.
Severity results headings on all tables have been replaced by the heading # Of Mandatory
Copy/Sensory.
The Quality Rating values replacing Percent Acceptance data now represent a composite score
that includes factory audit results, manufacturing complaints, critical quality survey element results,
quality objectives, corrective actions, recurring issues, mandatory copy/sensory and incoming
rejections.
