We are sorry that you were dissatisfied with the taste of your cigarettes, and apologize for any inconvenience you experienced.

As you know, Philip Morris has very high standards for the quality of its cigarettes. Whenever one of our consumers is dissatisfied, we like to hear about it. We are also committed to working with our distributors and retailers to have our quality STANDARDS maintained throughout the distribution system.

IF CONSUMER HAS HAD CIGARETTES FOR A PERIOD OF TIME:
“Please note that an open pack of cigarettes, over time, may absorb environmental variables which may affect the overall subjective quality of the product.”

It would be beneficial for our quality efforts. Nevertheless, consumers like you, who care enough to let us know when they are concerned about one of our products, help us to maintain our high level of quality.
