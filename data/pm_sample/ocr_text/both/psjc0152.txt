PHILIP MORRIS U.S.A. INTER-OFFICE CORRESPONDENCE
120 PARK AVENUE, NEW YORK, NY 10017
TO: Yvette Robinson DATE: October 7, 1996
FROM Maria Cohen
SUBJECT: Observations and Recommendations for Club B&H Program:
Observations/Problems
- Warehouse issues: Materials shipped to JSG are getting lost. Verification of received
materials is communicated to us too late. This presents a problem when you need time to
conduct a trace.
- Talent: In some markets and Clubs we are not booking the right talent.
- Drawing gifts: The CD player unit, because of the size, is a problem for the winner to
take home.
- Signage: In some clubs the signage has been placed late.
- Surveyors training: Due to late notification, we have not been able to attend training.
- "Take one Flyers": Distribution needs improvement.
- Name generation is very low.
- Artist cooperation regarding PR needs improvement.
Recommendations
Warehouse issues: I recommend all materials be shipped to Phoenix Marketing
warehouses to eliminate loss of materials. They have been using these
warehouses for the last 5 to 6 years.
- Talent: JSG needs to improve the quality of our acts.
- Drawing gifts: For next year, use a blown up photo of the incentive giveaways to show people
what they have won. Arrangements for delivery of the actual gift could be made for a later date
so winners wouldn't have to carry gift with them, (when prizes are of a large size)
- Signage: Phoenix Marketing should be setting up and placing all signage in Clubs. JSG
should be responsible for the production end only (i.e backstage banners).
