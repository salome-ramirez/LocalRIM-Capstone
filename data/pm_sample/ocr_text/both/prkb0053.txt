1. Services
Greenfield will perform services, which will include
the following, in connection with issues relating to Project
Royce.
(a) Greenfield will conduct four focus groups among
male smokers. The focus groups will consist of: two groups with
MARLBORO Red smokers, one group in the 18-24 age category and one
group in the 30-40 age category; and, two groups with smokers of
price/value brands, one group in the 18-24 age category and one
group in the 30-40 age category. The focus groups will be
conducted on June 29 and 30, 1992 in Dallas, Texas. A draft
screener was previously submitted to and approved by Philip
Morris and a moderator's guide will be submitted to Philip Morris
for its advance approval on or before June 26, 1992.
(b) Greenfield will analyze the information generated
by the qualitative study and deliver a Management Summary Report
in a form satisfactory to Philip Morris within two weeks of the
