Dear Mr. Taylor:

This letter is to confirm that this office still recognizes the leadership of the Oneida Nation of New York to be Richard Christyjoin and Raymond Halbritter.

On December 23, 1987, Chief, Leon Sherardish of the Handenosaurus, certified the above named persons as the representatives of the Oneida Nation as requested by my decision letter of July 29, 1987. Chief Sherardish is the acknowledged spokesman for the Grand Council of the Handenosaurus, and his certification is deemed sufficient for the Department of the Interior purposes. Messrs. Christyjoin and Halbritter will be recognized as the leaders of the Oneida Nation until such time when changes in leadership occur through legitimate processes.

In response to a meeting in my office on December 15, 1988, with you and Mr. Halbritter, a copy of this letter will be sent to Neal Rose, District Attorney, Madison County, Army Carinici, Mayor, City of Oneida; and Frederick Scullin, U.S. Attorney, Syracuse, New York. It is our hope that all other Federal agencies, and State and local governments will work with this recognized leadership in any dealings with the Oneida Nation of New York.

Sincerely,

Assistant Secretary - Indian Affairs
