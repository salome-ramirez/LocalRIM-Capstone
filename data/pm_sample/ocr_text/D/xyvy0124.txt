Comments on some members of the Soviet Delegation whom we have met:

Y. P. Kolesnikov (55) - Deputy Chief and Chief Engineer of the Main Board for Tobacco Industry of the U.S.S.R. Food Ministry

Y. Kolesnikov is the heir-apparent to the position of Chief of the Central Tobacco Board currently held by Mr. Kolestov who is aging and in bad health. He is an engineer and was the senior member of the delegation who received us at the Dukat Factory. He has been present at all of the meetings which we attended, sitting at the right hand of Mr. Kolestov, and acting as chairman in his absence. Kolesnikov probably makes the final technical decisions with regard to the course of tobacco production depending for technical assistance on Messers Kelleev (62) and Grigoriev.

V. A. Grigoriev (40) - Chief of RosGlavTabak of the U.S.S.R. Food Ministry

Grigoriev is probably the engineering brains of the Central Tobacco Board. He has the title of Chief of the Tobacco Board of Central Russia where most of our work will be done. Grigoriev doesn't say much but seems to be well informed both politically and technically. It is very likely that he is Mr. Kelleev's successor. It is felt that Kelleev is not along on this trip due to the fact that he is nearing retirement. Kelleev is the chief technologist for the Central Tobacco Board.

G. A. Krasivsky - Chief of the Design Bureau of the Krasnodar Research Institute of Food Industry of the U.S.S.R. Food Ministry (Doctor of Technical Science)

Krasivsky is the technical expert in the field of reconstituted tobacco. He is scientifically oriented and does not appear to be too well versed in the technology. He was present at the Dukat Factory meetings and at the Symposium. Although he is supposed to be based at the Food Research Institute at Krasnodar, we did not meet him there.

E. N. Zelezinsky - Chief of the Department for Tobacco and Makhorka Production of the Main Board for Tobacco and Beet farming of the U.S.S.R. Ministry of Agriculture

Pleasing personality. Relaxed. Apparently the effective man responsible for tobacco and makhorka production. Very interested in the details of tobacco farming. Quizzed Billy Apple intensively at the reception on problems relating to this.
