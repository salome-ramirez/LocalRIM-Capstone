Dear Helen:

Sorry about my lack of promptness but things have been a little hectic around here and then I had a wonderful stay at the hospital for a week and a half.

Anyway, here are the answers to your questions. Yes, we do have a merchandising policy. We can allot Philip Morris approximately 1% of the net expenditures. And we can do issue mailings with a letter from the publisher, or custom merchandising programs or cooperate with other magazines to formulate a worthwhile and effective merchandising program.

Actually, Helen, we are quite flexible regarding this subject. Also, Creative Ideas, has been recently purchased by PSC Publications. And, as a result, Russ Weller & Associates will be handling the Philip Morris account in Chicago. The phone number is 559-0700.

And, finally, it really has been fun working with everyone at Leo Burnett on the Philip Morris account. I am sure I'll be calling on you again very soon.

Thanks for your support and interest in my magazine.
