H. Wakeham April 24, 1964

C. V. Mace, Jr.

Urgent Information Required from Hugh Cullman for Updating the Economic Study on TFP and DAP-BL

After reading Hugh Cullman's April 16th memo to you re. the subject and discussing it with Roger Saleeby, we have decided that we must have the following information to prepare a meaningful report. By meaningful is meant a report which will compare the TFP and DAP-BL processes throughout the five-year forecast period.

Here is the information we need from Hugh to accomplish this.

1. Forecast of yearly demand throughout the five-year period ('65 through '69).

We require the demand expressed in terms of pounds of DAP-BL per year. Jet Lincoln's sales forecast will have to be converted into pounds of DAP-BL adjusting for any change in its percentage and for any change in the total weight of tobacco used in the cigarette blends during the forecast period. In the original report, I arbitrarily assumed the percentage reconstituted in our cigarette blends would increase from 18 to 22, and that the weight of tobacco used in our cigarettes would not change during the period. I would prefer that the Marketing Department make these projections and provide us directly with the yearly poundages of reconstituted tobacco required.

2. Estimate of DAP-BL capital and operating costs to meet the projected demand.

As soon as we obtain the five-year projected demand for reconstituted, I would like to be furnished with an estimate of the additional capital investment, if any, and of the yearly cost per pound for meeting the projected demand using the DAP-BL process. This information would permit a direct comparison of the two processes throughout the forecast period. You will recall that in the original study we did not project DAP-BL costs beyond our current yearly production rate (21 x 10^6 pounds per year).
