December 9, 1994

Mr. Richard E. Pope, Jr.

Dear Mr. Pope:

As you have gathered, our advertising agency sent you a standard sorry letter. What you may not know is that we receive many, many inquiries about how to become a Marlboro Man. Our Agency answers all of them in a similar way, encouraging none and divulging little information. The mystery of our campaign is important to us.

Sadly, you'll have to stop dreaming about becoming one of our cowboys -- for two reasons. First, we only use real western ride and rope cowboys in our advertisements. Second, we currently have all the cowboys we need for many, many years to come. I'm always sorry to disappoint an aspiring Marlboro Man but I hope you now understand why it's not in the cards.

Warmest thanks for your interest and best regards for a happy holiday season.

Sincerely yours,

/kd
cc: J. Morgan
C. Tucker
