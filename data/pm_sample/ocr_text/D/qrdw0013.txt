TO: Dr. Seligman
FROM: C. V. Mace
DATE: November 19, 1964
SUBJECT: Information required from Managers to develop the plans and timetable for the three cigarette development programs

I. As to a P. M. Multifilter modification with substantially improved mucus flow and incorporating polyox in the filter, I need to know by November 25th:

A. Confirmation at AME of the mucus flow results on fused polyox carbon plus CA (12.5 mm PC - 7.5 mm CA, dual plug) with MF₂ blend.

B. Preliminary estimate of the cost of fused polyox carbon plus CA plug (alternative one) and of polyox carbon on tow plus CA (alternative two) both versus MF₂ plug.

C. Estimate of time required to get alternatives one and two to the commercial production stage taking into account when the polyox will be available in the form desired.

II. As to a revised MF₃ product with low TPM (10 mg), high nicotine, superior pulmonary clearance, and a low carcinogenic index, I need to know by December 9th:

A. Confirmation at AME of the mucus flow results on the second MF₃ prototype using the 10% Maryland blend and confirmation of the results for fused polyox carbon plus CA as requested for cigarette development program I.

B. Estimate of the number of blend combinations we may have to evaluate and the time it will require to get low B(a)P and superior pulmonary clearance result.

C. Estimate of when we might have a substance which when added to the tobacco blend will reduce B(a)P delivery.

D. Estimate of when the carcinogenic indices (Iverson, skin painting, etc. tests) will be ready for routine use and also how much time will be required for routinely testing a cigarette sample.

E. Re-estimate of time required to improve the flavor of this product so that it at least doesn't lose to Parliament.
