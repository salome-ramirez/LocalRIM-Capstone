Beta Series B Design Conformance Testing Summary

4. Charger Operational Conformance

Operational conformance to specification for the "B" series charger design was evaluated by sampling plan via the following tests:

075CC2P Lighter Slot Recharge Time Test
062CC1P Exit Air Temperature Safety Test
064CC1P Charger Case Temperature Test
019CC1P Cleaning Power Interruption Response Test
052CC1P Lighter/Battery Installation into Charger

Lighter Slot Recharge Time

Charger Case Temperature Test

These tests verify that the case temperature and exit air temperature during active cleaning do not present a safety hazard.

A 5 charger sample was tested. Case temperature criterion was max. = 70° C. Exit air criterion was max. = 100° C.
