NORFOLK, Va. (AP) — Rocker Joe Walsh and country singer John Anderson will begin a tour of performing duty at a Navy base here Friday.

Everybody brings a blanket and has a good of time," said Alan Kincer, a spokesman for the Little Creek Naval Amphibious Base, where up to 6,000 people are expected for the outdoor show.

The show is the first of six in this year's Marlboro Music Military Tour, which raises money for such things as gymnasium facilities and athletic equipment at host bases. Other featured acts will include Lynyrd Skynyrd, Damn Yankees and 38 Special.

Anderson's hits include "Seminole Wind," and "Straight Tequila Night." Walsh's include "Life's Been Good."
