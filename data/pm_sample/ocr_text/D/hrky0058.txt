The following details specifications, timing and costs for conducting qualitative research among adult smokers for the New Products Task Force Team.

We will conduct a total of eight (8) focus groups on Tuesday, October 12 and Tuesday, October 26 in Englewood, NJ.

Each group will consist of 3 respondents who smoke Marlboro most often and 3 respondents who smoke any brand other than Marlboro most often. Additionally, all will smoke a half a pack a day or more. All will say they purchase their own cigarettes. All will smoke filter, king size or 100's, non-menthol, full-flavor, medium or light cigarettes. Per group a mix of "soft pack" and "box" purchasers will be included.

Four (4) groups will be conducted each evening among the following:
• 2 among 18-24 year olds; 1 male and 1 female
• 2 among 30-35 year olds; 1 male and 1 female

Each group will consist of 6 respondents. Groups will be scheduled to last 2 hours. Groups will be conducted at 6:00 pm and 8:00 pm each evening. Two moderators will be conducting groups simultaneously.

A summary report of findings will be available no later than November 10, 1993.

Costs are estimated at $29,700 ± 10%, inclusive of all research-related expenses and fees, travel and facility food expenses for respondents and viewing clients.

I trust this meets with your approval.
