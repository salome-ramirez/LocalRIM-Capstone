August 3, 1967

Dr. H. R. Wakeham
Vice President and Director
Research and Development
Phillip Morris Research Center
P. O. Box 3D
Richmond, Virginia

Dear Wake:

Under the sponsorship of TRC, Dr. Homburger is investigating the effect of an anti-nicotine substance developed and produced by Siegfried Company. At the present time, Dr. Homburger intends to vaporize this substance. Should this be possible, this substance could be incorporated in a cigarette. Dr. Homburger suggested that if these attempts are successful, that Philip Morris acquire Siegfried Company in Zofingen, Switzerland. Dr. Homburger knows the owner and president of the company Dr. Breler (spelling not confirmed) and feels that he could bring the parties together. Dr. Breler apparently is near retiring age and has no successors in his family who could take over direction of this small but well-known company.

I will be leaving today for Switzerland for a two week stay with my parents. During my visit, I will attempt to see Max Hausermann. In case I can do something on behalf of Philip Morris or for you personally, please let me know. My address in Switzerland is: Alte Landstrasse 75 - Kusnacht - Zurlich - CH 8700 (Zlp Code) SWITZERLAND, phone number - (area code) 051 - 900-314.

Sincerely yours,

Peter C. Luchsinger, M. D.

cc: Mr. Robert Carpenter
Dr. Thomas Osdene
