MEMORANDUM

To: Matthew Winokur
From: Jim Tozzi
Date: January 11, 1999
Subject: Meeting with EPA Officials on Data Access/Data Quality

We have been successful in getting an agency-wide meeting with EPA to discuss Data Access and Data Quality issues. Several offices will be involved in the meeting, including the Office of Resource Management & Administration, the Office of Science Policy, and the Office of the Assistant Administrator for Research & Development. The meeting will be held at 4:00 p.m. today.

We plan to go over several points with the EPA staff, including the outcomes of our Data Quality panel at the Third Annual Conference on Improving Clinical Data Bases for Health Policy Development. Specifically, we will raise the consensus from that forum that government should not question whether the Data Access and Data Quality provisions should exist or must be complied with, but instead should invest the time and resources to make those provisions work.

At the meeting, we will also discuss the very important developments underway with the American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS), which is, as you know, the publisher of Science magazine. AAAS has agreed to conduct a joint symposium with Federal Focus on this issue. They will draw upon leading scientists both within and outside of government to offer views on this topic. I think that cooperation with AAAS, as represented by this symposium, will offer considerable credibility to our overall effort, and we expect to file the proceedings of the symposium as part of the public comment on the OMB proposal. In order to have as wide a distribution of the proceedings as possible, Federal Focus will pay for transcription and production of the proceedings.

If there are any specific topics which you would like to suggest on this issue, please call me.
