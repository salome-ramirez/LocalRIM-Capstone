CAST LEAF PROGRAM

I. OBJECTIVE

To satisfy worldwide PM tobacco sheet requirements.

II. STRATEGIES

1. Identify modifications to the RL and BL Plants to increase their capacity.

2. Explore available jobber capacity.

3. Develop a slurry casting process capable of producing RL and BL sheet types.

4. Reduce environmental impact of recon plants.

III. ACTION PLAN

1. (a) Run increased line speed trials in RL and BL.

(b) Demonstrate increased BL production by higher slurry solids content. This will be achieved by reducing the feedstock particle size and increase line speed.

2. Identify and qualify jobbers capability to make products with the same subjective characteristics as PM.

3. (a) Build a Cast Leaf pilot plant.

(b) Develop products to meet target subjectives with available feedstocks.

(c) Identify process and equipment design for a commercial plant.

4. Analyze the RCB, RL and CL processes and develop modifications to reduce environmental impact.

IV. PROGRAM BENEFITS

Develop a reconstituted tobacco facility which can be built in 15 million pounds/year capacity increments with lower capital outlay and environment impact than existing recon plant designs.
