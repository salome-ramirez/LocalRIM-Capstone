TOBACCO: INDUSTRY SHOULD WIN HIGH STAKES POKER GAME

KEY POINTS:

• President Clinton announced in the State of the Union that the DOJ will pursue a Federal reimbursement lawsuit against the tobacco industry to recoup its health care expenditures associated with tobacco use.

• While the President's action is both surprising and disappointing, largely because we believe that such a claim is unlikely to survive an industry motion to dismiss, we are maintaining our Strong Buy rating on MO.

• However, tobacco stocks will likely be under pressure in the short-term. We also realize that it will take somewhat longer than we had hoped for tobacco stocks to reflect their underlying value, and that tobacco issues will now remain on the "front page."

• We believe that Clinton's move largely reflects a strategic political appeal to liberal Democrats, rather than the underlying strength of such a claim. We are reducing our 12-month target price on MO from $73 (15% discount to the market) to $67 (25% discount to the market).

• Greatest threat: if the industry were unsuccessful in largely dismissing a Federal reimbursement action prior to trial, the industry would likely be forced to settle such a claim (given its scale; realistically the industry cannot try a case with such a large damage claim). A reasonable worst-case scenario is to imagine a second Medicaid-like settlement (i.e., a $0.45 per-pack price hike).

• Consistent with our somewhat out of consensus view, we believe that a pending Federal claim, from a practical perspective, will likely bar the spin-off of material non-tobacco assets.

• We maintain a Strong Buy rating on MO ($50), an outperform rating on BAT ($9 7/8), and a Neutral rating on RN ($28), UST ($32), and MPP ($9).
