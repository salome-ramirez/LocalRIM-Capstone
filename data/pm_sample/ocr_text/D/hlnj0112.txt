DAP-BL - Dust Loss Problem

The attached note from Mr. Hind opens up another approach to solving the dust loss problem with DAP-BL. If it is at all practical, I would urge that the necessary alterations be made in the pilot plant so that the spray application can be made between the dust feeder and the "B" ovens. If it can be devised mechanically, we should provide a roller to further embed the dust into the semi-dried binder while in the "B" ovens.

We would appreciate any comments you have on these ideas.
