Title Method Development for Retention Study
Objective To establish a clinical method for estimating the retention of certain smoke constituents such as nicotine, TSNAs, CO, aldehydes, and dienes in human respiratory tract. To evaluate the influence of inhalation patterns of adult smokers on the retention and to determine the relationships between the amounts of various smoke constituents retained in adult smoker's respiratory tract and the levels of the biomarkers of exposure for these smoke constituents.
