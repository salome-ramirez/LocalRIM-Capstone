Dear Elizabeth:

Per our conversation today, the proofs I received for the perfs have been reviewed by our production managers. The following changes need to be made:

(1) Perfs need to be extended so that they "bleed" 1/4" past the trim marks. This will ensure that the perfs will bleed off the page.

(2) The margins on the coupons need to be adjusted to allow for the punching and variations in folding and printing. It is our recommendation that the copy start 3/8" from the trim line on the binding edge and extends to within 3/16" from the thumb edge.

If you have any questions regarding these specifications, please give me a call. I hope that we will receive a revised proof prior to production.
