Reuland Heads Gillette Safety Razor Co.; Turley Succeeds Him in Canadian Post

BOSTON, MASS.—Richard S. Reuland has been appointed president of Gillette Safety Razor Co. in the U. S., according to an announcement by William G. Salatich, group vice president-domestic operations for the parent firm, Gillette Co.

Mr. Reuland, who has been president of Gillette of Canada Ltd. since 1961, will be succeeded there by Joseph F. Turley, who has been general manager of Gillette Espanola, S. A., Seville, Spain.

After joining the company in 1940 as a salesman, Mr. Reuland served Gillette Safety Razor as executive assistant to the general sales manager, manager of headquarters accounts and eastern division manager.

From 1949 to 1951, he was assistant general manager of Gillette of Canada, Montreal, and became vice president in 1956. From 1957 to 1961, he was executive vice president, and since 1961 president and managing director.

His associations include the Royal St. Lawrence Yacht Club, Dorval, Que., where he is a past treasurer and member of the executive committee; Toilet Goods Manufacturers Association of Canada, of which he is a director;
