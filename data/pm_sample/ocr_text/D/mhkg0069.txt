WHO ARE WE?

MEDIA STAR PROMOTIONS may be the best kept secret in the industry. Our staff has been designing, managing and executing national promotional campaigns for some of the world's best known products since 1982. In addition to providing the full depth of our services to our many in-house clients, we often work as a major OUTSOURCE, in essence becoming an invaluable extension for other national agencies.

MEDIA STAR PROMOTIONS specializes in high impact national TOURING EVENTS. We can design and operate every aspect of a special event, ENTERTAINMENT oriented promotion or multiple market extravaganzas.
