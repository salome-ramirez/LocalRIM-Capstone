Subject: GC/MS Analysis of Sample AB-133

Sample AB-133 was prepared by S. Phillips for GC analysis by diluting with dichloromethane (1:21, v:v). A 1 µl injection of the dilute solution was made on a fused silica capillary column coated with DBWAX. The Table below refers to the two chromatograms, and lists those GC peaks examined by GC/MS. The identifications were made from the best matches of the mass spectrum with those in our libraries. Where the relative retention times match with those of authentic compounds, an asterisk (*) follows the RRT value. Note that this same sample type has been analyzed as AA-233, with a memo sent to you on March 20, 1989.
