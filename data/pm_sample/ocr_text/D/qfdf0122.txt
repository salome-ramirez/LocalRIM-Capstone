May 6, 1968

MEMORANDUM

TO: Messrs. Grant Reeves
Haas Smith
Hetsko Thompson
Hoyt Yeaman
Ramm Zahn

FROM: William Kloepfer

Attached for your information is the final draft of a news release scheduled by the American Medical Association for publication Friday morning, May 10. It announces additional grants of $8 million by six cigarette manufacturers to the American Medical Association Education and Research Foundation on tobacco and health.

No disclosure of this information should be made, of course, prior to Friday.
