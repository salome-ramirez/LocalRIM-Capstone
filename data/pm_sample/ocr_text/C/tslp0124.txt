Subject:. IDEA DISCLOSURE FOR THE INDIRECT CIGARETTE
The indirect cigarette is designed to permit generation of an aerosol
resembling cigarette smoke by the indirect use of heat generated by
corbustion. It has several functional advantages over conventional
cigarettes:
1. No combustion gases enter the mainstream smoke, hence there is
no necessity for the presence of co there.
2. The particulate phase of the aerosol is generated from pure
substances, and its composition is under full control; hence it is
capable of being made, not only not unhealthful, but postively healthful.
3. The vapor phase provides the subjective impact known as flavor,
and may be caused to resemble tobacco smoke closely or to provide other
flavors, possibly more desirable.
4. Any physiological properties.attributable to cigarette smoke 00050311
components such as that attributed to nicotine, can be produced with the
indirect cigarette, but without decomposition products and therefore with
less of the active ingredient.
On the other hand, it appears to have no compensating functional
disadvantages.
The basic idea of the indirect cigarette is to erect a physical barrier
between the ccmoustion zone and the smoke generation zone in the cigarette,
so as to bar passage of ccmbustion products but to permit heat to pass; the
heat which passes is utilized for generation of an aerosol whose physical and
organoleptic properties may be completely controlled by choice of composition
and georetry. The indirect cigarette is made to resemble a conventional
cigarette in outward aspects, and is used in a similar manner.

residue (II) resembling tobacco. The suitably processed II is distributed in
