Please refer to my memo dated June 12 for full details concerning the latest
developments in Brazil.
Senate Commission on Social Affairs
As there was no quorum today the Commission did not discuss the possibility of
inviting the two company Presidente to appear. The next meeting ia scheduled for
Thursday, June 20.
It is possible that the Health Minister may appear on the 20th as originally scheduled,
however, this needs to be confirmed.
Consumer Protection Commission
The Consumer Protection Commission in the House of Deputies approved today their
intention to proceed with an investigation into the Y-1 tobacco issue and to invite
David Kessler et al as noted in my memo yesterday. The meeting will be public and,
as such, we should expect the media and anti-tobacco groupa to be present. The
industry would also be allowed to attend and to ask questions and/or shoot down
credentials of the guest witnesses.
Timing on this Commission is undetermined. The earliest guess is that it will convene
in July.
