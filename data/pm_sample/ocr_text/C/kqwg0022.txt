a scale from 1 to 10, where 10 is the best rating
you could give it and 1 is the worst.

finished testing. If we were trying to build an

stronger, somewhat stronger, not change the strength,
make it somewhat milder or much milder?
Should it be made much harder to draw, somewhat harder
to draw, stay about as it is, be made somewhat easier
to draw or much easier to draw?
