Q.5 Is it true that you are targeting Camel smokers?
A.5 Dave's was developed to attract adult smokers of competitive discount brands.
Q.6 Does this mean PM is putting more emphasis on the discount category?
A.6 The strategy behind Dave's is consistent with PM USA's overall strategy
announced on April 2, which is to inject brand equity into the discount category
and develop trademarks that consumers ask for by name (rather than basing their
purchasing decisions on price alone.) Basic and Dave's should ensure that PM
captures a share of that category.

advertising. - Moreover, in the examples of Betty Crocker, Aunt Jemima, Bartles
& James and Icehouse, consumers appear to have been aware of the manufacturer
without a problem.
