FROM: Henry B. Merritt
SUBJECT: Seminars on Freon at Wilmington, Delaware
On Thursday, March 12, a meeting was held at
Wilmington, Delaware, with members of Du Pont's Freon
Products Division. Those from Philip Morris attending
the meeting were:
Mr. W. G. Lloyd
Mr. H. B. Merritt C70-00020
Mr. J. B. Knight

Mr. W. R. Wilkinson
Dr. R. Fagan

Mr. G. J. Roewe
Mr. Charles Reinhardt
Mr. D. E. Kvalnes
Mr. Tom D. Armstrong
Mr. Carl Brock
Mr. C. B. Jacobson
Mr. Charles L. Gray, Jr.
Dr. Raymond L. McCarthy
This meeting will be reported on by the various
members of Philip Morris that attended in the
following manner:
(1) W. F. Kuhn will report on Freon chemistry and
analytical methods.
(2) R. Fagan will report on toxicity.
(3) Henry Merritt will report on process discussions.
(4) Du Pont representatives will supply a summary of
their meeting minutes.
