On-line Marketplace (pm.Options.com) Available to Ventilation Professionals,
Opens Business Opportunities Hospitality Business Owners
Technology and the Internet have clearly changed the way many businesses
operate. Not only can owners read the latest technical publications on-line, they can
order parts and equipment for their businesses.
Now a new web site – pmOptions.com -- is opening up business opportunities
for heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning (HVAC) professionals. Logging onto the
site can mean increased equipment sales, installation, sale of preventive maintenance
contracts, repeat business and referrals.
Owners of hospitality businesses -- restaurants, hotels, bars, bowling centers and
airports – are working to improve indoor air quality (IAQ) both in existing facilities and
newly constructed buildings to comfortably accommodate both non-smokers and
smokers, and they need information, equipment, and services from HVAC experts. It is
an industry with more than 10 million employees, $350 billion in annual sales, and
annual average sales growth of 7.6 percent..
To help the hospitality industry address the issue and link them to indoor air
quality (IAQ) contractors and engineers, Philip Morris USA has created a new on-line
