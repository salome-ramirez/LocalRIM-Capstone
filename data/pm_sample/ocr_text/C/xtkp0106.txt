SubJeCT: .D PILOT PLANT OPERATIONS - WEEK OF NOVEMBER 23, 1987
Project ART Stem Line
The Stem Line only ran two days, producing approximately 1600 lbs of fine
shredded ES stem.
On Tuesday, while installing a hanger clamp in the ceiling, the sheet metal
mechanics hit an overhead light fixture with the lift, and in doing so
knocked the fixture to the floor. There were no injuries, however, an
incident report was file.
Two corrugated belts were ordered from Jerry Brothers Belting to replace the
ones on the conveyors at the discharge of each reordering cylinder. These
belts should greatly reduce the amount of stem which currently falls on the
floor.
Project ART Citrate Line
There were no runs made on the citrate.line as the ART Facility did not run
the entire work.
The overhead light fixtures were pulled out and cleaned during the down
period.
Tower Operations
There were no runs made in the tower. The shop began fabricating parts to
be used on the air bag replacement. RLum
