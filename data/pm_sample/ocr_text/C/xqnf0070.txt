AUBURN, Wash (AP) For years, Washington state tax collectors
have watched hundreds of millions of dollars in revenues go up in
smoke as various tribal smoke shops sell untaxed cigarettes to
non-Indians.
That is why they're excited about a proposed deal between Gov.
Gary Locke and four Western Washington tribes that have agreed to
levy the equivalent of Washington's 82.5 cents-per-pack tax on
cigarettes

The state likes the deal because it would give consumers less
