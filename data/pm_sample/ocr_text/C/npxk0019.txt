Marlboro Team Ponske drivers Emerson Fittipaldi and Al
Unser, Jr. qualified sixteenth and seventeenth respectively.
Andre Ribiero took his first Indy car career pole and went
on to win his first race and the $75,000 Marlboro Pole Award
"win from the pole" bonus. Michael Andretti finished socond
followed by Al Unser, Jr.
Al Unser, Jr. moved from tenth position to sixth after his
first pit stop and moved into third on lap 127 where he
remained to take his third consecutive podium finish and his
fourth podium finish of the season. He is currently in
fourth place in the PPG Cup Point Standings with 1ll points.
Emerson Fittipaldi worked his way through the field to fifth
position by lap 155 where he finished and scored 10 PPG Cup
points. He is currently in twelfth position with 61 points.
Since Andre Ribiero won from the pole, the MarlboroPole
Award bonus for a "win from the pole" will be worth $15,000
in Vancouver.
