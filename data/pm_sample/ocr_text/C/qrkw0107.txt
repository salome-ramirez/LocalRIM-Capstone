MONTHLY DEVELOPMENT SUMMARY
TITLE 1982-1984 TOBACCO BLENDS
OBJECTIVE
To develop cigarette models with the most probable 1982-1984 tobacco
blend formulations for consumer evaluation. Experimental cigarettes must
meet specifications for tar and must be subjectively equal to the current
brands.
CURRENT STATUS
1. Virginia Panel evaluations of the Y82-3 blends are awaiting
results from the field.
N3199 Marlboro Y79-3 vs. Marlboro Y84-1 shows no significant
difference on preference for Marlboro smokers, however, the
other full flavor smokers preferred the control (62/38).
This test will be repeated.
3. Preliminary results for N4267 Merit Y79-3 vs. Merit Y84-1
shows no significant differences on preference
N7096 B & H Lights Y79-3 vs. B & H Lights Y84-1 has been
rejected and will be remade.
PLANS & TIMETABLE

of B & H Lights Y84-1 Blend September, 1982
2. Complete and ship National Panel Evaluations
of the Marlboro Y84-1 Blend September, 1982
3. Schedule Virginia Panel Evaluations of the
Y83-1 Blends September, 1982
