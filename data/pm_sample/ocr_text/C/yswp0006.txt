Subject: Share of Market for the Portland Trading Area
This note is in response to your inquiry (see attached) about the
possibility of obtaining monthly sales information from American
Strevell, Portland, Oregon.
On October 17, 1977 I spoke with Dave Baker Division Manager, Portland
Market to confirm that we will be able to receive monthly sales
information from American Strevell. Dave informed me that we will
no longer be able to secure monthly sales information from H.S. Smith
& Son, Vancouver, Washington and that American Strevell is the
replacement account.
We have received the competitive sales data for American Strevell for
the month of August and have included it in our sample for the share
of market reports. We will continue to include this account in all
future reports.
If you have any questions or require additional information please
let me know.
