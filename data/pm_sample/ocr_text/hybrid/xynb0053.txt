Re: Amendment to Agreement Dated August 14, 1992
Dear Dr. Villarreal:
This letter when countersigned where indicated below, will amend the
agreement between Philip Morris Incorporated (“Philip Morris") and Monterrey
Research Group (Monterrey"), dated August 14, 1992 (the “Agreement") pursuant
to which Monterrey agreed to perform research services for Philip Morris. The
terms and conditions of this amendment are as follows:
Paragraph 2 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and the
following paragraph is substituted therefor.
In full and complete consideration for the services
rendered by Monterrey throughout the term of the
Agreement, Philip Morris will pay Monterrey $36,521.10,
of which $17,887.00 will be paid within 10 days of
execution of the Agreement, and $18,634.10 will be paid
upon Monterrey's submission of an invoice and complete
performance hereunder. The $36,521.10 payment includes
all expenses.
All other terms of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.
