RFK Jr. Wows NY Dems
Crain's Insider writes, "the best speech at the State Democratic Committee's cattle
show" 9/29 for prospective '98 candidates "may have been given by one of the few
noncandidates in the room": Robert Kennedy Jr.. The enviro atty "got a standing
ovation for his stinging denunciation“ of the GOP and what he said were its "ties to
corporate polluters. As if to prod him." Dem Chair Hope introduced RFK Jr. by saying:
"We hope the day will come when we will again have a Bobby Kennedy as a senator
from New York." Afterward, RFK Jr. said of his political future: "I may run someday, but
I like what I'm doing today" (9/30). (Back to Contents)
43 OHIO: In Plea Bargain, Oaker Pleads Guilty On Two
Counts
Cleveland Plain Dealer's Diemer & Epstein report, ex-Rep. Mary Oaker (D) has
agreed to plead guilty 9/30 to two misdemeanor election law violations, "bringing to an
abrupt end a criminal case" involving her failed '92 reelection campaign “and her misuse of
the U.S. House bank." The DoJ's House Bank Task Force agreed to drop five felony
charges for accepting her guilty plea. As part of the plea agreement, Oaker "admitted
pumping" $16K into her '92 campaign, using the names of others to avoid legal
contribution limits. "She also acknowledged "contacting people to see whether those
people would be willing to allow their names, or the names of people they know, to be
used as contributors ... even though the people contacted would not be providing the
funds" (9/30). (Back to Contents)
