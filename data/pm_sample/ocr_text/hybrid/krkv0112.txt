Subject: Salmonc!la/Microsome (S/M) Assay: Evaluation of 8857-63 and 8857-92
Two pure compounds (8857-63 and 8857-92) were submitted for both mutagenicity and
toxicity evaluation in the S/M assey over a broad dose range (0.0001-10.0 mg/plate). Both
compounds were tested in tester strsins TA98 and TA100 with and without metabolic
activation (S9).
From the 100 mg/ml stock solution prepared in DMSO (1), serial dilutions were made,
and the following doses tested: 0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1.0, and 10.0 mg/plate. Samples were
tested randomly according to the standard operating procedure (2,3). Acceptable responses
were obtained for all of the controls (see attached control data).
Both compounds formed precipitates when the 10.0 mg/plate dose was added to the
assay constituents. Test results indicated that there was no mutagenicity for either compound
under any of the test conditions (see attached experimental data). Sample 8857-63 exhibited a
>50% kill (cell death) at the two highest doses tested (1.0 and 10.0 mg/plate) in both tester
strains. Sample 8857-92 was not toxic in cither strain.
