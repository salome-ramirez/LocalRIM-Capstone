INDUSTRY COMMENTARY
We estimate that the domestic industry shipped 127.27 billion units of cigarettes in the Industry up 5.3% in
third quarter of 1994. This represents a 5.3% increase over the prior-year period's volume for Q3
shipments of 120.90 billion units. Premium share and volumes, overall, continued to
show positive results from the price cuts of 1993. We believe that more of an apples-
to-apples comparison will be available in the fourth quarter of this year, when we see
shipments down on a year-to-year comparison.
We also anticipate that the fourth quarter may be helped to some slight degree by loading
as the trade looks for a slight price increase to come in November.

The leading products for the quarter included Marlboro in the number-one spot with
a 17.2% increase in volume to 36.35 billion units and a share increase of 2.8 points to
28.5% of the market. Winston stayed in second place despite a volume decrease of 23.4%
to 7.41 billion units and share down 2.2 points to 5.8% of the market. GPC grew 17.1%
in volume for the quarter to 7.27 billion units with share up 0.6 point to 5.7% of the
market. Doral increased volume for the quarter 27.1% to 7.12 billion units with share
up 1.0 point to 5.6% of the market; Newport grew volume 11.8% to 6.54 billion units
with share up 0.4 point to 5.2% of the market; and Basic increased 5.1% in volume to
5.93 billion units with share flat at 4.7% of the market. Benson & Hedges fell in volume
for the quarter, down 2.6% to 3.05 billion units.
