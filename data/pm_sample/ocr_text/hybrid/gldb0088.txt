Testimony of Derek Crawford
Director, State Government Affairs,
Philip Morris Companies, Inc.
Before the Ohio Tobacco Settlement Task Force
My name is Derek Crawford, regional director, state
government affairs, for Philip Morris Management Corp.
behalf of all of us at Philip Morris, I wish to thank the
Task Force for this opportunity to testify.
The problem of youth smoking is a complex one and the
efforts of many segments of society are needed to make
progress on this critical social issue. Now, thanks to the
recent tobacco settlement, Ohio has an unprecedented
opportunity to enhance the state government's role in such a
multifaceted effort.
We urge the Task Force to consider recommending that
Ohio use a portion of its settlement funds to finance a
comprehensive state program to reduce the use of, and access
to, tobacco products by minors.
We make this recommendation in the spirit of
cooperation as Philip Morris augments its efforts on youth
smoking prevention as well. We also make this
recommendation taking due note that Ohio has already taken a
number of constructive steps to reduce access by minors to
cigarettes, including retail licensing, the posting of
warning signs in retail stores and restrictions on the
placement of vending machines.
In addition, the state has been airing youth smoking
prevention messages in the mass media. The state is to be 2073938088
