extinguishing cigarette which you circulated to Henry und mc. This

so that if pressure builds unduly, we would have a shelf item to resort
to. Since you have raised additional questions to Henry, our current
thinking is for him to make an oral presentation describing the entire
situation. lt is felt that this would be more oppropriate than what
would turn out to be a rather voluminous document. We shall try to
schedule this conference early upon your return.
