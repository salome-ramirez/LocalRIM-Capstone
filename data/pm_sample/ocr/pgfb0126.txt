4. Several heaters coated with nickel aluminide were pulsed during the last couple of weeks.
Heaters coated with 1 mil and 1.5 mil werg pulsed at djfferent energies to attain a maximum
temperature of 600 C, 820°C, 1050%1 150 C, and 1250 C. Blades coated with 1 mil thick coating

examined using several techniques.
