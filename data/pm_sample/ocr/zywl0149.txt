Summary
Eighty three still exposed and 46 formerly exposed workers, exposed especially to dust of carbon
black and extreme heut climate conditions up to 2 and 30 years, were asked using a siandurdized
questionnaire concerning complaints of thelower airways, The workers were investigated clinically,
by X-rays and functionally to find out disturbances of health in the lower airwaya. ‘The sputum
was investigated eytologically. Moreover, the certificates of death were checked. The results were
compared to those of 144 control persons living in the same region. The still exposed and the for-
merly exposed workers were suffering from chronic bronchitis, from non-apecifie bronchial hy-
perreactivity and from obstructive ventilatory disturbances in dependence on the duration of
exposition more frequently than the control persons. But the meun values of the one-second value
of forced expiratory volume in per cent of the vital capacity of every group were not different. Se-
vere disturbances of the lung function were stated in some cases mostly exposed more than 20
years. On 3 workers, exposed more than 20 years, roentgenological signs of a pneumovoniosis were
diaguosed demonstrating the fibrogenic effect of carbon black, The cytological findings of sputum
didn’t show any new aspects. Compared to the other people no deviating frequencies of causes of
death were found, especially not more cases with neoplasma. The mostly less frequencies of symp-
toms and findings of the formally exposed workers were estimated as a hint for regression of the
Aisturbance. The expected dependance of the signs on smoking habits was not recognized. So the
expoaition deseribed was emphasized as a professional noxious factor, particularly as compared
to the control persons important dliffarences were found und the influence of the duration of ex-
position on the symptoms and findings was significant. Workers expozed to soot suffering from
chronic bronchitis, nonspecific hyperreactivity, and especially obstructive ventilatory disturbances:
should be held in pulmonary dispensary in time.
