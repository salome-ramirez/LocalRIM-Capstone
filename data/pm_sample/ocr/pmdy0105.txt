This provides background and coupon information for Merit’s Duplicate Responders

Background
Merit Blind Challenge ’88 respondents, who participated in the Blind Challenge 87,
were informed they were not eligible to enter the program again. However, in
appreciation for their interest, we sent them a thank-you letter with a free carton
coupon offer upon the return of 5 UPC symbols to Kankakee. To date, 1,600
respondents returned 5 UPC symnbols for a free carton coupon. As the expiration date
for this offer is 3/31/89, we expect to receive additional responses.
Coupon Information
The initial 1,600 respondents were fulfilled by Johnson & Quin the week of 2/6.
Attached is a copy of the coupon that accompanied the letter. These coupons should
begin to clear the week of 4/10.
The mailing to additional respondents is planned for the end of March. I will inform
you regarding the number of respondents & coupons to be sent out as soon as I know.
