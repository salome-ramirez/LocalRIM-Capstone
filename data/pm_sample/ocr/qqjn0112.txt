Sublect: CHANGE REQUEST - SYPMOD AND TYPP APPLICATIONS M. Schulz
1., @PROBLEM DESCRIPTION
It has become obligatory to control the amount of pesticides
contained in our products being sold in certain countries;
and in particular, the Pesticide MH39 for Germany. As a
means to control, the PME and Affiliate Leaf Depts. make use
of computer support models to assist in the selection of
tobacco grades and lots to be used in cigarette fabrication;
these 2 models being the TYPP on the EDC mainframe and the
P.C. based Blénd Development Model located in the HQ Leaf
Department. These models make use of TLA data contained in
the SYPMOD Application which does not presently contain a
date field for MH3g. The Blenders are currently calculating
and entering data manually into their models.
As legislation in this area continues in the direction of
greater control, it is possible that additional pesticides
or other chemical substances, though not currently known,
may be needed to be analysed and controlled. ;
2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES
As selected raw tobacco lots have been and will continua to
be chemically analysed for MH39 content, historical data is
being accumulated. The HQ Blending Dept. is asking that the
TLA Application and the TYPP Application be modified to
handle this additional attribute. The modifications should
be such as to be able to accomodate additional attributes
not yet known or defined.
3. BENEFITS
These modifications will help render more efficient use of
Blending expertise.
