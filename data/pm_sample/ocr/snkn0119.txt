Purpose
To improve the effectiveness of P.M. USA's Direct Marketing
efforts by providing consumer research information and analytical
support for Direct Marketing programs.
Working Relationships
The incumbent reports to the Manager of Consumer Research.
The incumbent has daily contact with the Manager of Direct
Marketing and Administrator of Direct Marketing in order to plan
and initiate the consumer research which is an integral part of
direct marketing programs. During these meetings results of
ongoing projects are also discussed. The incumbent provides
analytical support to the Manager of Direct Marketing on a
continuing basis.
The incumbent has daily telephone contact and meets weekly with
the advertising agency Associate Research Director and
Manager of Database Marketing to coordinate direct marketing
research projects and participate in direct marketing database
discussions.
The incumbent has weekly contact with Brand Managers in order to
discuss brand programs and results of direct marketing research
projects.
The incumbent works closely with external market research
suppliers to plan research projects and monitor the quality and
cost of their work.
The incumbent is required to travel 20% of the time, occasionally
for extended periods.
Position Demands
An advanced degree either in the Social Sciences or Business
Administration is required with five years previous marketing
research experience, preferably with a research supplier,
consumer products company or direct marketing data base
management company. A strong statistical orientation and hands
on experience with computer modeling and software packages such
as SAS or SPSS is required. Previous experience with utilizing
or managing large databases is also required.
Strong analytical capabilities and excellent written and oral
communication skills are required. The incumbent should have
extensive experience in questionnaire development and a strong
background in general consumer research. The incumbent must be
able to interact with people at all levels within and outside the
company with tact, diplomacy and professionalism.
