SUBJECT: A New Marlboro Idea
About six months ago, | read an article in the Morning Newsbriefs which spurred an idea
that I've been tossing around ever since. As time went on, | became more convinced that
a potential Marlboro opportunity exists which is worthy of a concept test. After all, many
thought Medium was a “crazy" idea - and we proved otherwise.
What | have in mind is a cigar or line of cigars from Mariboro. Here's why:
+ Cigars are the latest trend in smoking. Popularity is evidenced by growing sales in
cigar snops, a new magazine (Cigar Aficionado) devoted exclusively to this form of
smoking enjoyment, and cigar clubs where you can puff without hassle.
» It's counter cultural. Just like the new popularity of steak houses, its a trend which
suggests a backlash against what the majority says is "the right thing to do".
+ It's image reinforcing. The testosterone level on this can't be beat. The clubs |
referred to earlier are male bastions. Notice the attitude of the next man you see
smoking a cigar. |t's machismo at its finest. This can be a special place in Marlboro
Country that "a man can cail his own".
+ Its new news from Marlboro - perhaps even bigger than a line extension -- because
it's so unexpected. The halo effect could benefit the entire trademark.
+ | personally know of four ex-cigarette smokers who now puff cigars, of which three
smoked Marlboro. It's perceived as a lower health risk -- and that's an opportunity.
Some may say "No” -- we are a cigarette company. Yes, we are a CIGARette company,
but more importantly a tobacco company, that produces a product which satisfies a
sensory desire for smoke. Pepsi was a cola company -- that is until the introduction of
Pepsi Crystal.
Although 1991 domestic cigar sales of $705 million (+2%) is relatively small by our
standards. it's the potential of the category where the real opportunity lies.
Obviously, I'm not in a-position to have ail the issues scoped out (feasibility, production,
pricing, margins, etc.). However, a line of "MAVERICK" cigars is a conceptual idea which
may nave ail the elements of the three B's that quide us.
