JAZZ CLUB CLOSED: The
L.A. Bomb Shelter, which opened
June 3 in Claremont, has closed.
Efforts to reach the owner, Larry
Norager, were unsuccessful
although a sign at the club
entrance says the venue will
reopen in the future.
The club, located in the
Griswold’s Hotel and Entertain-
ment Complex, was promoted as
a place to catch contemporary
jazz. The Garden Sandwich Shop,
next door to the club and also
owned by Norager, has closed as
well.
