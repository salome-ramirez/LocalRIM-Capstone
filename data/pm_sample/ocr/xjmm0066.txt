STIPULATED ORDER
WHEREAS discovery is ongoing and the plaintiffs are actively taking depositions of
current and former employees of defendants and of experts retained by defendants;
WHEREAS plaintiffs are in possession of documents over which defendants may assert
claims of privilege ar protection;
WHEREAS both parties desire a protocol so that the depositions may proceed without
having to be reconvened if witnesses are instructed not to answer questions regarding documents
subject to privilege or protection assertions and any such assertions are subsequently overruled;
WHEREAS defendants do not want their failure to instruct a witness not to answer
questions regarding documents subject to privilege or protection assertions to be deemed a
waiver of those privilege or protection assertions;
AND NOW this day of December, 1998 upon agreement of counsel, it is hereby
ORDERED that the following protocol will control use of documents during depositions in the
above-captioned case over which defendants assert claims of privilege or protection:
In Defendants shall assert any claims of privilege or protection on-the-record at the
time plaintiffs seek to use the document at issue;
