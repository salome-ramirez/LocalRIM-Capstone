Product Technologies
Cigarette Design and Performance Piatform
Mission: Develop the knowledge, technologies, and software tools
needed to design cigarettes with specific performance targets.
Seepe: Our plan includes the development of a computer-based,
cigarette-design tool that will allow users to predict the performance of
both conventional and unconventional products. Cigarette performance
includes normal delivery parameters, specific smoke components,
sidestream smoke visibility indices, ignition propensity indices for defined
tests, and sensory information The model! will be the repository of the
most up-to-date knowledge of the effects of cigarette design on cigarette
performance. The program must be well engineered so that refinements,
modifications, improvements, and new knowledge can be easily included
and verified. The program must be rigorous, flexible, accurate, reliable,
easily maintained, and exhaustively validated through an assessment of
the performance of a wide variety of cigarette designs. Any modifications
to the program should be accompanied by further experimental cigarette
data that can be added to a database for model validation. The program
must have a user interface that allows users with a wide range of needs
to easily access and exercise the program. The program needs to allow
designers to test new concepts computationally, thereby requiring a
concerted effort to add features and knowledge to the program on a
continuing basis.
Assumptions. We assume the availability of: Semiworks to produce the
samples required; PTL to measure their performance; ARD to evaluate
the required chemistry of tobacco, papers, and filters; Product
Development to measure sidestream visibility; Tomorrow to measure
ignition propensity; a panel to assess sensory information; Product
Technology groups to provide tobacco chemistry, smoke generation, and
selective filtration information: and ITD to support the identification of,
conversion to, and maintenance of the best computer platform for the
CDM.
Project Descriptions:
Filter Studies
* Effects of carbon filters on gas phase deliveries.
* Dual concentric filter performance.
* Performance of new filter materials and constructions, e.g., -
Crest and formed filters like MBF and SCS.
* Selective filter performance.
