ETS - MALAYSIA
Last month the Malaysian Health Minister announced that he would soon be
regulating to ban smoking in eleven types of venues including government
buildings, shopping centers, indoor and outdoor stadiums and all hospitality
venues, (Currently, smoking is allowed in bars and in adequately ventilated
smoking areas of not more than 50% in restaurants.)
WRA is working with Scientific Affairs: and local Corporate Affairs staff to
develop a strategy to defeat the proposed néw smoking restrictions and to
investigate the feasibility of arranging the installation of best practice
ventilation systems in the airport and train terminal currently under
construction and in selected hospitality venues.
