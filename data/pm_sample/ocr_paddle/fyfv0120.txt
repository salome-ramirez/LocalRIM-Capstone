LOCAL ELIMINATION OF SIDE-STREAM
ORGANISATIONS
Manufacturer of Active Ashtray (possible ISO-ION or POLLONEX)
Technology Evaluation Firm (possibly STAEFA)
OBJECTIVE
To provide a device that readily allows "ACCOMMODATION" between
smokers and non-smokers with no compromises on personal standards,
that is practical in both the home and the office and that brings
concentrations of ETS components below existing standards.
BACKGROUND
A large percentage of component concentrations in ETS
originate from sidestream smoke, the percentage increases with
lower delivery cigarettes. In an office or home environment this
is produced while the cigarette is resting on an ashtray and is of
no benefit to the smoker. If this SS can be efficiently removed
from the air in the immediate vicinity of the cigarette only a
very small volume of air need be processed and the largest
contributor to ETS can be removed at minimal cost and no
inconvenience. Some SS will necessarily escape when the smoker is
holding the cigarette and nothing is done about exhaled MS, but
the impact on ETS concentrations is substantial, and people can be
seen encouraging and demonstrating "ACCOMMODATION".
STATUS
A preliminary patent search has shown that many novel ways have
been proposed for accomplishing this local ventilation/filtration.
Pollonex have been contacted and are sending information on a
device that has been on the market for several years. Should this
not seem adequate ISO-ION have demonstrated the ability to build
small table unit utilising corona discharge for high particulate
removal efficiency.
A model is being developed to show the removal efficiency
necessary to maintain ETS component concentrations below existing
air quality standards.
RECOMMENDATIONS
Locate alternative vendors through patent search and marketing
catalogues. Identify and purchase possible units. Have efficiency
measurements made and published by a recognised company. If these
measurements can meet air quality standards, work with CA,
marketing and legal to determine ways of utilizing such units for
the "ACCOMMODATION" message for workplace and home environments. 2028366113
