SUBJECT: Weekly Shipment Performance w/e 12/17/99
Industry volume was up +0.2 billion units from last week but remained below
recent averages at 7.0 billion corresponding with continued inventory depletion by
the trade.
PM’s shares were below recent averages but stronger than last week at: SOI:
49.3%; Premium SOC: 58.9%; and Discount SOC: 21.4%.
RJR's share was stronger than last week and closer to recent averages at 23.0%.
This likely reflects a return from several weeks of payback of promotional product
shipped last month. Other major manufacturers’ shares were relatively in line with
recent averages.
The Discount category share for the week was down -0.7pp versus last week to
25.5%, while on a YTD basis the category was down -0.3pp to 26.7%.
