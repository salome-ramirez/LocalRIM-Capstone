1. Services.
(a) Consultant will provide consulting and technical analysis services to PM USA's
Fulfillment department related to the development of specific computer programs to be used by
PM USA for the fulfillment and/or tracking of merchandise to be offered to smokers 21 years of
age and older (each such computer program, a “Project", and collectively, the “Projects”). Such
services will include, but not be limited to, the following:
(i) provide written requirements for computer program functionality for a
Project, as designated by PM USA (the “Requirements");
(ii) manage the development of functional specifications and computer code
prepared by outside vendors designated by PM USA in accordance with
the Requirements for use by PM USA on a Project;
(iii) review outside vendor functional specifications and computer code as set
forth in Paragraph 1 (a) (ii) to ensure that such functional specifications
and computer code perform in accordance with the Requirements;
(iv) manage the development of test plans for the functional specifications and
computer codes prepared by outside vendors; and
(v) review outside vendor test plans and test results to ensure that such test
results are in compliance with the Requirements.
