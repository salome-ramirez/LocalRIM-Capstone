"PM's Cabarrus Plant's Work With Minority Firms Lauded"
Charlotte Observer/Knight Ridder/Tribune (08/27/98) Hopkins, Stella
The Cabarrus cigarette factory of Philip Morris won the corporation of the year award from the
Carolinas Minority Supplier Development Council. The non-profit council promotes minority
business development and business opportunities for minority-owned companies. Marilyn Bass,
executive director of the council, said Philip Morris does an "exceptional job" working with minority
business. The award, presented Wednesday during the council's annual conference at the Adams
Mark hotel, puts Philip Morris in the running for the national award in October.
