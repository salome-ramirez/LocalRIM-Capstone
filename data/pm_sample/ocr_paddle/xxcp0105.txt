Subject: Parliament Lights "G" Graphics Test
Attached is an update of the Parliament Lights "G" graphics test in
Arizona and Rochester based on the share of market method as well as
the share of P.M. method. At this time, it appears that the change
is having no significant impact on Parliament Lights sales.
The share of market method shows that the average shares through
December are close to the expected levels in Arizona (.02 below) and
Rochester (.11 below). At the same time the share of P.M. method
shows similar results. Translated into market shares the average
through January is .O1 below the expected level in Arizona and .03
below the expected level in Rochester.
