This letter is to provide further background as to licenses and arrangements regarding the marketing and
manufacturing of the Z-CARD™ in North America.
The patented Z-CARD (#5156898) is the invention of George McDonald of Guernsey. His company Z-
Industries maintains the rights to the intellectual property portfolio and the corresponding trademarks for
the Z-CARD and logo. Attached is a copy of the patent grant for the Z-CARD patent.
Instep Marketing (doing Business as Z-CARD North America) is the sole and exclusive franchisee for
the Z-CARD throughout North America. Attached please find the opening summary of the franchise
agreement that outlines the granting of the rights for marketing and manufacturing to Instep marketing
from Z-Industries.
As part of the franchise agreement for the sales and manufacturing of Z-CARDS is the ability to appoint
representatives for the sales of Z-CARDS to various clients. Intervisual Communications is one of such
representatives of the Z-CARD. We work with them in each of their offices (NY, IL & CA). Intervisual
Chicago is the office with the rights for sales to Leo Burnett and the office that previously sold the Z-
CARD™ produced for Merit in 1993.
NCL Graphic Specialties is a printing and specialty binding company based in Waukesha, WI. NCL is the
sole printer and binder of Z-CARDS within the United States. The manufacturing process requires
special purpose built binding machinery (manufactured and patented by Z-Industries and owned by Instep
Marketing) this binding machinery is placed, maintained and run at NCL Graphic Specialties.
