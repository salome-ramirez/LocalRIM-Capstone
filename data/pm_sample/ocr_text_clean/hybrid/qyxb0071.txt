Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA)
[] OSHA Plans IAQ Workshops on Exposure Assessment, Hospitality Sector Issues
OSHA is apparently planning at least two workshops related to its proposed rule on indoor air and
indoor smoking. According to Marthe Kent, the agency's director of regulatory analysis, "two or
three" workshops could be held in the spring and summer of 1998. Kent spoke to the February 12
meeting of the National Advisory Committee on Safety and Health (NACOSH), mentioning that
likely topics for the workshops would include exposure assessment issues and the use of source
controls in the hospitality sector. She did not indicate whether the workshops would be open to the
public.
Kent also said that a “full regulatory team," consisting of members from her office, the Health
Standards group and the solicitor's office, is working on the IAQ rule, and noted that “several”
contracts, including one for document analysis, are still pending. Kent said that OSHA staff are
continuing to work on an LAQ standard as if the possibility of legislative action on tobacco were “not
hanging over our heads." Calling the LAQ rulemaking “a massive undertaking" with many
