Subject: No Smoking Areas on Aircraft Lu RRu
Herewith for your information is a memorandum
distributed by the Civil Aeronautics Board to all
U. S. air carriers notifying them that their compliance
with the Regulation to provide no smoking areas has been
less than satisfactory and calling on them to accommodate
all non-smokers who desire to be segregated from smokers
even if this requires infringing on the area reserved
for smokers. The memo states (p.3): "Thus, in point of
law, no passenger is guaranteed the right to smoke
although a non-smoker is guaranteed the right to be
adequately segregated from the smokers."
Also attached is a letter dated December 26, 1974 from
ASH which was sent to each carrier. ASH is putting the
airlines on notice that it will file and prosecute formal
complaints with the C.A.B. on behalf of any non-smoking
11 passenger who experiences a failure of the carrier to
comply with the segregation rule.
