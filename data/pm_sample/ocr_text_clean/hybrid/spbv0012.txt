SUBJECT: Merit Pack Test
This topline report reviews the reactions of 144 non-menthol
Merit smokers to two alternative packs of Merit Regular.
Specifically, smokers evaluated a slightly updated Merit Regular
pack versus the current pack. The new pack incorporated several
changes including a new border, lettering size and the removal of
some copy on the back of the pack (See Attached). The changes
were intended to give the Merit Regular pack a contemporary look.
The basic design and artwork on the pack was not altered.
The sample was comprised of non-menthol Merit Kings and 100's
smokers: In addition, age and sex, breaks mirrored the
demographic profile of current Merit Regular smokers.
Initially, respondents were shown and asked to evaluate each pack
separately. Respondents were then shown the two and asked their
preference. Finally, they were questioned about their
expectations of the cigarettes in the New Pack and their
likelihood of continuing to smoke Merit if the pack were changed.
SUMMARY
Overall, the survey findings indicate that Merit Regular smokers
are very receptive to the New Pack. In particular, males and
younger smokers were particularly inclined to like and prefer the
New Pack. Females and older smokers tended to be more resistant
to the change and the New Pack. It appears that if Merit parent
were to change over to the New Pack, it would probably have
minimal impact on brand usage among all demographic smoker
groups.
Most respondents recognized that the New Pack's design is more
contemporary looking than the Current Pack's. In addition, they
concluded that cigarettes in the New Pack would be no different
than cigarettes in the Current Pack.
