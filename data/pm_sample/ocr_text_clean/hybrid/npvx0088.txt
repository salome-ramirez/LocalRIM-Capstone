Trip Report - Cindi Bright
Cindi visited the Herman Miller Company in Grand Rapids, Michigan to look
at the type of lab equipment they supply. Millcare in Mexico, a division
of Herman Miller, is one of the three companies Mexico received bids from
for CI lab equipment, Cindi was very impressed with their equipment, but
the price may be a problem for Mexico. As an advantage, since Millcare is
located in Mexico, there would not be an importation charge for the
American style lab furniture.
Trip Report - Joe Love
Ecuador
Visited Ecuador for the running of CTB trials and were having problems with
viscosity. Produced two blends with CT, but were not satisfied with the
CTB casing. The test equipment has to be out of Ecuador by August 15, so
at this time the CTB trials are first priority.
Also discussed Secondary Audit. Ecuador is agreeable to have it done, and
the focus at this time is more on the mechanical side. Secondary Audit
will have to wait until the CTB trials are finished.
For May, Ecuador was running 82% market share.
Venezuela
Also discussed Secondary Audit in Venezuela, and they are also agreeable.
Team will depart for Maracay around July 23.
Looked at the HT tunnels to get a feel for what they are doing.
For May, Venezuela was running 17-188 market share.
