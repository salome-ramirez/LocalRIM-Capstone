To the Members of the International HO-RE-CA:
STAYING AHEAD
An Inititalve Grows. .. Following our "issues alert" in the Ho. Re.Ca News (July 1989) concerning restaurant
smoking restrictions and a special report on indoor air quality (October 1989), we now provide further informa-
tion and suggestions on measures to stay in front of the issue.
If smoking is not yet a concern in your restaurants, this material can be used simply as background informa-
tion. If, however, customers have begun to request separate seating for nonsmoking or government regulations
are being considered, this program will help owners and operators to assess and adjust positively to their cus-
tomers' smoking preferences. The materials in this kit suggest steps to assist national associations in deyelop-
ing campaigns which reflect their own individual and geographic perspective.
TAKING THE INITIATIVE
For the restaurateurs, this initiative grows from the convicton that creating a comfortable, congenial dining
experience is a valued tradition and an individual art.
To preserve that tradition and counter prospeets of unworkable regulations umposed from outside, the
campaign takes the initiative to welcome smokers and nonsmokers and accommodate their preferences with
equal regard. Also. it asks the customer to reatfirm the traditional courtesy that has permitted smokers and
nonsmokers to get along harmoniously for generatons.
WATCHDOGS AND EMPTY TABLES
For employees, the campaign provides a soluton to serious problens that legislated smoking restrictions
bring: empty tables: angry or itl-at-case customers: loss of jobs and revenues from lost business: the need
tor employees to act as policemen. reterees or peacemakers in conflicts among customers.
ACCOMMODATIONAND SATISFACTION
For customers, the program represents increased personal choice as well as a cooperative atmosphere
conducive to a more pleasant, satsfying dining experience. We believe the campaign will help customers
understand how mutual courtesy and respect can resolve contliet better than legal mandates. And they are
likely to apprectate how cager the restaurateur is to accommodate their individual preterences.
