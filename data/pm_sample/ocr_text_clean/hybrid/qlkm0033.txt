Subject: Marlboro Blend "Y" Test -
Minneapolis/St. Paul
Attached is a summary of Marlboro Red's performance in the Minneapolis/
St. Paul test market. Actual share continued to fall below expected share
through 1980. It appears that the national introduction of Marlboro
Lights Box may have had a negative influence on Marlboro Red share during
the 4th quarter, particularly during October (the first month of the
introduction). This would indicate that the gap between actual and
expected shares for this quarter was not as wide as the numbers seem to
show.
If you have a need for further information, please let me know.
