Dear Karen:
Please find attached the recent statement that was presented to the museum several days ago
from the artists represented in "1965-1975: Reconsidering the Object of Art." As Richard
mentioned. we have posted this document below the statement that Hans Haacke prepared the
week earlier. This is a contradiction to Carol Vogel's piece in the New York Times today,
stating that the document was posted next to the works of all the participating artists -- it was
not. And, as you can see from the statement, only eight artists are involved, versus the
eleven that were mentioned. I do think, that both Philip Morris and MOCA presented
themselves in the best light in the article; in total support of artistic expression.
On another note, we are now preparing for our one last TC reopening event -- the "Return of
Jazz to Central Avenue -- For One Night Only." We have sold over 800 tickets, and I will
let you know next week the exact numbers and success of the event. Of course, we wish you
could be here.
Thanks, Karen, and I look forward to talking with you soon.
With very best regards.
