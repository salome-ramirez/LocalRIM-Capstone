INTER-OFFICE CORRESPONDENCE

To: Mr. M. C. Bourlas & Mr. R. C. Esperdypate: February 1, 1972
From: L. F. Meyer
Subject: Nicotine Analysis
Although late, I would like to take this opportunity
to commend you on the excellent work you performed in
developing the acetic-anhydride procedure for the analysis
of nicotine. Not only have you come up with a method to
replace the cyanogen bromide procedure but it appears to
be specific for tertiary amines and could be adopted
industry-wide for nicotine analysis. We are attempting
to have Technicon automate this method and will keep you
posted of our efforts. Again, thank you for a job well
done
