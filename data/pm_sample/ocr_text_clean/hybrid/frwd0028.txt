"Philip Morris Calls Waxman's Charges Misleading"
Washington Post (08/02/95) P. A14
Philip Morris has accused Rep. Henry A. Waxman (D-Calif.) of attempting to mislead the
American people by charging that the company increased nicotine levels in low-tar cigarettes
during the 1970s and early 1980s. Waxman, a leading foe of tobacco companies, made the
charge in the House on Monday, citing evidence he said led to the conclusion that Philip
Morris purposely raised nicotine levels in commercially marketed cigarettes. Waxman
charged thāt nicotine levels had been unnaturally high in Benson & Hedges low-tar cigarettes
and in Merit Ultra Lite.
