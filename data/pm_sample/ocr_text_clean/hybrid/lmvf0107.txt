Subject: QUARTERLY REPORT

Analysis of the optical and electronic properties of supported metal oxides catalysts has been continued to elucidate the surface structure of potential selective filtrants. The Ni/Al₂O₃ absorbance spectrum has been partially resolved into five Gaussian components. The Cu/Al₂O₃ absorbance spectrum has been analyzed in terms of a tetragonal distortion of the spinel lattice structure. Simultaneous crystal field fits for the tetrahedral and octahedral sites are being attempted. Preliminary progress in this research has been prepared for publication (1) and oral presentation (2,3).

First sets of data from the variable parameter smoking machine have been fit with continuous smooth curves. Pressure drop-flow characteristics and TPM delivery data were fit for the Marlboro monitor 15 with a variety of filters. Computer programs were written and tested for calculation of TPM, H₂O, tar, and pressure drop as a function of time during a puff. Initial trials indicate a close correlation with values obtained from the human smoking simulator.

A response (4) has been made to a recent CORESTA report which concluded that the Greiner paper porosity measurement could not be simply related to other porosity methods. Demonstration of just such relationships were given in this memorandum.
