Heating Up Cigarette Sales
by Beena Grothers

Imagine a program that allows retailers to benefit from the expertise of a category leader such as Philip Morris — in everything from promotions to space allocation. Imagine receiving extra monies for every pack of Philip Morris cigarettes sold.

That sounds like the kind of program viable only for big retail chains — but not the independent retailer. Right? Wrong. Through SAM'S Club, taking par in Philip Morris' Retail Masters program is as simple as ringing up a pack of Marlboros.

Why Retail Masters?

When members take part in Retail Masters, they can expect improved sales not only of Philip Morris products but in the entire cigarette category. Philip Morris offers "pure category management". That includes guidance in everything from:

• Pricing
• Display fixtures
• Incentive programs

There are some other good reasons to use SAM'S Club as the primary supplier for Philip Morris. "The best part of doing business through SAM'S Club is you don't have to buy a minimum as you would through a traditional wholesaler," says Rick Sherman, SAM'S Club cigarette buyer. "The member comes to SAM'S Club to buy tomorrow's inventory with today's sales. This improves cash flow and profitability" by eliminating the necessity of carrying large inventories.

And SAM'S Club prices for tobacco products compare favorably with those of candy and tobacco wholesalers.
