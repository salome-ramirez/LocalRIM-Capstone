Re: Tobacco Advertising
Dear Messrs. Chapman and Dworkin:
When I received your proposed consent decree on May 25th, I
was encouraged that Philip Morris agreed that it was willing to
resolve this matter through a consent decree. However, I was
surprised that your client waited until the day we set as a
deadline to "resolve" this matter to submit its first proposed
version of the decree.
As you will recall, we sent you our first proposed consent
decree on October 4, 1994. Since then, we have been attempting to
respond to your expressed concerns by modifying a number of
proposed decrees. We sent you a revised draft on March 9, 199s,
and another on April 19, 1995. As you also will recall, at the
meeting of April 26, 1995, Mr. Hunger set a deadline of May 25,
1995, giving your client an additional thirty days to agree to a
settlement of this matter. Yet is was not until the final day of
the grace period agreed to by Mr. Hunger that your client chose to
respond with a proposed decree of its own.
