PROJECT TITLE: SMOKE RESEARCH
PERIOD COVERED: May, 1993
1. SUBJECT Conventional Cigarette/Baseline Study
A. Objective: To establish a baseline profile of chemical and physical components
related to critical properties of conventional cigarettes. The reference cigarette
1R4F will be used to determine and/or confirm deliveries of specified
components in sidestream and mainstream smoke. The initial studies will focus
on the sidestream components.
B. Results:
1. The list of "critical components" has been divided among team members and
determination of appropriate analytical procedures have begun.
2. Mainstream and sidestream TPM, tar, nicotine and water deliveries were
determined on the 8-port smoking machine. These results were reported in a
memo.
3. Cambridge pads and XAD-4 resin were evaluated for their sidestream smoke
collection efficiencies for 3-vinylpyridine and quinoline from the 8-port smoking
machine. Eight 1R4F cigarettes were smoked under normal conditions with two
Cambridge pads in-line. The front and rear pads were separately extracted to
evaluate retention and potential sample breakthrough. It was determined that
breakthrough does occur under the standard 5 L/min. flow and that wide
variations in breakthrough are evident. However, the complex workup
procedure may well be a significant contributor to the variations observed. In
contrast, XAD-4 resin tubes provided a more efficient and rapid analysis. By
connecting three tubes in series to collect sidestream from three 1R4F cigarettes
at 1 L/min. (total flow through the chimney = 5 L/min.), a quantitative recovery
for both compounds was established. Quantification by selected ion monitoring
of masses 105 and 129 was conducted by GC/MS.
4. 1R4F and IM13 cigarettes were smoked in the sidestream smoke chamber.
Sidestream constituents including aldehydes, nitric oxide, ammonia, CO and
CO2 were measured. This data was reported in a memo. Several efforts have
been made to collect aldehydes from sidestream smoke using a single port
smoker set up at 8-port type smoking conditions. Collection of sidestream
smoke into dnph-acetonitrile solution fails to trap formaldehyde although other
aldehydes are detected. Formaldehyde appears to collect on the walls of the
glass smoke chamber. Additional runs using a modified device are underway.
5. Reassembly and testing of FTIR and GC instrumentation is in progress for
other identified smoke components. Two 20-port smoking machines are being
set up for use in the baseline and for future studies.
5. A series of side stream chamber runs was completed for 1R4F and IM13.
Analyses are complete and the information is being summarized.
