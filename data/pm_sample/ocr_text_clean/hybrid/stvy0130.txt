2 Attachment C
All records described below are covered by this disposal suspension
notice:
1. Any and all records relating to the use of ammonia, ammonia
chemistry or ammonia compounds, in cigarette manufacturing
or processing.
2. Any and all records relating to the effect of ammonia, ammonia
chemistry to ammonia compounds on tobacco, cigarettes or
cigarette smoke.
3. Any and all records relating to nicotine delivery or "impact" of
cigarettes or cigarette smoke.
4. Any and all records relating to the correlation between nicotine
and product sales.
5. Any and all records relating to the leaf biending process and
nicotine levels in cigarettes or cigarette smoke.
6. Any and all records dated from January 1, 1984 to the present:
(a) created by INBIFO, its employees, officers or agents,
and records created by Philip Morris, its employees,
officers or agents while working for or at INBIFO;
(b) created by Philip Morris; its employees, officers or
agents and sent or delivered to INBIFO;
(c) identified as indices, tables or lists, including shipping
invoices and other shipping records, currently or
previously maintained by Philip Morris, of records
received from, or transmitted directly or indirectly to,
INBIFO, its employees, officers or agents.
7. Any and all records relating to the policy, procedure,
requirement or methodology for measuring the alkaloid or
nicotine content of tobacco and/or tobacco by-products,
including but not limited to, tobacco stems, particles, dust, slurry
or sheet, at any point during the cigarette blending or
manufacturing process.
