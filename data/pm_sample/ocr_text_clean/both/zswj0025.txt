TIPS FOR E-SERIES LIGHTER KITS
• After removing lighter from charger, you may have to push the
heater cartridge back in with your thumb until you hear the click.
• Coach Consumer on removing a break-off.
• The charger may have a louder noise than the D-Series charger.
• When putting the lighter into the charger, the consumer may
have to jiggle the lighter somewhat to enable red light to come on.
