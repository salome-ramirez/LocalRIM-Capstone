Subject: Methoprene on Cigarettes in the Marketplace- A Summary of 1990
Cigarettes purchased in the marketplace have been analyzed on a monthly
basis to monitor the amount of methoprene present. An HPLC method developed
in ARD was used for the analyses and the report was distributed each month
except for April and May (no sample was submitted). The results of each
brand (including Marlboro, Winston and Kool) for the year of 1990 are
summarized in the attached table.
