I. TOBACCO BINDERS (T. Majewski, W. Gentry, A. Robinson, P. Henderson)

A. Objective: Design, formulate, and evaluate adhesives for the bonded ends, foam injected, and pneumatically formed rod programs.

B. Status: Dextrans and polydextrose binders are being evaluated as substitutes for corn syrups and citrus pectins. Maltodextrins are being examined as tobacco shred stiffeners.

C. Plans: A study is being initiated to determine the influence of thermal degradation conditions on the rheological properties of citrus pectins. An instrument has been acquired and modified for examining the effect of temperature and moisture on the mechanical modulus of cigarettes. This instrument will be used to evaluate the influence of tobacco shred stiffeners and binders on cigarette firmnesses under smoking conditions.

II. FILTER FLAVORS (T. Van Auken, A. Finley)

A. Objective: Develop filter flavor-release systems for enhancing the flavor of low delivery cigarettes.

B. Status: A PVA/menthol filter-flavor system has undergone an accelerated aging study. Product Development has developed a means of applying PVA/menthol to filters and plug wraps. Cigarettes made from this run are being examined for menthol migration and delivery. PVA/menthol formulations are being studied for optimum release properties.

C. Plans: A laboratory screening technique for evaluating the release properties of PVA/menthol films is being developed.

III. HUMIDOR PACK (T. Van Auken, J. Banyasz)

A. Objective: Assist Product Development in selecting a satisfactory moisture conservation system for cigarette packages.

B. Status: A number of salts have been examined for saturated solutions having an RH in the range of 55 to 65%. A computer model which predicts the RH of saturated salt solutions has been used to select candidate materials. The moisture transmission of a film selected for packaging the salt solutions has been measured.

C. Plans: A variety of dicarboxylic acids has been ordered. They will be converted to their potassium salts and evaluated.
