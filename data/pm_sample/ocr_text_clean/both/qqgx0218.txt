PHILIP MORRIS U.S.A.
PHILIPMORRIE Inter Office Correspondence
TO: Sara Casey DATE: July 28, 1999
FROM: Diana Braunshteyn & Brendan Anderson
SUBJECT: Accord November & December Direct Mail Briefs
Background:
As Accord expands its distribution in the Richmond area in September of 1999, the brand
will also increase its Marketing support in order to increase the awareness, interest and trial
amongst competitive adult smokers and to reinforce the purchase decision of current
Accord consumers. In particular, the post-expansion Direct Mail plan currently consists of
the following elements:
Accord Owners:
Expansion Notice - This piece will be sent to consumers who are Accord Owners as of
September 20, 1999. It will inform these consumers that Accord will be available in
more stores and that they are still entitled to receive free Lighter Cartridges.
Welcome Mailing - This piece will be sent as a bounceback to new Accord Owners
upon receipt of their Warranty Card. This piece contains a free Accord carton coupon.
Spare Parts Mailing - This piece will be sent to new Accord Owners four weeks after
they purchase an Accord Kit. This piece will inform Accord consumers that they are
entitled to a free Spare Part , Lighter Carrying Case and Lighter Cartridges.
Survey - This piece will be sent to new Accord owners eight weeks after they have
purchased a Kit. An Accord Travel Case will be offered as a bounceback incentive
item.
Competitive Smokers:
Lifestyle Brochure - This piece will deliver Accord product information to these
consumers with new lifestyle photos in a comprehensive and inviting way.
In order to complete the 1999 Direct Mail plan, this brief requests the development of the
following Direct Mail pieces:
• November Competitive Direct Mail
December Accord Owner Holiday Direct Mail
• December Competitive Holiday Direct Mail
