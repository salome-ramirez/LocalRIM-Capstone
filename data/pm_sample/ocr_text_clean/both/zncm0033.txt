Dear Dave:
We are planning to field our Transshipment Survey again in March 1986.
As you may recall from the last survey, NPD tabulated responses for approxi-
mately 240,000 stores, and we expect a similar response rate in March.
The survey forms we are using in March are very similar to the prior survey
with the exception that the March survey will contain fewer data elements.
I have attached a copy for your review indicating the data we would like
tabulated. The only variable data entry is on the number of suppliers per
account, and I believe by reviewing your records for April's survey, you
will be able to determine an average number per account for your cost
computations.
The computer tape you send us should be sorted by market and by territory
within market. The tape should also be accompanied with a format and sent
to my attention.
Prior to receiving the surveys from the field, you will be sent a tape
indicating the territories that are expected to respond. This tape may
then be matched against the survey tape to determine the non-response
territories. A list of these non-response territories should accompany the
tape you send me.
In addition, if any account should report a percent of total business which
does not sum to 100 percent, then it should be identified and edited.
I would appreciate a written proposal which reflects the issues outlined
above by January 9.
Sincerely,
Roy Anise
