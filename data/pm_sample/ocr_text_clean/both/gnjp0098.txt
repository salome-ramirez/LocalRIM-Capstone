Based on input from Mike Tiller, Dave Mertz, and myself the
following is a proposal for initial testing to determine the
impacts of returning centrifuge sludge to the RL process. The
benefits of returning the sludge to the RL process are: (1) to
eliminate landfill related activities and (2) to potentially
increase RL process yield by 2%.
Test Objectives:
1. To determine the effect of centrifuge sludge on finjohed sheet
subjectives.
2. To determine the effect of centrifuge sludge on finished sheet
bacteria levels.
3. To monitor and further identify processing parameters and
characteristics which may be impacted by the return of
centrifuge sludge to the RL process.
Summary:
The goal of this test is to produce representative RLTC with and
without centrifuge sludge for comparison. It is proposed to run
the test on Line 3. To produce RLTC with centrifuge sludge for
this test; the sludge will be returned to the process by
introducing the centrifuge sludge into the broke pulper, diluting
it to the proper consistency, and pumping it to the machine chest.
Because of potential contamination of the system, it is recommended
that the test (approximately 4 hours) be run just prior to a
shutdown and cleaning.

Test Objectives:

1. To determine the effect of centrifuge sludge on finished sheet subjectives.

2. To determine the effect of centrifuge sludge on finished sheet bacteria levels.

3. To monitor and further identify processing parameters and characteristics which may be impacted by the return of centrifuge sludge to the RL process.

Summary:

The goal of this test is to produce representative RLTC with and without centrifuge sludge for comparison. It is proposed to run the test on Line 3.
