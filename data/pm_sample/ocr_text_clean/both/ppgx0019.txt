It’s getting closer to fair time

By Brian Soergel
Daily Bulletin 7750D

In a little more than a month, the biggest county fair in the country opens right here in the Inland Valley.
The Los Angeles County Fair, held at Fairplex in Pomona, opens Sept. 8.
Themed “America’s Fair,” the 68th edition will run for 24 days and will attract well more than 1 million people. The Marlboro Music Tour will return on Sept. 9 with country singers Rick Trevino and Pam Tillis.
On Sept. 10, Colombian superstars La Internacional Sonora Dinamita will perform with La Banda Pufa, Garciela Beltran and Bronco as part of the Marlboro Music Tour.
Advance tickets are available through Sept. 7. Individual tickets are $6, $3 for children. Tickets at the gate will be $8 and $4. Group sales for 30 or more are also available.
For recorded information on the fair and other events at Fairplex, call (909) 865-4298. The general number is (909) 623-3111.

REGGAE ON THE COAST: Michael Rose, former lead singer of Black Uhuru, will be featured Saturday and Sunday at a reggae festival in Oxnard. Also appearing will be Maxi Priest, Pato Banton and others. The shows begins at 11 a.m. at College Park.
Rose has a new, self-titled album on Heartbeat Records.
Information: (714) 740-2000.

BEN HARPER
UPDATE: Claremont resident Ben Harper, signed to Virgin Records, will perform Aug. 15 at House of Blues in Hollywood. Also on the bill are The Roots.
Information: (714) 740-2000.

JAZZ CLUB CLOSED: The L.A. Bomb Shelter, which opened June 3 in Claremont, has closed. Efforts to reach the owner, Larry Norager, were unsuccessful although a sign at the club entrance says the venue will reopen in the future.
The club, located in the Griswold’s Hotel and Entertainment Complex, was promoted as a place to catch contemporary jazz. The Garden Sandwich Shop, next door to the club and also owned by Norager, has closed as well.

BOYS OF SUMMER: The Beach Boys will sing their many hits tonight at 7:30 at Blockbuster Pavilion in Devore. Opening is Christopher Cross. The 44-year-old Cross, who lives in Loves Me,” and “Independence Day.”
Tickets are $15. The show is at 8:30 p.m., with doors opening at 5. The Midnight Rodeo is at 295 E. Caroline St., San Bernardino.
Information: (909) 824-5444.

BLUES ON THE BORDER: A Blues Music Festival is scheduled Monday at 6 p.m. at the Star of the Desert Arena at Buffalo Bill’s Resort and Casino in Stateline, Nev. Performing are B.B. King, Jimmie Vaughan and Elvin Bishop.
Tickets are $22-$35. Information: (702) 382-1212.

HOME STUFF: The Home Decorating, Remodeling and Landscaping Show is today through Sunday at the Long Beach Convention Center, 300 E. Ocean Blvd., Long Beach. Among the 475 booths will be a new emphasis on landscaping, gardening, flowers and plants.
