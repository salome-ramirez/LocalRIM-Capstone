CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY: A PM USA CORE STRATEGY
Suggestions for looking at CR as Part of our 2002 Game Plan
Context
As part of including Corporate Responsibility as one of our PM USA Core Strategies this
year, a company-wide Task Force with representatives from every function has been gaining
perspective both on how other companies are approaching this critical work and how we can
be even more focused in fulfilling our own Mission of responsibility here at Philip Morris.
CR Situation Analysis
At your Functional Game Plan meetings this year, the Corporate Responsibility Task Force
member(s) from your function will help provide an overview of our PM USA learnings to date
regarding corporate responsibility.
In addition, please review the following enclosed materials as part of your Game Plan
Situation Analysis as it relates to Corporate Responsibility:
The full statement of our PMUSA Mission, including our Values and revised Goal
statements.
The Eight Key Areas of Corporate Responsibility, which provide additional detail for
your consideration.
As you review these documents, consider the following questions (but don't be limited by
them):
What areas of Corporate Responsibility do we think relate most directly to the work
of our function that we should be paying special attention to?
Where are we doing well?
Where do we see opportunities for improvement?
What key issues or questions does our CR Situation Analysis raise for us that we
need to include as part of our overall Game Plan thinking for this year?
Key Functional Initiatives in CR
As you cluster all of your function's overall Game Plan key issues and guestions, ask
yourselves:
What Biq Question(s), if any, emerge in terms of Corporate Responsibility that we
need to work on?
What CR related Key Initiative(s ) might we need to consider in our 2002 Game
Plan in order to:
engage our employees and/or relevant external stakeholders in this
assessment?
take action in one or more critical areas that we think need our immediate
attention during 2002?
If you have questions or need further information, please talk with the Task Force member
from your function (list attached), or call:

CR Situation Analysis
At your Functional Game Plan meetings this year, the Corporate Responsibility Task Force member(s) from your function will help provide an overview of our PM USA learnings to date regarding corporate responsibility.

In addition, please review the following enclosed materials as part of your Game Plan Situation Analysis as it relates to Corporate Responsibility:
- The full statement of our PMUSA Mission, including our Values and revised Goal statements.
- The Eight Key Areas of Corporate Responsibility, which provide additional detail for your consideration.

As you review these documents, consider the following questions (but don’t be limited by them):
- What areas of Corporate Responsibility do we think relate most directly to the work of our function that we should be paying special attention to?
- Where are we doing well?
- Where do we see opportunities for improvement?
- What key issues or questions does our CR Situation Analysis raise for us that we need to include as part of our overall Game Plan thinking for this year?

- What Biq Question(s), if any, emerge in terms of Corporate Responsibility that we need to work on?

- engage our employees and/or relevant external stakeholders in this assessment?
