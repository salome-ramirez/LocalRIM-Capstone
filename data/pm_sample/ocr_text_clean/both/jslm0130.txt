INTER-OFFICE CORRESPONDENCE
Richmond, Virginia

To: Mr. H. Grubbs and Dr. R. Ferguson
Date: May 8, 1990
From: Ted Sanders
Subject: Mechanistic Studies of Magnesium Carbonate Papers

Listed below are the specific projects agreed upon at the recent meeting to discuss research into the mechanism of sidestream reduction for magnesium carbonate papers. In each case the group carrying out the research is specified as well as a completion date or a date to reach a given milestone.

1. Surface area of filler materials - This function is routinely being carried out by Gordon Bokelman’s project in Chemical Research.

2. Fractal dimensions of magnesium carbonate particles - This project is being investigated by the microscopy group using image analysis. Feasibility will be determined by June 1.

3. C-labelled tobacco experiments - This project, to be carried out by Roger Comes’s group in the Chemical Research Division, is estimated to take six months.

4. Smoke pH - This work will be done within the Analytical Research Division. Method development and validation will take about one month. Samples will be run as needed.

5. Permeability of paper as a function of temperature, possibly including relative humidity effects - Kimberly-Clark has been contacted about determining paper permeability as a function of temperature for those papers which we can send them (standard cigarette paper, 093 paper, E2560 paper, magnesium hydroxide paper). If the results are illuminating, we can then carry out additional work on papers which we cannot send KC in-house. It should be noted that data presented by KC indicate that paper permeability does not change as the temperature is increased until the paper begins to ash. However, this study may still be of interest, since permeability of the ash can also be determined.

6. Correlation of strength of ash with sidestream reduction - This work is currently being done by Gordon Bokelman’s group in the Chemical Research Division.
