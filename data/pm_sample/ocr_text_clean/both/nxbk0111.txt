INVOLVEMENT IN THE
SMOKING CONTROVERSY
1955
September: Federal Trade Commission (FTC) tells cigarette
manufacturers to make no health 1mplications in their advertising.
1957
July: Surgeon General Burney says statistics indicate that
excessive cigarette smoking is a causative factor in lung cancer.
Sen. Bennett (R-Utah) introduces bill requiring cigarette packs
carry label, "Warning: Prolonged use of this product may result
in cancer, in lung, heart and circulatory ailments, and in other
diseases."
Sen. Richard Neuberger (D-Ore.) proposes eliminating tobacco
price support progrem.
1958
May: Sen. Neuberger proposes bill providing state grants to
educate school children on "harmful effects of tobacco, alcohol
and other potentially deleterious consumables."
July: Rep. Blatnik (D-Minn.) introduces bill to give FTC power
to control filter cigarette advertising, requiring statement of
tar and nicotine content if word "filter" is used.
