Preparation:

Solution 1:

Separately prepare and store the following:

A
Methylene Blue 0.1 g

B
Crystal Violet(10% w/v in 95% ethanol) 3.3 ml

Procedure:

3. Stain 1 min. with Solution 2; rinse well with water; blot dry.

4. Examine under oil immersion at 1000X magnification with direct illumination (not phase contrast): blue-violet is positive (either entire cell or intracellular granules); yellow-brown is negative.
