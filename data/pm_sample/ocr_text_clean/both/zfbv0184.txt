BY - LAWS
THE COUNCIL FOR TOBACCO RESEARCH - U.S.A.
(As Amended February 1, 1964)

The name of this organization is The Council for Tobacco Research - U.S.A. (herein called the "Council"). Its purposes, objectives and powers are as hereinafter stated:

I.

Purposes and Objectives

The purposes and objectives of the Council are to aid and assist research into tobacco use and health, and particularly into the alleged relationship between the use of tobacco and lung cancer and to make available to the public factual information on this subject.

II.

Organization

Each corporate member of the Council shall from time to time appoint an individual to serve as the personal member of the Council representing such corporate member and any such personal member may designate an individual to serve as his alternate who shall, while so serving, have the same status as a personal member.
