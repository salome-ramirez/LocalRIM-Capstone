Gentlemen:
I recently read an article by Steve Coll of the Washington
Post, which was printed in our local newspaper, The Houston
Chronicle. The title of the article, "Tobacco industry fired
up to fight against smoking bans by business."
Mr. Coll has done the public a service by making us aware of
your industry's tactics, especially its efforts against
Northwest Airlines.
Let me tell you that any cigarette, pipe, or cigar smoke that
I am forced to breathe is a breach of my right to breathe air
without tobacco smoke. Everyone knows that tobacco smoke is
harmful to one's health. I am personally allergic to tobacco
smoke.
There are many decent, and God-fearing farmers, distributors,
and others involved in the tobacco industry. They should,
however, begin to find other means of making a living.
For the highest executives of the tobacco industry to fight
for the right of 40% of the public to impose unacceptable
conditions on 60% of the public is immoral and ethically
wrong. I look forward to the day when all airlines--like
Northwest--ban smoking regardless of the time of flight.
I have pledged to fight back. First, by enlisting members of
my extended family--father, mother, brothers, sisters, and
their families [there are 10 family units]--to refuse to buy
Nabisco brand products, and those food products owned by the
other tobacco companies. And, if I detect that the tobacco
industry is hostile in its tactics, and misuses its financial
resources in promoting the rights of smokers over the rights
of non-smokers, I will organize a non-profit organization to
operate on a massive scale to oppose you, publicly and by
organizing boycotts against your food products. Houston is
a good place to start, as I am well aware of the market share
that Nabisco commands in Houston.
Also, I encourage you to refrain from CMA (customer marketing
agreements) as now practiced by Coca-Cola and Pepsi Cola.
These are amoral and are against the very foundations of this
country's free enterprise system.
Warm regards, A. Wuci Zntshic A. Wade Blankenship
