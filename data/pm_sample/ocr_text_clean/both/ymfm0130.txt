PARK 500
INTER-OFFICE CORRESPONDENCE
CHESTER, VIRGINIA
To: W. E. Garrison Date: March 12, 1985
From: D. L. Westra Dw
Subject:. Evaluation of Louisville By-Product
As per our conversation last week, we have analyzed the
ES by-product sample you brought to Park 500 and produced
the following results:
Solubles (by refractive index) 53.3%
Oven Volatiles 10.2%
Total Solids 84.4%
*TEG 0
*NO3-N 0.23%
*Potassium Sorbate TRACE
*Urea 0.05%
*Ammonia TRACE
(*Based on Dry Weight)
Judging by the particle size distribution, ranging from
dust to one inch long stem parts, I would suggest this could
be used at Park 500 (when dried to a maximum of 14% 0V)
as a by-product substitute.
/pw
cc: J. A. Gravitte
