The end of December is quickly approaching. The divisions of SW, MK, MZ and SE have requested that their inventories be reduced. In the case of Southwest, the request is to have 8 weeks supply on hand at the close of business on December 31 (after receiving and billing). We are still in need of communication from MZ, SE and MK outlining their intended inventory levels.

In determining the level your division would like obtained, please keep the following points in mind.

1. The number of taxing jurisdictions your division services and must keep in stock.
2. Your divisions receiving abilities as it pertains to deliveries that will take place on January 4th. The closer your on hand the higher the importance Mondays deliveries will be.
3. Remember to communicate to this office the percent of volume your division will bill between Saturday, December 26th and Thursday, December 31st. This is critical in managing the inventory as closely as possible.
4. McLane Company and VMI partners will need to take into consideration any areas that will not be making deliveries on Thursday, December 31st. This increases the importance and accuracy of the orders delivered on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday.

Please respond as soon as possible so final preparations and planning may take place.

High Plains was copied on this note due to the fact that at one time HP was to be part of this exercise.

Thank you for communicating back on these points as quickly as possible.
