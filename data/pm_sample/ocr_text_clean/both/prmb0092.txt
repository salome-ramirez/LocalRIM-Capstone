Subject: CORESTA Smokings: - Cigarette Model X8D9ECW
As requested, cigarettes were smoked dynamically on the prototype
CORESTA smoking machine to determine mainstream and sidestream TPM and puff
count. Single runs were carried out on the two models supplied by you.
Duplicate runs were conducted on the IM13 control used to establish smoking
parameters on the smoking machine. A run consists of five smoking ports,
three cigarettes smoked per port for a total of fifteen cigarettes per run.
Smoking conditions are set to give appropriate mainstream delivery for the
control. Data are presented below for the models studied. Cigarettes were
smoked in the order shown on 3/7/90. For comparison, also included is
smoking data from the same model smoked earlier.
Model Mainstream TPM (mg) Sidestream TPM (mg) Puff Count
IM13 22.3 25.2 9.0
X8D9ECW Control 11.1 12.4 10.3
X8D9ECW Test 9.7 11.1 10.6
IM13 22.4 25.1 9.0
X8D9ECW** 11.5 12.0 10.4
** Smoked February 21-27, 1990. Three run average.
2
X8D9ECW Control - Cigarette model with 63g/m wrapper containing 11.8%
KH₂PO4 applied to a KC E2560 paper.
X8D9ECW Test - Same wrapper with filler pulled from the cigarette and
replaced with a commercial "insert".
Distribution: Copuelelom
