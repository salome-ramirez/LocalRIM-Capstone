Dear Mr. Hoyland:
I am writing in response to your letter concerning domestic
cigarettes intended for export being sold in the Miami area. As
you may know, the Federal Trade Commission's jurisdiction over
tobacco products is limited.
Under the Federal Cigarette Labeling and Advertising Act,
("Cigarette Act") 15 U.S.C. § 1337, the Federal Trade Commission
is authorized to ensure that the Surgeon General's mandated
health warnings are displayed in alternating sequence on
cigarette packaging and advertising in the United States. While
the Commission administers the Cigarette Act, the Department of
Justice enforces it. The Comminsion may also rely on Section 5
of the FTC Act, 15 U.S.C. & 45, to prohibit unfair and deceptive
advertising of tobacco products, based upon either the content of
the ads or the advertising practice.
I have forwarded your letter, including the sample cigarette
packages, to the Department of Justice, Office of Consumer
Litigation, for review. If we can be of further assistance,
please do not hesitate to contact the agency.
Sincerely,
