120 PARK AVENUE, NEW YORK, N.Y 10017
TO See Distribution DATE: March 21, 1985
FROM: B.S.G. Weinstein
SUBJECT: Rio Market Check - Grand Rapids
Arthur Goldfarb and I visited the Grand Rapids area on Thursday and
Friday, March 14-15. We were accompanied by two auditors from Datatrack
and assistant Division Manager, Dee Kumko. There were two purposes of
this visit.
1) To check on the retail audits being conducted by Datatrack.
2) To monitor initial sales of Rio at retail.
MARKET BACKGROUND
Grand Rapids is an average Philip Morris market (101 S.D.I.) but an
above average Menthol market (118 S.D.I.). It is the strongest Marlboro
Menthol market (>600 S.D.I.) in the country. Kool performs slightly
above average (116 S.D.I.), while Salem (91 S.D.I.) and Newport (106
S.D.I.) are average. Generics are only average (92 S.D.I.) while
25/pack brands have > 240 S.D.I.
MARKETING HIGHLIGHTS
Retail Audits - Datatrack is doing an accurate and thorough job of
making sure all cigarettes are counted from all sources (visible inven-
tory, displays, backroom, behind counter) and all invoices are checked.
When discrepancies are noticeable, data is rechecked with store person-
nel and PM Sales personnel to ensure all sales are counted.
Retail Sales - Retail sales for Rio started March 4. Although it is
still too early to read, (the brand has only been in the retail stores
for approximately one and a half weeks), the following were observed:
of 32 stores visited, an average of 8-9 packs were sold.
There was Tittle difference in larger volume stores vs.
smaller volume stores. The highest sales were 50 packs sold
in the souvenir shop at the Marriott Hotel.
in the four ethnic area accounts visited, initial sales were
slightly higher (8-20 packs) than the average rate for all
stores.
there was a noticeable difference in the brand's sales in
various socio-economic areas: trial was greater in low-
er-scaled areas (4-14 packs) than upscale areas (less than 5
packs).

Retail Sales - Retail sales for Rio started March 4. Although it is still too early to read, (the brand has only been in the retail stores for approximately one and a half weeks), the following were observed:

- of 32 stores visited, an average of 8-9 packs were sold. There was little difference in larger volume stores vs. smaller volume stores. The highest sales were 50 packs sold in the souvenir shop at the Marriott Hotel.

- in the four ethnic area accounts visited, initial sales were slightly higher (8-20 packs) than the average rate for all stores.

- there was a noticeable difference in the brand's sales in various socio-economic areas: trial was greater in lower-scaled areas (4-14 packs) than upscale areas (less than 5 packs).
