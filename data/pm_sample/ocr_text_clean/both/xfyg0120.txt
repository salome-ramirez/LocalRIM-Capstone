FDA STATEMENTS ON TOBACCO INDUSTRY MAKE FOR GOOD BUYING OPPORTUNITY

COMPANY: AMERICAN BRANDS (AMB-NYSE-$33 1/8)
PHILIP MORRIS (MO-NYSE-$56 5/8)
RJK NABISCO (RN-NYSE-$6 3/4)
MONK-AUSTIN (MK-NYSE-$16 3/8)
DIRELL BROTHERS (DBRL-OTC-$21 1/2)

DATE: February 28, 1994
ANALYST: John C. Maxwell, Jr.
TELEPHONE: (804) 782-3630

The FDA's Commissioner Kessler released a letter last Friday that implied the tobacco companies have deliberately maintained an addictive level of nicotine in manufactured cigarettes and, as such, cigarettes could be a target for regulation as a drug.

We tend to believe that this recent action has little or no relevance the industry has for years worked to reduce levels of nicotine and tar in its products. We also point out that any action by the FDA would need to go through Congress before being enacted, and we feel that this is unlikely to happen.

Therefore, we view the current weakness in the tobacco stocks as a good buying opportunity.
