SUBJECT: Bristol Price Reduction Program - Elements

The positioning of Bristol as a low net price offering necessitates that the brand be supported on an ongoing basis in the second half of 1990. Unlike other brands, Bristol’s proposition doesn’t lend itself to pulsed promotion with incentives or deals that are generally perceived by consumers as "Special Offers". Consequently, our Bristol promotional efforts will be directed at incenting a low retail price utilizing display and signage.
