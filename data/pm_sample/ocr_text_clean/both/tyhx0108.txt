DJN 10:06 Philip Morris/Smokeless Cigarette -2: Doesn't Leave Ashes>MO
NEW YORK (Dow Jones)--Philip Morris USA confirmed reports it
will test a cigarette smoking system that eliminates smoke and
ashes.
In a press release Thursday, the Philip Morris Cos. (Mo) unit
said the new smoking system consists of a cigarette designed to
be smoked while partially inside an electronic "Puff Activated
Lighter" that heats the tobacco in the cigarette to burn temperature
each time the smoker takes a puff. The cigarette does not burn
continuously, but burns only when puffed, without leaving ashes.
To smoke the new cigarette, consumers insert it into the Puff
Activated Lighter and, with only the filter tip exposed, take
their puffs from the cigarette while it remains in the lighter.
The lighter is battery-powered and activated only when a smoker
takes a puff.
The new cigarette is a shorter version of a current filter
cigarette, about 62 mm long compared to an 85 mm king-size filter
cigarette.
The new cigarette delivers about 3 mg of tar and 0.2 mg of
nicotine when smoked in the specially-designed lighter.
The lighter is about the size of an electronic pager and is
powered by a compact rechargeable nickel cadmium battery. It has
a graphic display showing how much of the cigarette remains unsmoked
and the level of charge remaining in the battery.
Philip Morris USA will make the new cigarette and market the
lighter; Sanyo Electric Co. (SANYY) will make the lighter, battery
and charger in Japan.
The consumer research, which will be conducted in the U.s.
and Japan, could last up to a year.
(END) DOW JONES NEWS 10-23-97
