in the Canadian market.
The conventional approach to achieve this is to engineer
the cigarette with the lowest possible puff count through
use of faster burning cigarette papers, longer tipping paper
and lower tobacco weight.

of this approach becomes very limited for LTN cigarettes,

BOARD MEETING - APRIL 1979

As Dominique has shown, there are strong indications of consumer preference for enhanced tar and nicotine impact in the Canadian market.

The conventional approach to achieve this is to engineer the cigarette with the lowest possible puff count through use of faster burning cigarette papers, longer tipping paper and lower tobacco weight.

However, lately this approach becomes more restricted in achieving significantly higher impact per puff as the scope of this approach becomes very limited for LTN cigarettes, particularly for products with tar deliveries below 10 mg. In view of this, we have decided to evaluate a different approach by using a high alkaloid and high E.T. blend in order to maximize the nicotine impact. The development project, code names VX, commenced during the third quarter of 1978 with the objective to evaluate this high alkaloid blend on a number of products with tar deliveries ranging from 5 to 12 mg.
