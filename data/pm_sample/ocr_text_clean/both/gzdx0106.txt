July 23, 1981

DM-159-U
TO ALL DIVISION MANAGERS:

WINSTON ULTRA
DUAL COUPON

Beginning September 1, 1981, WINSTON Ultra consumers, who responded to our WINSTON Ultra "Free Sample Request Program" appearing in February and March issues of magazines and newspapers, will be receiving by direct mail, a dual coupon. This dual coupon is store redeemable for one free pack with a pack purchase and for $1.00 off-a-carton purchase.

This promotion requires no special effort on your part.
