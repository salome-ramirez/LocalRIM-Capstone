TITLE
Industrial and Vehicular Contributions to RSP in Outdoor Air
(Proposed)

PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR
Dr. Alberto Izzotti
Institute of Hygiene and Preventive Medicine, University of Genoa.

OBJECTIVE
To pursue our multi-pronged approach in supporting work on main influential factors in human lung cancer.

PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR - BACKGROUND
Dr. Izzotti has published on tobacco mutagenicity, bacterial antimutagens and is currently working on industrial and vehicular contributions to RSP in outdoor aid. As Genoa is an extremely polluted area - air, soil and water - considerable interest is being paid to health effects. Though Dr. Izzotti has made unhelpful statements on active smoking, he has shown interest in working with PM.
