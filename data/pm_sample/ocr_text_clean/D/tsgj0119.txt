DATE: April 16, 1970

FROM: E. D. Wylie

SUBJECT: Mailout Study of 85 mm Flush Fluted Marlboro vs. 85 mm Production Marlboro

Project # 2100 Study # FFM-3

Purpose: To determine, in terms of subjective smoking characteristics, consumer acceptability of a flush fluted 85 mm Marlboro vs. an 85 mm production Marlboro. Delivery of the experimental cigarette will be reduced by 2.5 mg FTC tar from that of the control.

Test Design: Panelists will be asked to compare each of the two cigarettes with their regular brand on a nine-point scale of acceptability. Five qualitative attributes will be ascribed to one, neither, or both of the two cigarettes. Finally, a direct preference question will be asked.

Presentation: Paired Comparison Monadic Other

Quantity: Five Pack/Five Pack (Split Carton) Other

Cigarette Descriptions: The control will be an 85 mm production Marlboro. The experimental will combine a standard Marlboro rod with flush fluted air diluted plastic filter, with delivery reduced by 2.5 mg to a level of 17.0 mg FTC tar.

Length: 85 100 Other

Panel: National POL MetroPOL Free Lance Other

Number and Type of Panelists: 600 regular filter smokers
