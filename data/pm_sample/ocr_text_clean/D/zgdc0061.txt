Dear Congressman Billey:

The Culpeper County Chamber of Commerce Board of Directors voted unanimously to request your support for legal reform legislation that would be effective in stopping frivolous law suits that continue to fill court dockets. Businesses and organizations need to be protected against costly suits that should never be allowed in a court of law.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Norma Dunwody
Executive Director
