MARLBORO SKI CHALLENGE AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made as of the day of 1983, between Philip Morris U.S.A., a division of Philip Morris Incorporated, 120 Park Avenue, New York, New York 10017, ("Philip Morris") and Aspen Skiing Company, 0060 Atlanta Avenue, Aspen, Colorado 81611, an owner/operator of skiing facilities located in Aspen, Colorado ("Aspen").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Philip Morris desires to establish a promotional program involving a ski race course and automated timing, referred to as the Marlboro Ski Challenge ("MSC"), to be conducted at Aspen's skiing facilities, to promote and foster recreational skiing in the United States and to provide commercial identification related to the MSC for Philip Morris' Marlboro brand;

WHEREAS, Aspen desires to promote and foster recreational skiing in the United States and to conduct the MSC at its skiing facilities in Aspen, Colorado; and

WHEREAS, the parties desire by the terms of this agreement to set forth their respective rights, obligations and responsibilities in regard to the MSC.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties covenant as follows:

1. MSC Equipment. Philip Morris agrees to provide all electronic and mechanical equipment and supplies necessary to conduct the MSC on Aspen Mountain, in Aspen, Colorado. It is understood that the MSC on-site equipment will consist of an automated timing and racing system manufactured by Reliable Racing Supply. The elements of that system include: automated timing equipment that has the capability for side-by-side ski racing; a large time display near the finish of the course; an individual results printout; a total results printout of the times of each day's skiers in order of finish; dual pro starting gates; slalom poles; all fencing necessary to enclose the racing area; and all the miscellaneous fixtures and supplies required for the proper operation of the MSC. Philip Morris shall be responsible for the installation and removal of the MSC at the beginning and end of each ski season.
