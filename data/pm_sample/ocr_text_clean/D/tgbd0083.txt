TI - SUPER CAPACITOR - A NEW BACK-UP POWER SOURCE.
AU - ROWAND DOUGLAS
OS - NEC Electronics
SO - New Electronics v 20 n 15 Jul 21 1987 p 17 (NWELAC)
LA - ENGLISH (EN)
DT - JA (Journal Article)
TC - A (Applications)
AB - The super capacitor is a small-sized, non-polar high-capacitance electric double-layer capacitor. It works by storing electric charges in an electric double-layer surface of active carbon and electrolyte. The super capacitor occupies only 1/100th the volume of an aluminum electrolytic capacitor of equivalent value, but offers capacitance up into farad units, enabling it to be used as a back-up power source in electronic appliances or as the current source for actuators and electromagnetic valves.

TI - IMPROVED SELF-DISCHARGE CHARACTERISTICS OF THE ELECTRIC DOUBLE LAYER CAPACITOR.
AU - SUZUKI TETSUO; KATSU KEITARO; OKAMOTO KOUICHI
OS - NEC, Tokyo, Jpn
SO - NEC Res Dev n 82 Jul 1986 p 118-123 (NECRAU)
LA - ENGLISH (EN)
DT - JA (Journal Article)
TC - T (Theoretical); X (Experimental)
AB - Self-discharge characteristics of the Super Capacitor have been improved by using an ion permeable porous separator of less porosity. The Super Capacitor is an electric double-layer capacitor (EDLC) which has several pairs of carbon paste electrodes separated by an ion permeable separator soaked in a dilute sulfuric acid electrolyte. The self-discharge current of the EDLC depends greatly on the pore volume of the separator and decreases with decreasing pore volume. When the required backup current is small, such as 10 mA or less, the self-discharge current cannot be ignored, and this capacitor's backup time capability for unexpected power shutdowns will be extended compared with former EDLC. As the equivalent series resistance increases with improvement in self-discharge, this capacitor is suitable for backup which requires a relatively small current over a long time.
