An essential sensor is a device that senses the initiation of a puff by the person operating the device. The sensor can be a sensor that reads to the flow of air into the mouthpiece which loads to the user's mouth; it can be a pressure sensor that senses the lowered pressure near the liquid to be nebulized due to the start of a puff by the operator; it could be a movement detection sensor that senses the deformation of the capsule as the operator starts a puff and causes lowered pressure in the capsule; thus allowing this surface in the capsule to deform inward to relieve the change in pressure.

The preferred method is to sense the change of pressure in the capsule so the operator starts to puff using a pressure sensor.
