Subj.: MARLBORO ADVENTURE TEAM 1992 - Trainingcamp and Event

Dear Leslie,

This year we will have again - for the 4th time now - our MARLBORO ADVENTURE TEAM trainingcamp in the area of Moab/Utah from June 27 to July 4, 1992.

Participating country in 1992 will be Germany and for the first time we will have a participant from Russia. We will use all our original MARLBORO ADVENTURE TEAM vehicles as there are the 4-wheel jeeps, the Honda motorbikes, wild water rafts and quarterhorses.

All local authorities were informed and we got all our necessary permits without any problem.

Out of this trainingcamp we will select the eight teams for the final event in August.

This year the MARLBORO ADVENTURE TEAM event will take place from August 15 to August 29, 1992 and will lead us through 5 states: Utah, Nevada, Arizona, New Mexico and Colorado. Starting point will be at the Lake Bonneville/Salt Desert on August 17th, 1992.

Also in this year we are again utterly aware of the highly sensitive area and I personally will take care together with my staff that everything will be handled and carried out as in the previous years.
