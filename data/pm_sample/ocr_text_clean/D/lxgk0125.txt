To: Tom Humber
Windy Leavell

Fr: Eric Schlippers
Andrew Stern

Re: NSA D.C. Recruitment Drive

Despite four days of rain, sleet and snow storms which hampered our efforts, we are happy to report the D.C. Recruitment Drive was an unqualified success, yielding 6,178 new members at a cost per member of approximately $4.70. Please note, we will expect 300 - 400 completed membership application forms to arrive today or tomorrow from Virginia Beach, Va., and Baltimore, Md. This will not affect our final budget which, after taking into account all professional services and out-of-pocket expenses, will not exceed $31,000.

Please find attached our completed membership application forms. These have been spot-checked for illegible handwriting, duplicates and false names (i.e., Elvis Presley, Socks Clinton, etc.) As these newest members of the NSA are entered into the database, we recommend they be specially coded to identify those members who were directly recruited by our people, versus those solicited by direct mail. This will allow the NSA to track the viability of these new members, as to their activism, enthusiasm, membership renewal rate, etc.

Due to the success of our initial "test" run in Washington, we strongly recommend an extension to include several targeted sights in Maryland. A great opportunity exists there due to the recent administrative order banning smoking in all public places. Our initial tests of recruiting members in the Baltimore-area proved successful. There is also the potential to recruit both civilian and military NSA members in Annapolis.
