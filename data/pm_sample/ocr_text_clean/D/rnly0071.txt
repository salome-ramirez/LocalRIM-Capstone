Cleveland Plain Dealer's Diemer & Epstein report, ex-Rep. Mary Oaker (D) has agreed to plead guilty 9/30 to two misdemeanor election law violations, "bringing to an abrupt end a criminal case" involving her failed '92 reelection campaign "and her misuse of the U.S. House bank." The DoJ's House Bank Task Force agreed to drop five felony charges for accepting her guilty plea. As part of the plea agreement, Oaker "admitted pumping" $16K into her '92 campaign, using the names of others to avoid legal contribution limits. "She also acknowledged "contacting people to see whether those people would be willing to allow their names, or the names of people they know, to be used as contributors ... even though the people contacted would not be providing the funds" (9/30).
