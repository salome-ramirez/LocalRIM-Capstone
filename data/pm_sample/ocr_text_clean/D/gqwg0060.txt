Re: PHILIP MORRIS

Dear Helen,

REDBOOK's merchandising policy has been explained to your Bill Korduplewski verbally. This letter will confirm our conversation.

Our merchandising budget is based on one-half of one percent of the net cost per page. For the February through September period Philip Morris has earned $5,450 (34.790 pages). Our current contract indicates that 15 additional pages are planned in REDBOOK which, if executed, will contribute an additional $2,423 for a total of $7,873.

This merchandising budget can be used in various ways, such as:

* Custom merchandising to specific needs --
* Contests for the Philip Morris sales force.
* Mailings to tobacco wholesalers.
* Letters mailed to a list determined by you or Philip Morris.

Contests for either the sales force or wholesalers could include premiums such as TV sets, cameras, record players, etc.

REDBOOK would be willing to "pool" our merchandising dollars with other Hearst Magazines towards a joint effort.
