Assistance was given to Project 1300 in the formulation of an enzyme slurry of high viscosity (1). The enzyme slurry, prepared in the BL plant pilot plant under Mr. Spann's and my supervision had a viscosity of 80,000 cps (2), a pH of 4.4, and a solids content of 15.18% (3).

Samples of the enzyme slurry were brought back to R. & D. for evaluation. One portion was diluted by Mr. Spann to 8.5% solids in the Waring Blendor and cast at 35 mils thickness (4). A sample of this handsheet is attached to this memo. This sheet contains tiny holes, resulting probably from air-bubbles introduced during the dilution step in the Waring Blendor.

Another sample of the enzyme-RCB slurry was cast by Mrs. A. Shiflett in our laboratory. The dry sheets were submitted to tensile strength analysis. The results are listed in the attached table. A sample strip of the sheet is also added to this memo.

The tensile strength and work results at two moisture levels show that the enzyme-RCB sheet is much stronger than the DAP-BL sheet and, according to my information (5) incomparably stronger than the new sheets from the AMF company.

The enzyme slurry has very characteristic properties which distinguish it from the DAP slurry. Brookfield viscosity and solids content do not characterize fully a slurry. DAP slurry prepared lately for the manufacture of DAP-RCB in the BL plant pilot plant had solids contents between 14.8 and 16.7%. Their viscosities varied between 20,000 cps (for a slurry with 15.3% solids content) and 145,000 cps (for slurry with 15.7% solids content).
