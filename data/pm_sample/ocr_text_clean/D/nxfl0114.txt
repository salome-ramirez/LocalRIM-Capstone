USA PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

ART

Production of additional quantities of B&H De Nic cigarettes for Tampa test market was completed June 26, 1990.

POL 0271 (Blend #215 with charcoal-on-tow filter) was approved for shipment July 2, 1990. Shipment is scheduled for this week.

Half Nic Line Extension

Cigarettes were produced using Blend #242. Subjective evaluation is in progress by Flavor Development.

Menthol

Cigarettes for POL 0412 (0.2 tpp and .03 mpp) were produced on July 2, 1990. Due to the requirements of two week aging and the time to complete analytical, it may be necessary to schedule the shipment of this test toward the end of the week of July 16, 1990.

Full flavor 85mm prototypes were produced using the K blend and the N blend for subjective evaluation.

Best of the Lowest

BOLD

Primary is complete on filler for blends 244 and 245. Cigarettes using blends 244 and 245 are scheduled to be made the week of 7/2/90.
