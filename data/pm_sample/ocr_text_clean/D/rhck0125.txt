Dear Mr. MILES:

THIS IS MY SECOND LETTER TO YOU SINCE SEPTEMBER 1993, FOR THE PURPOSE OF EXPRESSING MY DEEP CONCERNS OVER THE LATEST AND UNPRECEDENTED WAR THAT IS GOING ON AGAIN THE CIGARETTE INDUSTRY.

THE ANTISMOKING GROUP ACTIONS, THE GOVERNMENT FASCISTS INVESTIGATIONS, THE FDA, AND OTHER SPECIAL INTEREST GROUPS INCLUDING THE HEFTY PREMIUMS PAID FOR THE SHORT TERM AND LONG TERM PUT OPTIONS ON THE MO'S STOCK, ALL INDICATE THAT ALL THESE GROUPS ARE DETERMINED TO FIGHT HARD FOR THE TOTAL DESTRUCTION OF THE CIGARETTE INDUSTRY IN THIS COUNTRY.

UNDER THE PRESENT CIRCUMSTANCES THE PHILIP MORRIS MANAGEMENT SHOULD SEPARATE THE U.S. TOBACCO BUSINESS FROM THE REMAINING ASSETS OF THE COMPANY FOR TWO REASONS:

First, TO ELIMINATE ALL THE UNCERTAINTIES WHICH KEEP THE COMPANY'S STOCK DEPRESSED, AND SECOND, TO SEAL THE REMAINING ASSETS FROM ANY LIABILITIES THAT MIGHT ARISE FROM LAW SUITS AGAINST THE TOBACCO INDUSTRY WHICH UNDER THE PRESENT SENTIMENT, PUBLICITY, AND CLIMATE AGAINST THE TOBACCO INDUSTRY, MIGHT PROVE TO BE DEVASTATING FOR ALL OF US WHO HAVE CONSIDERABLE INVESTMENTS IN MO'S STOCK.
