MARLBORO COUNTRY STORE
SALES MOTIVATION PROGRAM

OBJECTIVE: Motivate Sales Force and Create Excitement for Marlboro Country Store Program

STRATEGY: Send Country Store Video/Sales Kit to all Sales and Key New York Representatives

RATIONALE:
• Cost Efficient
• Visual/Easy to Understand
• Promote Teamwork

PROGRAM: Provide:
• Country Store Video
• Brochure
• Sales Contest
• Gift

Country Store Video - Use video to create impactful message and visual tone for MCS. Include program details in a fun involving format. Investigate making interactive.

Gift - Validate the importance of individuals to the program. Send a medium/high end item from the catalog. Video details how to receive gift which will be mailed under separate cover.

Brochures - Provide written details/reference for program. Visually focused. (1 Sales/1 Trade)

Sales Contest - Establish a "MCS Sales Unit of the Month". Select one winner monthly from each Region. Promote teamwork as all individuals in unit win. Allow each individual to choose one of three possible MCS high end items. Determine by independent judging panel. Publish winners in The Force.
