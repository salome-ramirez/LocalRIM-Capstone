General Environmental Policy
What is the Environmental Policy at Philip Morris and its operating companies?
Our environmental policy affirms our commitment to comply with all laws
and regulations that apply to the conduct of our businesses. Beyond this we
aim for continuous improvement to reduce the impact of our products and
activities on the environment, while continuing to satisfy the expectations of
our customers for quality products.
Our policy is clearly expressed in our nine Environmental Principles, which
the Company adopted in 1992 and which have been updated since. These
principles are explained in the latest edition of our report on environmental
progress which has just been published.
Our nine Environmental Principles can be distilled to three focus areas:
Compliance, Continuous Improvement, and Communication. Our
environmental policy is grounded in these core areas.
Environmental Management
How does PM and its operating companies manage environmental issues on
worldwide basis?
We aim for a consistent approach to environmental management, which
promotes compliance with applicable laws and regulations, anticipates future
trends, and fosters continuous improvement. We have management systems in
place to support these objectives.
At the corporate level, we have a Vice President and three Regional Directors
of Environmental Policy within our Worldwide Regulatory Affairs group.
Their role is to develop and maintain our environmental policies and to
coordinate the activities of our operating companies in keeping with our
Environmental Principles. *
