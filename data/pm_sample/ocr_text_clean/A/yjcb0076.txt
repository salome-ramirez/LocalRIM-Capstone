MARLBORO TEAM PENSKE
MILLER LITE 200
MID-OHIO SPORTS CAR COURSE
POST-RACE QUOTES
August 13, 2000
“I’m so proud of everyone at Marlboro Team Penske they really did a great job this weekend.
The Marlboro car has been perfect all weekend and I want to thank Andy Borme (engineer) for
giving me such a fantastic car, Tim Cindric for calling a terrific race and everyone at Honda for
the great engine. i also want to congratulate Honda on their 50” win.
This weekend was very tough for me because I was still recovering from a virus so I was never
100%, and this track is very demanding both physically and mentally. During the race I never
talked on the radio because I was trying to save my energy. I also want to thank Dr. Steve Olvey
and Liz at the CART Medical Facility because they took great care of me all weekend.
At the start of the race we were just trying to stay with Gil and conserve fuel. When he went into
the pits ] had a clean track in front of me and | just started pushing to stretch my lead. I came in
for my stop one lap later and my crew did a fantastic job to get me back out in first. From then on
I just kept on pushing and pushing to continue to stretch my lead. It really feels great to win
again and to see the Marlboro cars on top.”
m Helio Castroneves, Marlboro Team Penske, #3 2000 Marlboro Honda Reynard
@ >) = Winner, Miller Lite 200
“What a great day for Marlboro Team Penske and our friends at Honda. Second is a good result
for us given we added to our points total. We had a good start and | was able to stay in front and
conserve fuel. I could tell Helio (Castroneves) was very strong and I knew if he got by us it
would be tough to beat him. We came in for our first pit stop (lap 29) which was good but I got
caught in traffic on my out lap. Helio took over the lead, came in a lap after us and was able to
maintain first place. By the time J got by the traffic, Helio was 15 seconds ahead and I knew it
would be tough to catch him. I wanted to be aggressive but I had to manage my own instincts
and bring it home. Congratulations to Helio and to Honda on their 50" Champ car victory.”
@ Gil de Ferran, Marlboro Team Penske, #2 2000 Marlboro Honda Reynard
