Proposed Q&A for TFPP Burley Pilot Program, Feb. 1, 2000
Q: What are you doing?
Today, the USDA is expected to announce a large percentage cut in the
[basic/effective] burley quota for the 2000 crop. As a result, we’re
announcing our intention to proceed this year with a Burley Pilot
Program. In this pilot program, we will contract directly with a
limited number of large and small burley tobacco farmers to explore an
alternate system for purchasing tobacco. We will continue to
participate in the current auction system for the remainder of our U.S.-
grown leaf requirements.
We intend to purchase all the tobacco in a participating farmer’s quota
as determined by the USDA under the Federal Tobacco Program.
Q: Why are you doing this?
A: As the largest purchaser of American-grown tobacco, we at Philip
Morris are concerned about American farmers and the decreasing
availability of the grades and styles of tobacco we need to maintain our
cigarette blends. This program will provide participating burley
growers with a reliable and steady source of income. It will help them
