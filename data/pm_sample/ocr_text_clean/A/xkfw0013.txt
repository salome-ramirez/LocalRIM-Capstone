i. INTRODUCTION . ; " “
The major objective of this Project is to identify those
compounds in whole smoke condensate (WSC) which contribute to
the biological activity of this complex mixture. To achieve ~
this goal, the members of this Project must be knowledgeable
in the isolation of specific compounds or compound classes which
contribute to either an in vivo or in vitro biological response.
This Project is aliso responsible for the collection and
preparation of WSC not only for in vivo and in vitro biological
systems but also for the chemical separation and identification
of the active components.
A short-term objective is to ascertain the composition of
WSC from an expanded tobacco cigarette relative to that from an
appropriate control.
