r. J. Morgan called you today regarding the proposed agree-

hey suggested that Philip Morris incorporated pick any
country and sign a licensing agreement for that country. The
agreement would be for a six-year period for a payment of
$15,000 per year minimum. The agreement is non-cancellable.
In other words, the agreement for total know-how would be a
maximum of $90,000 if Philip Morris did not produce any
wrapless plugs. During the first year after start-up of
production in this original country, Philip Morris will be
free to ship wrapless plugs to other countries. After the
first year, the agreement will continue for another five
years; however, Philip Morris will not be able to ship plugs
from this facility to any other countries. If other countries
wish to produce wrapless plugs, they will need to work out a
licensing agreement with Cigarette Components for that specific
country. It will be under the terms of the original country;
namely, for a period of six years and $15,000 a year minimum,
without any cancellation clause. In other words, this is
trial licensing agreement, and will enable Philip Morris
International to evaluate wrapless plugs in other countries
without making a final commitment to license wrapless plugs
in those countries.
