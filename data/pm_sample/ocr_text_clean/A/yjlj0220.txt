Current brand characteristics
@ooz/002
This is the core of the discussion, which will help us develop positionings that get beyond
lifestyle and connect to the brand. For that reason I think we need to take a more si
approach that we typically do. Here are my suggestions
Divide into groups that smoke the same brand (unless everyone smokes Marlboro) Have
the smokers of the brand develop an exhaustive list of attributes that describe their brand.
Could set up with a hypothetical like (imagine I'm a smoker from another country and
don’t know anything about American cigarette brands. I want to know all about your
brand so I can decide if I want to smoke it.)
Have the sub-groups present the list. See if anyone has anything to add. Then talk about
what they like best about it, how it is different from other brands, who is the imaginary
smoker of the brand.
Save switching questions until Jater in the discussion. First get all the info about current
and previous brand.
Previous brand characteristics. Go through the same exercise in as much depth. Get the
list of characteritics, likes etc. Then compare the lists and use as the basis for the
discussion of what changed, what stayed the same and why. Then ask about switching.
Finally, do the same thing for Virginia Slims (if not Virginia Slims smokers)
This is a more structured approach than we usually take. But I think it is very imporrant
that we get at the brand and switching issues indirectly (by comparing lists) as well as
directly (what did you change/what stayed the same) to avoid the problem we've seen
before of people giv
ing superficial responses when discussing their switching behavior.
