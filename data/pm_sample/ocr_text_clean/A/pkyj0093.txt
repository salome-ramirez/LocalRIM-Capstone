FDA/MacDonough to Employees 1
DATE: October 25, 1994
TO: All Miller Brewing Employees
FROM: Jack MacDonough
SUBJECT: FDA Attempt to Regulate Tobacco Industry
As many of you are aware, Food and Drug Administration
(FDA) Commissioner David Xessler has spent a considerable
amount of his time this year trying to convince Congress and
the American public that the FDA should assert regulatory
control over the tobacco industry.
Although Commissioner Kessler will not publicly admit
it, we believe that Ais true motivation in seeking
jurisdiction over our industry is so that he can create a
regulatory environment that would lead to the prohibition of
cigarette use. For example, he may dictate that cigarettes
only be sold through a doctor's prescription.
These efforts are of particular te concern to us at
Miller Brewing. If Commissioner Kessler is allowed to
regulate the tobacco industry, the door will be opened for
him to seize regulatory control over other industries of
which he may disapprove -- the beer industry perhaps.
You can lp oppose this latest attempt at government
interference in our business, by sending a letter to the FDA
in opposition to any change in the way cigarettes are
currently regulated. You can have your comments added to che
official public record by sending a brief letter to the
following address:
Docket 94P-0069
Dockets Management Branch
Food and Drug Administration
Room 1-23
12420 Parklawn Drive
Rockville, MD 20857
Note: In order to comply with federal
regulations, you must enclose three copies of your
letter in the envelope along with the original. The
FDA must receive a total of FOUR copies of your
letter. Please feel free to make your copies at work,
using your facilities' copy machines. Submissions to
the FDA are public documents.
