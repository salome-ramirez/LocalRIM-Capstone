Project BETA Meeting:
The weekly BETA meeting was held on Friday, February 3, with representatives
present from Leaf, Manufacturing Services and R&D. .
Preliminary results of the 4 VP tests have been received but not tabulated
as yet. Results should be available this afternoon (Table I).
The 7 improved candidates for VP testing have been fabricated and analytical
results received (Table I). Because of dilution fabrication problems mentioned
in last week's report, only three candidates are available for immediate shipment
to VP. The remaining candidates will be remade and shipped to VP by February 15.
A summary of the 7 initial BETA HTI test results has been received from Mr.
Mazzitelli and a review of the interpretation of the results indicates that it
may be necessary to have Mr. Mazzitelli come to Richmond for discussions.
Subjective evaluation of new BETA candidates incorporating Hi ET DBC blends,
Hi-ETA blends with DIET ET, and modified "B" blends has resulted in 8 candidates
for VP testing. Further screening of these 8 will be attempted prior to VP
requests (Table II). Re-evaluation of physical aspects of the BETA cigarette
(RTD-dilution relationship) will be undertaken with the Hi ET blends. .
Several new BETA candidates have been fabricated incorporating minor modi- .
fications to the Hi-ETA and "B" blend samples (Table III). These will be evaluated
and, if promising, incorporated into VP testing by March 1. By March 15, readouts
from all VP testing should be available for requesting HTI testing by April 1.
This will enable us to have HTI readouts by May 15.
Dr. Gannon suggested that fermented burley in place of fermented bright be
evaluated with the Hi-ETA blends. Mr. van Harn suggested the possibility that
we evaluate Amaralinho Flue Cured tobacco at the 5% level.
In-house Clermont cabinet fermentation runs with and without sorbate treatment
have been completed. Chemical analyses on these tobaccos are under way. The only
conclusion that can be drawn at this time is that sorbated tobacco can be fermented
(it did attain a temperature of 128°F). Further definition of a fermentation pro-
tocol will be established after analyses on these tobaccos are completed.
The next meeting will be Friday, February 10, at 9:00 AM. 7
