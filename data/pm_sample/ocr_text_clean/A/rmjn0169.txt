Marl Jnlimited/Fall Fes
Continues to be #1 focus with participation in the November elements of sweepstakes eligible participants at
90%, 89% participating in Retailer Rewards and 66% participating in Unlimited Heat. Heat participation was
low due to the previous utilization of flex dollars with October's Marlboro flex. Basic utilization of MTC
budget is at 50% vs. national average of 30%. Unlimited POS is staying up and we are "refreshing" as
needed; current utilization is at 92%. Utilization of OPM headers was low at 73% and this element is
receiving additional emphasis.
Retail Masters
Resignings are on track for the most part. We have aggressive action on the part of RJR to knock out
exclusive contracts in both Wawa and Store 24, Up-front payments (bonuses?) of significant amounts have
been offered. We are meeting with these two accounts on Monday to ascertain details. Both Wawa and Store
24 have seen significant volume increases since signing exclusivity agreements but the magnitude of RJR's
offers are large enough that they feel obligated to re-examine their category merchandising.
Coverage/Span

remaining 5 positions will be accommodated within the agreed upon January 1 timeframe.
