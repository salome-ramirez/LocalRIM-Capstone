Weekly Status Report for Aerosol Research (2704)
To: D. B. Losee
From: P. J. Lipowicz
Date: 2 July 1992
Tung is working full time with Joe Dobbs and Barry Fischer on the Short
Cycle Impregnation Process.
Murphy has disclosed a Beta heater design which would be manufacterd by
integrated circuit technology. The essence of the design is that the
heater is composed of multiple parallel heaters. Each heater has a
fairly high resistance, so contact resistance is less of a problem.
The
pros and cons of this idea are still being worked out.
Murphy has assembled a unit for subjective testing of a cold-swept device
using Spargate for flavor.
My modeling work indicates that coagulation and condensation can be
considered as one process. My current model is able to produce the
correct size distribution for smoke with very little input information.
The implications for Beta are that fast tobacco heating rates will
produce larger sizes.
Longer residence time in the cartridge will
produce larger sizes.
Improved mixing should reduce size.
