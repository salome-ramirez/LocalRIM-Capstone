TOBACCO BINDERS (T. Majewski, W. Gentry, A. Robinson, P. Henderson)
A.
Objective: Design, formulate, and evaluate adhesives for the
bonded ends, foam injected, and pneumatically formed rod programs,
Statu: Dextrans and polydextrose binders are being evaluated as
substitutes for corn syrups and citrus pectins, Maltodextrins are
being examined as tobacco shred stiffeners.
Plans: A study is being initiated to determine the influence of
thermal degradation conditions on the rheological properties of
eitrus pectins. An instrument has been acquired and modified for
examining the effect of temperature and moisture on the mechanical
modulus of cigarettes. This instrument will be used to evaluate
the influence of tobacco shred stiffeners and binders on cigarette
firmnesses under smoking conditions.
EILTER FLAVORS (T. Van Auken, A. Finley)
A.
B,
Objective: Develop filter flavor-release systems for enhancing
the flavor of low delivery cigarettes.
Status: A PVA/menthol filter-flavor system has undergone an
accelerated aging study. Product Development has developed a
means of applying PVA/menthol to filters and plug wraps.
Cigarettes made from this run are being examined for menthol
migration and delivery. PVA/menthol formulations are being
studied for optimum release properties.
Plans: A laboratory screening technique for evaluating the
release properties of PVA/menthol films is being developed.
HUMIDOR PACK (T. Van Auken, J. Banyasz)
A.
8B.
Objective: Assist Product Development in selecting a satisfactory
moisture conservation system for cigarette packages.
Status: A number of salts have been examined for saturated
solutions having an RH in the range of 55 to 65%. A computer
model which predicts the RH of saturated salt solutions has been
used to select candidate materials. The moisture transmission of
a film selected for packaging the salt solutions has been
measured,
Plans: A variety of dicarboxylic acids has been ordered. They
will be converted to their potassium salts and evaluated.
