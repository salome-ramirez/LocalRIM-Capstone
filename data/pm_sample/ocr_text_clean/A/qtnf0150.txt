SMOKE RESEARCH - CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT
A.
B.
Objective: To develop materials to facilitate the selective reduction of Hoffmann list compounds from
mainstream smoke.
Results:
=> Magnesium Ammonium Phosphate
* Met with representatives of SWM to discuss results of handsheet work using magnesium
ammonium phosphate. SWM indicated that the material had papermaking properties similar to
that of calcium carbonate, and that they did not anticipate any major problems with taking this
material to the mill. Discussed time schedules and what would be needed for a mill trial. A mill
trial was scheduled for June 8°.
Received analytical data from Galbraith Laboratories and determined usable Lots of the Martin
Resources, Inc. magnesium ammonium phosphate.
* Handling efforts to get 7,500 pounds of magnesium ammonium phosphate, with a particle size of
2 to 5 microns, to SWM by the end of this month. Have prepared purchase orders and have had
multiple contacts with supplier of material, jet milling folks, and SWM.
© Discussed desired paper configurations with S. Tafur and prioritized paper compositions for
June 8" mill trial. Also discussed methodology for determining magnesium ammonium
phosphate levels in the papers.
* Whole smoke aldehyde analyses of the machine-made cigarettes prepared using the University of
Maine magnesium ammonium phosphate papers were performed and the results consistent with
data collected on handmade cigarettes using handmade papers - demonstrating the stability of the
material in papermaking and cigarette making processes.
+ Requested, received, and submitted to S. Plunkett three sets of machine-made Beta cigarettes
prepared using the University of Maine magnesium ammonium phosphate papers (from
December pilot run), and a standard Beta cigarette using a calcium carbonate filler in the
outerwrap. She’s been asked to pay special attention to ammonia levels in mainstream smoke.
e Received, reviewed, rewrote, and returned the patent draft concerning the use of ammonium-
containing compounds as fillers in papers for the reduction of aldehydes in smoke.
¢ Received sample of magnesium ammonium phosphate in paste form from Martin Resources,
Inc.
=> Filter/Tobacco Additives
* Received four powdered zeolite samples.
* Located and reconditioned hydraulic press in order to press powdered samples.
Pressed, sieved, and submitted NH4-ZSMS zeolite to E. Thomas for evaluation in a plug-space-
plug configuration. Initial results encouraging.
* Ground, sieved, and submitted activated alumina to E. Thomas for evaluation in a plug-space-
plug configuration. Initial results not so encouraging.
