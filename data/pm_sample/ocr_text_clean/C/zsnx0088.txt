Subject: MONTHLY REGIONAL REPORT - MAY 1985
MEXICO
G. Bolanowski visited Mexico in May for a follow up visit on the Quality
Audit program. His trip report has been issued. Progress in this program
is quite notable.
B. Riggan, PM USA leaf, in coordination with CIGATAM management is working
on a program to alleviate leaf shortages in Mexico. Details of this
program should be wrapped by the end of June.
Visit of CIGATAM staff to B&H Canada to see Shredded Stem & CTIS operation
has been postponed until August/September.
J. Love wil1 visit in late June to review progress on new primary and
secondary facilities for Mexico City & Guadalajara.
GUATEMALA
Used equipment, ex PM USA, for the primary and secondary improvement
projects will be shipped from Richmond in June.
F. Lowman is scheduled to visit TACASA sometime in June to follow up on
the proposed new C.I. Lab.
A. Vulovic will visit in June to work on new product projects, LARK & TAS
85.
COSTA RICA
J. Horner visited San Jose in May to review progress on the new facil-
ities. Factory construction is still a few weeks behind schedule. The
office building is approximately on target with 40% of the work completed.
Quality of construction appears adequate. Movement of primary equipment
was virtually completed at end of May. Trials start up of primary is
expected in June/July.
W. Evans will visit in June to follow up on the electrical and primary
installation.
