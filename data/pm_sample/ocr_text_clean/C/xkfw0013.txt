I. INTRODUCTION
The major objective of this Project is to identify those
compounds in whole smoke condensate (WSC) which contribute to
the biological activity of this complex mixture. To achieve
this goal, the members of this Project must be knowledgeable
in the isolation of specific compounds or compound classes which
contribute to either an in vivo or in vitro biological response.
This Project is also responsible for the collection and
preparation of WSC not only for in vivo and in vitro biological
systems but also for the chemical separation and identification
of the active components.
A short-term objective is to ascertain the composition of
WSC from an expanded tobacco cigarette relative to that from an
appropriate control.
II. EXPERIMENTAL
A. Condensate Preparation
The WSC from twelve cigarette types is currently in
progress for in vivo testing. This endeavor will continue
through the duration of the test. This effort will broaden
in scope in the coming year. If an additional eighteen
cigarette types are approved for testing, additional smoking
machines, auxiliary equipment and personnel will be needed.
Presently, it requires about one month to properly
process WSC from twelve cigarette types. Three additional
smoking machines (two 30-port machines and one 240-port 10018771
machine) have been ordered. Attention will be focused on
the required cold storage space necessary to achieve this
goal.
