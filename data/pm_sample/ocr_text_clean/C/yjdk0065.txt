SUBJECT: 1998 Marlboro Auto Racing
OVERVIEW
The 1997 CART World Series demonstrated the tremendous competition and excitement that currently exists in
open wheel racing in North America. In 1998 the CART World Series will be called the Fed Ex Championship
Series. All the race teams have developed racing packages that can take them to the winner's circle on any
given day. We expect each race in 1998 to be a fight to the finish between the most competitive drivers and
teams in the worid. With a completely redesigned chassis, suspension and aerodynamic set-up, expectations
are high for Marlboro Team Penske (MTP) to be on the podium throughout the season. The Marlboro 500
will close out the 1998 series and we look forward to seeíng a Marlborò Team Penske driver crowned Series
Champion.
The following is an overview of the 1998 Marlboro Racing Promotion Program.
Team Sponsorship
• Marlboro Racing will sponsor a two-car team in 1998 with drivers Al Unser Jr. and Andre Ribeiro. Both
drivers will pilot identical 1998 Marlboro Penske Mercedes in the Fed Ex Championship Series. Unser Jr.
will drive the #2 car and Ribeiro will drive the #3 car. Roger Penske is the team owner and Rick Mears will
continue to serve as team advisor.
Driver Record:
Al Unser Jr. (#2) - 1994 Indy 500 Champion.
- 1994 PPG World Series Champion.
- 1992 Indy 500 Champion.
- 1990 PPG World Series Champion.
Andre Ribeiro (#3) - 1996 Marlboro 500 winner and Rio 400 winner.
- 1995 Loudon winner from the pole.
- 1994 Finished runner-up in PPG-Firestone Indy Lights Series en route to
"Rookie of the Year" honors.
Title Event Sponsorships
The third annual Marlboro Grand Prix of Miami is set to run at the world class Homestead Motorsports
Complex. A 1.5 mile oval race track in Homestead, Florida, about thirty minutes south of Miami.
The twelfth annual Marlboro 500 is scheduled to complete the season and potentially crown the Series
Champion at the California Speedway in Fontana, CA. Constructed by Roger Penske, this state of the art
oval track promises to be The Event of the year.
