SubjeCt: REGION 5 - DRS WEEKLY HIGHLIGHTS
CAMBRIDGE
We continue to hold distribution brought about through the
Cambridge Blitz. In most instances, product is selling
quite well with some complaints on Menthol. We may
eventually lose some Menthol distribution, however, we
expect to maintain the majority. Positive efforts being
made with value centers, however, space remains a problem
in selected markets.
MARLBORO MENTHOL LIGHTS BOX
Distribution is 95% on regular and 90% on both packings of
lights box. Recent supermarket audits showing excellent
distribution with slightly improving sales. current
promotions impacting both distribution and inventory
levels. The 2F1's generating visibility and trial. With
continued support, we should hold most distribution and
continue to increase awareness and trial.
ALPINE
The National direct account launch going extremely well
with no major turndowns. It is estimated that 908 of
direct accounts will accept product before January 3ist.
