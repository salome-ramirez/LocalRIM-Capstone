SUBJECT: Nielsen Retail Pricing Update - 12/10/94 (week ending)
SUMMARY
Pack price gap decreased to lowest level since mid-year (58¢ or 43.6%).
Basic's price disadvantage with the Lowest Discount brand decreased to 80%.
<99¢ activity decreased to 15%.
KEY FINDINGS
Prices and Gaps
In C-stores, Marlboro's average price decreased -1¢ to $1.91 as Flex Fund activity
increased +4 points while the average Lowest Discount pack price increased +1¢ to
$1.33, its highest level since 11/5, as money-off promotional support decreased on all
Discount brands except GPC and Montclair. The resulting price gap decreased to 58¢
or 43.6%, its lowest level since 6/25.
Regionally, flex fund activity increased in Regions 1, 2, and 3, causing Marlboro's
average price to decline. Price gaps have decreased on both a one week and four week
basis in virtually all Regions.
