Summary
A joint hearing of the House and Senate Veterans’ Affairs Committees met on March 25 to hear
testimony of representatives of AMVETS, American Ex-Prisoners of War, Vietnam Veterans of
America and the Retired Officers Association.
Statements by House and Senate members at the hearing shed little new light on the issue of the
$17 billion line item in the President’s FY99 budget for paying benefits to veterans for service
cornected, smoking related illnesses.
Below is a summary of tobacco related remarks made by members of the two committees.
Representative Stump (R-AZ)
Chairman Stump made VA compensation for tobacco related illnesses the main topic of his brief
remarks. He reiterated past statements, saying that veterans should be included in any tobacco
legislation.
Senator Specter (R-PA)
Specter opened his statement by saying that the overall FY99 budget for the VA was insufficient.
He repeated his past remarks that if the budgeted $17 billion is not ultimately used for paying
tobacco claims, it should be kept within the VA system and spent for other operating expenses.
