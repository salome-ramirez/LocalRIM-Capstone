Dear Member:
With this letter I am sending you 5 packs of each of two different test cigarettes
which I hope you will enjoy testing for me. I would appreciate your doing this test
in the following way:
1. All of these cigarettes should be smoked only by the person whose name appears
on the label above. This is because I would like a very select group of people
to try these cigarettes.
2. Please start by smoking 3 or 4 packs of the cigarettes coded E3 (and no others).
As soon as you have finished smoking them, please fill out the answers to Part
I of the report form on the back of this page.
3. Then smoke 3 or 4 packs of S7 (and no others) and answer Part II of the report
form.
4. Finally, smoke some of the remaining cigarettes of both codes E3 and S7 and
answer Part III.
5. About ten days from the time you received these cigarettes, we will telephone
you to get your reactions to them. Please keep the completed report form
handy. This will make it easier for you to tell us about your reactions to the
test cigarettes when we telephone you.
That's all there is to it! I hope you can start this test right away, as I am most
eager to have your opinions.
Cordially,
Jauet
Janet Hall, Director
