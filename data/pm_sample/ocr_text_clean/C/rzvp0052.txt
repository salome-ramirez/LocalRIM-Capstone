Dear Ms. Lancaster:
Your November 7, 1995 letter regarding the complaint filed with your office by Mr.
Richard Hughes has been forwarded to me for investigation and response.
Comprehensive searches conducted by Philip Morris and third parties retained in
connection with the fulfillment of orders for Marlboro Country Store Gear have failed to disclose
evidence of an order for Mr. Hughes. On August 25, 1995 Mr. Hughes telephoned our Consumer
Affairs Department to advise us that he had not received the lighter that he had ordered. In
response, on August 28, 1995, we mailed to Mr. Hughes our standard age verification form for
him to certify that he is an adult smoker 21 years of age or older and requested a copy of his
canceled check. Mr. Hughes telephoned again on September 5, 1995 to say that he had not
received our letter. Investigation of this complaint revealed that the August 28, 1995 mailing to
Mr. Hughes had been returned to us as undeliverable. It was remailed on September 8, 1995.
We received Mr. Hughes' age verification form and copy of his check via return mail on
September 15, 1995. Upon receipt, our Consumer Affairs Department arranged for shipment of
one Marlboro Country Store Lighter to Mr. Hughes. It was sent via United States Mail on October
11, 1995. A follow-up call was made to the Hughes residence by our Consumer Affairs
Department and delivery of the lighter was confirmed by telephone on October 17, 1995.
In addition, our Consumer Affairs Department mailed one coupon, redeemable at retail for
a free carton of Philip Morris cigarettes, to Mr. Hughes on September 18, 1995 in consideration
for any inconvenience,
We trust that this information answers any question you may have concerning this
complaint.
