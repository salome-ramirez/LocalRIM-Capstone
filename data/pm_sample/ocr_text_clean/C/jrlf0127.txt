SubJeCT: MARLBORO MONTHLY SHARE ANALYSIS - MAY VERSUS APRIL
NATIONAL OVERVIEW
Marlboro's May share was the highest its been since 3rd quarter 1990 at
24.22%, but is still below year-ago share. One month share gains were the
second strongest in the industry behind PM's black and white business as
Marlboro continued its growth momentum, largely due to promotional support
in pack and carton outlets. Gains were across all non-menthol packings,
driven by strong growth in the southeast and west.
Marlboro share resumed growth in Supermarkets in response to the $2.00
off-carton price feature promotion. According to Infoscan, share peaked
at 20.9%, matching share during the year-end coupon drop. Weekly share
has fallen off more quickly from the May promotion, however, than after
the direct program. It is unclear what the cumulative effect on
Supermarket share trend will be.
The 3-pack t-shirt promotion helped Marlboro's share in Grocery outlets
(+0.4 pts vs. April), but share in Convenience was off for the month, as
declines in Regions 2 and 3 offset gains in the balance of the country.
Dramatic PV growth in these two regions (+2.9 and +1.8 share pts,
respectively) stunted Marlboro's growth potential.
Menthol's share, which has grown steadily since early 1990, declined for
the third consecutive month. This is concurrent with improved performance
for the non-menthol packings, and may reflect less visibility for Menthol
as we promote the mainline business.
COMPETITIVE SHARE PERFORMANCE
Although Price Value's share continued to grow, gaining +0.6 points in
May, Marlboro's increased promotional activity appears to have slowed the
category's momentum. In Supermarkets, the category declined -0.2 points.
In Region 4, where PV is highly developed and has been showing the
strongest growth, the category declined -0.3 points.
Camel Filter's May share remained flat versus April but grew . pts
versus YAG. Full Flavor and Lights have resumed growth at the/expense of
Ultra Lights as Camel refocuses on a pack-oriented, young adult strategy.
Promotional support has returned to convenience outlets and advertising
has shifted to 99's, the Hard Pack and mainline Full Flavor and Lights
Camel Joe. In the west, Camel's traditional stronghold, Filter and Lights 2
are regaining lost share.
