In 1988, Philip Morris and other companies founded the Center
for Indoor Air Research (CIAR) to sponsor high caliber research in the
field of indoor air quality. Since most people spend much of their lives
indbors, we are concerned that more should be known about this
important subject. The Center has prospered and gained favorable
standing within the scientific community. Seven eminent scientists are
on CIAR's advisory board and the Center now funds projects at major
research centers. We are proud of CIAR's progress and hope to enlist
other companies in this effont.
