Philip Morris U.S.A. CONFIDENTIAL
Credibility Core Strategies:
Develop as many venues as possible for the objective publication and presentation of
high quality scientific results through identification of existing venues and exploration of
options that are currently closed to us.
Develop optimum approaches to retaining consultants. This will include the
development of challenging scientific issues, and the development of agreements that will
stress consultant quality and objectivity.
Accommodation of Smokers and Non-Smokers Core Strategies:
Ensure that WSA is aware of all existing technology that could be useful for
accommodation of smokers and non-smokers, and develop new technology to fill in identified
gaps.
Ensure that we are aware of all developing regulations, devise meaningful methods of
evaluating levels of ETS, sponsor research to enhance our knowledge of ETS and indoor air
quality, and generate and promote the acquisition of high quality data that will be utilized in
making reasonable data-based decisions on reasonable levels of smoking within the
framework of regulations.
Identification and Prioritization of Scientific Issues and Preparation of Scientific Positions
Core Strategies
Develop a limited list of scientific issues that are important to our partners, develop
scientific reviews, and develop processes for rapid responses on key issues.
Determine what important technical areas are not currently covered by WSA and hire
scientists/consultants to address these areas. Develop a process which will ensure that
concise scientific positions are rapidly prepared on issues for which the company is likely to
have need and identify those issues where preparation of a detailed position is necessary.
Identification of Emerging Issues Core Strategy
Carry out a detailed situation analysis of emerging issues followed by periodic updates
in order to be able to identify those that are most likely to become important issues in the
future.
Global Communication Process and Integrated Information Management System Core
Strategies
Develop a global communication process that is completely compatible with the
systems in use throughout the Department.
Develop a fully integrated information management system that is compatible with all
WSA offices and develop centralized filing systems which can be accessed globally and which
allow easy retrieval of required information.
Communication of Scientific Information and Positions Core Strategies
Develop a process with our partners that will allow the communication of scientific
information in a concise and understandable manner that maintains scientific accuracy.
Obtain information from our corporate partners as to the level of detail required for
different types of communications and ensure that required information meets these
standards.
Develop a process to ensure that scientific components of company positions are
periodically checked to ensure that changes in the science that may have taken place have
not called these positions into question.
Develop a process that will allow scientists to present scientific positions to non-
scientific audiences and train certaín key personnel in order to improve communications skills.
Worldwide Integration Core Strategies
Develop a process, based upon a reliable central information source, that will allow
rapid identification of and response to key regional issues.
Develop required contacts within regions or markets that will allow communication in
the appropriate cultural context for that market or region, and to educate regional personnel
with regard to the issues.
