SubjeCt: SIT CEI #37 UPDATE
Attached for your review is the documentation of the ACT
"STAGE" of PDCA for SIT C&I #37, (component usage) along with
Action Plan SIT agreed to in the *CißCk" stage.
As you recall the team objective was to demonstrate with
TQM's that we do on average feed the blend formula assigned by
the Leaf Department (deviation to average within +/- .1o units
from 0.0).
After analyzing the post measures of effectiveness, two of
the component groupings still failed to meet the +/- .10 percent
deviation objective. They were VST and KST. The blend deviation
for these groups was +1.11 and -1.42 respectively.
It should be noted that during the data collection period no
adjustments were made to the pullsheet loading sequences for the
VST and KST component groups. The data indicates that by
computing the number of containers required in a sequence for the
VST and KST groups separately, a larger percent of deviation from
targeted usage occurs.
To reduce this deviation the team feels that the Excel
program used to configure pullsheets needs to be expanded so that
all the stem components can be computed together instead of
separately, this should eliminate the large imbalance between the
two groups and may eliminate the need to "adjust" pullsheets to
regain balance between the VST and KST groups.
Therefore the teams decision in A3b is to expand the Excel
program and use the new methodology to configure the pullsheets
needed for the upcoming blend change, collect data for four weeks
and then analyze the data using the methods used for the team
measures of effectiveness.
