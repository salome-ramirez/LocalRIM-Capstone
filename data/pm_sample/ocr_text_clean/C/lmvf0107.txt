Analysis of the optical and electronic properties of
supported metal oxids catalysts has been continued to
elucidate the surface structure of potential selective
filtrants. The Ni/Al₂O3 absorbance spectrum has been
partially resolved into five Gaussian components. The
Cu/AlzOç absorbance spectrum has been analyzed in terms
of a tetragonal distortion of the spinel lattice structure
Simultaneous crystal field fits for the tetrahedrul and
octahedral sites are being attempted. Preliminary progress
in this research has been propared for publication (1) and
oral presentation (2,3)
First sets of data from the variable parameter smoking
machine have been fit with continuous smooth curves. Pressure
drop-flow characteristics and TPM delivery data were fit for
the Marlboro monitor 15 with a variety of filters. Computer
programs wore written and tested for calculation of TPM, H,O
tar, and pressure drop as a function of time during a puff
Initial trials indicate a close correlation with values
obtained from the human smoking simulator.
