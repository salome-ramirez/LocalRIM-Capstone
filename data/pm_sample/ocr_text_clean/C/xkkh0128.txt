Gary, SSDs & Nick - SECTION 41
In my staff meeting yesterday, we discussed the upcoming FLR/Market Decision Audits, and
I wanted to pass along the preliminary details thus far:
• NRM Promotional Audit - This will be conducted by Market Decisions in 100 NRM
accounts across each Region, in two designated markets, from April through July. This audit
will examine the presence of the Basic B2G1F and Marlboro B4G1F promotional product at
retail.
• OPB Promotional Audit - This will be conducted by Market Decisions in 75 accounts (to
include both RM and NRM) across one market per Section. (NOTE: Section 43 will not be
included in the audit due to the low share and development levels of OPB.) The audit will
examine the incidence of any OPB promotion as well as the presence of permanent and
temporary OPB POS. This audit will occur from the last week in April until the end of May.
• June Price Promotion Audit - This will be conducted by Full Line Research (FLR). The
audit will examine the incidence and presence of the June buy down and corresponding
placement of POS. The audit is scheduled to occur from the week before Memorial Day until
one week after the Fourth of July holiday. To date, no further details regarding the audit
panel size and geographic market coverage has been shared.
Once I receive additional information, I will pass it along.
-Suzanne
