Objective 
To investigate smoking topography of K series EHCSS and Marlboro Lights (MLs) with 
CreSSmicro measurement device and to examine variability in measurement between dry 
air and particulate (smoke). Using 2 second puff. 
Materials 
Cigarettes 
ML to be supplied by Sensory Research 
K series EHCSS to be supplied by Ashland 
CreSSmicro device Series 2 
To be supplied by Sensory Research 
Setting scale factor 
Set scale factor using 50- 80cc (1 sec 50cc square and V% sine wave puffs on smoking 
machine puff), check with 30 and 80ml. 
Variables 
Puff Volume (Particulate=35, 60, 85 Air=20, 35,40, 50, 60, 85, 100), Cigarette 
Smoke(CS, with or without), Puff Profile (sine, square of Vz triangle), Cigarette (ML or 
K), Number of CreSS devices (3), Number of Cigarettes, Threshold setting (factory 
setting=20). 
Topography Parameters 
Puff Volume (PV), Puff Duration (PD), Peak Flow (PF), Puff Number (PN), Inter-Puff-
Interval (IPI)= 28 seconds (2scc puff).
