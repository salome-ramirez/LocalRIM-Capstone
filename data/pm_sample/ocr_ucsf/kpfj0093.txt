Dear Chuck :
As I advised you by telephone yesterday, we take serious
exception to the August 25, 1995 open letter from James J . Morgan
to the readers of the New York Times published this Sunday and
believe that that letter constitutes a clear breach of paragraph
5 of the settlement agreement . The reasons are as follows :
1 . In his letter, Mr . Morgan is clearly making statements in
the press on behalf of Philip Morris about the settlement
and the statement . The agreement mandates that any such
comment be made only by you .
2 . The agreement provides that neither party will "characterize
or embellish the correction statement in any way or expand
upon the correction statement ." Mr . Morgan's open letter
violated this provision in a number of respects . First his
letter charges that "ABC didn't apologize for
overstatements" . I can think of no clearer example of a
breach of our fundamental understanding that neither side
would make comments either trivializing or aggrandizing the
scope of the correction . Second, Mr . Morgan states "ABC
would not have acknowledged its error had we not initiated a
libel action against them" . As you know we believe that
assertion to be false . But more basically, we agreed that
both sides would refrain from making assertions of this
nature about the other . We have refrained thus far from
making any comment to the press about what we surmise
motivated this lawsuit or your decision to settle at this
juncture . We expect that you will do the same . T i d, Mr .
