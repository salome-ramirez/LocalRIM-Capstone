David Sachs of the Palo Alto (Calif.) Center for Pulmonary Disease
Prevention offered supporting evidence that the 5 mg per day was an
reasonable addiction threshold. Analysis of the expetiences of users
of nicotine patches indicated that 5 mg a day does maintain
physiological dependence, Sachs said .
At the end of the day, a majority of the panel voted that some
threshhold existed, but that there was insufficient evidence to
determine the exact amount of nicotine that causes dependence .
