4. Charger Operational Conformance
Operational conformance to specification for the "B" series charger design was
evaluated by sampling plan via the following tests :
075CC2P Lighter Slot Recharge Time Test
062CC1 P Exit Air Temperature Safety Test
064CC1 P Charger Case Temperature Test
019CC1P Cleaning Power Interruption Response Test
052CC1P Lighter/Battery Installation into Charger
Lighter Slot Recharpe Time

installed in the lighter) does not exceed 40 minutes. Revision 2P of this test also collected
informational data on other charger functions as indicated below:
