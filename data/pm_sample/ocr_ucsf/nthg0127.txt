TO : Select Field Managers DATE : November 30, 1990
FROM : Doug Nelson
SUBJECT : Parliament Pack CD/Cassette Promotion
During the February Sales Cycle, Parliament is sponsoring a
CD/Cassette offer in select markets and Region I . The CD's and
Cassettes are entitled "Parliament Platinum Collection II" featuring
recent hits from Michael Bolton, Paul Young, The Moody Blues, et .al .
Rationale
The objectives of this promotion are to generate trial among
competitive smokers, increase purchase and to reward current
Parliament smokers .
Consumer Offer
Consumers will receive a free CD or Cassette depending on the
following specifications :
3 Pack CD offer -- Region I
2 Pack CD offer -- Los Angeles, San Francisco, San Diego,
Hawaii and Chicago
2 Pack Cassette -- Region I, Los Angeles, San Francisco, San
Diego, Hawaii and Chicago
Note : The use of CD's or Cassettes should be based on high CD or
Cassette penetration in each market .
Additionally, a bounceback offer is available to consumers for a Sharp
remote control CD player or a Sharp non remote control CD player . The
cost is $85 .00 and 10 UPC's (plus $4 .00 shipping and handling) for the
remote control CD player and $75 .00 for the non remote control CD
player . The expiration date is May 31, 1991 or while supplies last .
Displays/Payment
20-unit display - CD $2 .00
20-unit display - Cassette $2 .00
Product Mix
Parliament Lights Kings Box only will be featured for this promotion .
