Dear Mary Ellen: 
Ken may be able to provide more information, but here is what I recall. 
Because we sold the idea of prediction as an Industry, it made sense to have a combined equation so you had a larger 
sample size from which to develop the equation and you did not raise issues such as "why is your equation different from 
Company X" which would lead to questions like why are your brands different from Company Y. 
This is similar to the benchmarking for smoke constituents where we are trying to sell the idea that the majority of the 
brands have similar characteristics such that one equation covers the majority of brands. 
Hope this helps.
