Project Objective :
To test the hypothesis that ETS exposure has an adverse effect on pregnancy
outcome .
Project Summary :
Prospective study using nested study design . Women enrolled within 12 weeks of
last menstrual period are invited to join an intensively monitored group with
four exposure assessments during pregnancy . All other study participants are
randomized either to biochemical monitoring which involves three exposure
assessments, or telephone monitoring which involves a single assessment .
