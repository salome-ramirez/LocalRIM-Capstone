I received your recent letter and appreciate your taking the time
to share your views with me regarding cigarette advertising . I am sorry
we disagree on this matter .
In the fifteen years since the original Surgeon General's Report on
Smoking and Healithi, scientists have gathered an overwhelming amount of
data on the consequences of cigarette smoking on the health of the
American people . The most recent report not only reaffirms the original
findings but points to an alarmingly high increase in smoking by young
people . In .addi'tion, the report concludes that smoking is the leading
preventable cause of premature death in this country .
Smoking is a legitimate government concern when health costs of
treating cancer caused by cigarette smoking is $5 billion a year and at
least $12 billion is wasted'in lost productivity and wages
.
In light of the findings in the Surgeon General's report, I will
support steps by HEW, the FTC and others to discourage young people from
starting to smoke and to encourage current smokers to quit .
Thank you again for taking the time to write . I hope you will
continue to ;advise me from time toltime onimatters of particular concern
