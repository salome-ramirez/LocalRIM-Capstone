Dear Wayne :
This is in reference to your NOW advertisement
appearing in The Hartford Courant on Wednesday, August 9,
1989, p . A12, and all similar ads .
Your advertisement falsely and inaccurately
misrepresents the nicotine delivery of our new product
NEXT as "0 .1 mg ." . The correct nicotine measurement is 0 .08
mg . We have clearly set forth this measurement in our
advertising . We have also clearly marked our packs and
advertising with the disclosure that NEXT contains "less
than 0 .1 mg ."
Moreover, NOW's nicotine delivery is 0 .14 mg ., an
amount 75% higher than NEXT . Accordingly, we regard your
assertions that NOW is "still,the lowest in nicotine and
tar" as equally false and misfeading .
As you know, a test market of a new product is a very
sensitive endeavor . As NEXT is a revolutionary new product,
accurate test results are essential . Your advertising
campaign is interfering with our ability to get vital,
accurate information and causing us irreparable harm .
Your advertising constitutes product disparagement in
violation of common law and-various state statutes as well
as false and misleading advertising in violation of Sect .
43(a) of the Lanham Act . We demand that Reynolds
immediately cease and desist all such improper advertising
and take all available steps to correct the misimpressions
already created by your advertising .
