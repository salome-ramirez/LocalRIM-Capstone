Per our conversation, below listed are several of the maintenance and service issues that 
need to be addressed prior to the scheduled train operation (promotional or scheduled.) 
The daily servicing will consist of locomotive and power car fuel, and fuel for any 
auxiliary generators on the passenger cars. In addition, potable water for the passenger 
cars, trash disposal, exterior washing and toilet servicing will be required on a periodic 
basis. 
Fuel - If the host railroads have the capability and are willing, it is recommended that 
they perform the service. If facilities are not available, local fuel contractors 
will be used on an as needed basis. 
Water - In that virtually all potable watering facilities have been removed (other than 
Amtrak's main service points,) logjd contractors will provide during layover 
times i.e. Cheyenne, Denver, Idaho Falls, Livingston and Billings. 
Trash Removal and Toilet Servicing - Local contractors will be employed as required. 
Exterior Washing - This will be dependent on weather conditions. In the interest of 
keeping appearance first class, I estimate a wash will be required twice each 
week by a contractor. 
It is understood that the undersigned with the assistance of Rader Railcar will arrange for 
the servicing of the train. In addition, it will be periodically necessary to stock each car 
with food and supplies. That will be coordinated with "onboard supervision" during 
layovers and/or servicing. 
Scheduled maintenance of the locomotives and cars is dictated by usage and the Federal 
Railroad Administration. (FRA)
