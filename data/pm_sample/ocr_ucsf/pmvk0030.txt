Fair Access to Indemnity and Reimbursement
Small-business ownets often cannot afford the qualified legal representation
necessary to defend themselves against labor relations board charges. This act
amends the National Labor Relations Act to reimburse a small business or labor
organization prevailing against a labor board action the atmmey's fees and
expenses used to defend itself.
The act applies to any employer with 100 employees or less, and a net worth of
no more than $1.4 million.
"Filing and pursuing a NLRB charge is essentially cost-free to the charging
party and extremely expensive for the respondent;" said Peter Rousos, director
of corporate human relations for Gaylord Entertainment Corp. in Nashville,
Tenn.
