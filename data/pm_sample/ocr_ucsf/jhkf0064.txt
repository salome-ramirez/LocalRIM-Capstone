CIGARETTE ADDITIVES
Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company introduced Natural American Spirit Cigarettes and Tobacco Products as an aftema-
tive to other commercially-produced brands which typically contain other ingredients in addition to
tobacco . These manufacturers may add sugar or glycerine, propytene glycol, reconstituted scrap tobacco,
processed stems, chemically-derived synthetic flavor enhancers, preservatives for longer shelf-life, and other unnatural
agents refened to as 'burn accelerators .' We add none of these things to Natural American Spirit Ggarettes and
Tobacco Products .
For one view on the issue of cigarette additives, please refer to the enclosed article, "What Goes Up In Smoke,'
reprinted from The Nation Magazine .
