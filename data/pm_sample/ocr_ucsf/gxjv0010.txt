There must be a huge communication gap between us, so let me
clarify a number of items which you are concerned about.
1.
Glenn Smith Presents, Inc. is contracted by Marlboro Music to
book their talent and coordinate their needs with the artists. We
also provide what promotional support and consultation that we can
for the fairs.
However, we cannot and do not directly solicit
radio stations to accomplish what is your job, nataely, advertising
the concert.
This is due to the fact that we fall under certain
federal regulations where Marlboro is concerned, which limit our
ability to advertise, promote, solicit, or publicize this concert
with radio or television media. All of this has been explained to
you before and this should not be a surprise. You need only look
as far as your Marlboro contract to discover their limitations, and
these also apply to GSPI.
2.
In terms of promotional effort, I have told you already on
numerous occassions what we are providing. Once again, there will
be an autographed guitar from Rick Trevino, and the winner and one
guest will receive backstage passes to meet Rick. I will execute
the meet and greet. The guitar will be shipped to your attention,
and you will be asked to have it delivered backstage to the
production office upon my arrival. Further, I can provide you with
a meet and greet with Diamond Rio for one winner and guest (no
guitar involved here).
Your radio station(s) can get CD's for
giveaways from their regional record reps. You should provide the
stations with tickets for giveaways.
All of this needs to be
coordinated between you and your radio station(s).
I
have no
direct involvement with it, other than to offer it to you and
execute it day of show. Further, I will coordinate a maximum of
two emcees on stage prior to the show to introduce the artists and
welcome everyone to the concert. Once again, you need to commit to
who you want to work with, and then let me know and I will execute
this backstage. No banners, no inflatables, no radio jukeboxes, no
signage of any manner will be allowed in the venue, unless it has
been approved by Jose Fontanez of Marlboro Music in advance.
