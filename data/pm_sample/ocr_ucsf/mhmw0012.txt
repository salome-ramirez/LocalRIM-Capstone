A meeting has been scheduled for Thursday, January 5 in the New York Oft-ice, conference
room 8.41.A2, commencing at 9:00 AM and ending by 1:00 PM. The meeting is designed

and relate it to point of sale materials - both permanent and temporary - required by Field
Sales to establish and maintain a strong Marlboro presence at retail.
In addition, we hope to identify the types and dimensions of POS that best service Field
Sales needs. As many of you know, we are working to minimize custom POS and replace
it with standard items that are supplied to accounts on a timely basis.
We therefore need a
Field Sales perspective on what will work at retail.
In order to adequately represent the Regions, each Trade Marketing Manager should survey
the SAM's and Account Managers in their Region to solicit the types of materials the
majority of their accounts can and will use to support Marlboro at retail. Imagery, price
leadership, and general brand presence are all part of the POS equation that we want to
have available for sales to utilize.
