This meeting will be reported on by the various
members of Philip Morris that attended in the
following manner :
(1) W. F . Kuhn will report on Freon chemistry and
analytical methods .
(2) R . Fagan will report on toxicity .
(3) Henry Merritt will report on process discussions .
(4) Du Pont representatives will supply a summary of
their meeting minutes .
The meeting opened up with discussions of the history
of marketing of Freon fluorocarbons with discussions of
their properties, toxicities and uses . The morning session
was generally devoted to chemistry and toxicity of fluoro-
carbons ; the afternoon session was a discussion of
