Dear Chuck: 
In response to your study requests dated May 13, 2002 for conducting genetic toxicology studies (mouse 
lymphoma, rnicronucleus, cytotox, and Ames assays) and smoke chemistry studies for Project 
Methylcyclopentenolone, IIT Research Institute (IITRI) is pleased to submit this letter proposal for your review 
and approval. The proposed studies, with the exception of the rnicronucleus assay, will be conducted on five 
types of cigarettes (three with various levels of methylcyclopentenolone, 1 Control cigarette, and the 2R4F 
Research cigarette). For the rnicronucleus assay, only four types of cigarettes (all except the 2R4F) will be used. 
The draft protocols will be submitted later for your review.
