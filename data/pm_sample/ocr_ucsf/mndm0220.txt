abstract: In the space below, please provide a descriptive summary of your proposed research project. 
The pathogenesis of heart failure is still only poorly understood. The transition from compensated hypertrophy 
to failure ;is accompanied by a progressive apoptotic loss of cardiomyocytes. Interestingly, stimuli of 
hypertrophy as well as apoptosis share the APT signaling complex as a central regulatory element. AP-I is a 
composite transcription factor,1 whose subunit composition and interaction with other transcription factors may 
vary. It cari be hypothesized that under conditions favoring cardiac growth or apoptosis the composition of the 
AP-l complex differs and, due to this difference, different APT controlled pathways become activated. 
Variations in AP-1 transcriptipn complexes may trigger the transition from hypertrophy to apoptosis in the 
myocardial'cells. 
It is the central aim of this project to identify on the cellular level the different compositions of AP-1 complexes 
under hypertrophy and apoptosis inducing conditions."Isolated cardiomyocytes from adult rat are used as 
model. The a-adrenoceptor agqnist phenylephrine and the NO-donor SNAP will be used as examplatory stimuli 
for induction of AP-1 mediated hypertrophy and apoptosis, respectively. The expression of AP-1 components, 
of interacting transcription factors, of target genes controlled by these specific AP-I complexes and AP-1 
driven "downstream"" genes specific for one or the other kind of stimulus will be identified with suitable 
molecular techniques. The causal roles of transcription factors and other genes will be tested with use of decoy 
or antisenseioligonucleotides. It will also be tested if adenoviral overexpression of identified pro-apoptotic AP-
1 components or dependent genes can transform hypertrophic stimuli into apoptotic processes or if pro-
hyp ertrophic genes protect the pells against apoptotic stimuli. Finally/ the relevance of the results obtained in 
the cellular model will be evaluated in an established in vivo model of cardiac hypertrophy and failure, i.e. the 
SHR-sp rat.; The expression of the previously identified transcription factors and other genes, related to 
hypertrophy or apoptosis, will b'e analyzed in these rats at the stages of compensated hypertrophy, transition to 
heart failure";and at manifest failure. A provocation test performed on hearts isolated from these animals will 
functionally demonstrate their relative propensity for apoptosis.

switch for induction of hypertrophy or apoptosis and heart failure.
