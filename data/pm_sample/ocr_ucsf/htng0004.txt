SUBJECT:
Cartier 10's Pack Graphics
DATE:
July 17, 1989
Given time, we think that the end panel graphics of the 10's pack
can be made similar to the 20's end panels.
However, we will not know this for sure until:
A)
Our Engineering department redesigns the end panel feeder an
the packer such that all top panels with "Cartier" are
oriented the same way. Presently, if we were to use
"Cartier" graphics, 50% of "Cartier" top panels would be
upside down.
B)
We know more about the quality of leaf stamping on plastic,
color as well as registration wise. Needless to say, our
experience with leaf stamping on plastic is limited. We -
simply don't know how good the registration is going to be
with the addition of a second color.
We have directed our Operation people to start with the
modification.
Obviously, we will not be able to do this in time
for the October launch in L.A., but our Operations people think
that they could do it for the national launch next year.
In view of the foregoing, we have no other choice than to stay
with the Cartier logo in gold for both the top and bottom panels
on the 10's pack.
There is one issue concerning the Cartier logo that we need
Cartier's input.
Because we'll not have the red or green color,
should we do only one or two oval lines outside the gold chain
link?
(See copy attach,ed).
We need their input as soon as
possible.
