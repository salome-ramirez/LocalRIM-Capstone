DEVELOPMENT PROJECT SUMMAR Y
TITLE : Low Lam-ina Blend s
OBJECTIVE : Develop and Evaluate Low Lamina Blends
CURRENT STATUS :
Virginia Panel testing of LLB (VP-4708)vs . both Basic and Merit showed
promising results . Low delivery smokers preferred LLB over Basic (< .08), and
full flavor smokers showed no significant differences in preference . Final
results vs . Merit showed no significant difference in preference for full flavor
or low delivery smokers . POL tests of this LLB model vs . Basic, Merit, and
Decade are in progress . A secondflavored LLB model (VP-4712) was also preferred
over Basic by low delivery smokers and was found to be more acceptable and rated
higher on the purchase probability scale .
Current work also involves development of 3 and 1-2 mg delivery models .
POL testing of a 3 mg~LLB model vs . Kent III was requested .
Tests of a 5 mg menthol LLB and a 5 mg LLB with WS against Merit menthol'
have been scheduled .
