MEDIOM VENDING DISTRIBUTION DRIVE PROGRAM ELEMENTS
1.
SUMMARY
Beginning in May and continuing through July 4, Marlboro will conduct a Medium vending distribution
drive in 5 top U.S. markets. The objective of this program is to ensure immediate Medium
availability/presence in smoker-friendly, social settings and to enhance Medium's legitimacy and popular
brand image. The program will be targeted to trend-setting, metro markets and communication to the
vending trade will be via direct mail.

III.
PROGRAM EXECUTION
A complete Medium vending distribution kit will be mailed to each of the targeted vendors (See attached
Vendor List). Each kit will contain a letter explaining the distribution drive, a certification
statement/invoice, a list of PM USA sales offices, a Racing Watch and Medium vending labels and
headers in-line with the number of machines that each vendor has (See attached Materials Ship List).
Vending distribution kits will be assembled in KDC and mailed to the 96 targeted vendors by April 30th.
