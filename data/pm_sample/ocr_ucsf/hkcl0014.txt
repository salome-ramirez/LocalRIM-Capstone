Region 2 - Nielsen Audit
As you requested, enclosed please fmd a confidential listing of the cooperation status of
major chains in the Nielsen audit panel within your Region. Please note this only
represents chain retailers in your geography, with the status of independents (which can not
be disclosed due to retailer disclosure agreements) varying widely.
Also enclosed, please find a comparison of Region 2 performance based on shipment and
Nielsen audit data Key findings from this comparison include:
•
Both data sources indicated fairly consistent trends for PM volume, PM Premium and
total Category share performance.
•
However, total PM share appears understated in Nielsen. This primarily reflects a
widening disparity between reported performance for PM Discount products in Nielsen
versus shipments. Nielsen does not appear to be fully capturing the growth of PM
Discount entries due to the account specific nature of discount expansion, particularly in
the more volatile Deep Discount segment The integration of the Nielsen expansion
panel (see below for details) in 1993 should help minimize this distortion.
•
During the last several months, Marlboro's share trend appears stronger in shipments
than Nielsen.
However, due to trade program activity, shipments are likely being
overstated, while Nielsen may be providing a more representative "leading indicator" of
the franchise's weakening performance.
Nielsen Audit Expansion
Effective with January 1993 data, the Nielsen audit service will be enhanced to include
expanded sampling of Convenience stores (including additional Gas Convenience outlets
and IvTmi Marts) and representation of other trade classes including Mass Merchandisers,
Liquor stores and Urban pack outlets. Each of these expansion trade classes will include at
least one full year of back history to allow for trended reporting and 12mm aggregations.
Region 2's sample size will be increased by nearly 27% (109 stores) with the largest
increase occurring in the Convenience trade class. See the attached summary for details on
audit expansion by Section for your Region.
