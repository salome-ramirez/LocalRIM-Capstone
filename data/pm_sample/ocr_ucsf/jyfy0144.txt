Dear Marty :
As you are keenly aware, the Supermarket Trade Class is currently struggling with how
best to merchandise the tobacco category . The current situation can be summarized as
follows :
• Overall tobacco sales are declining in the Supermarket Trade Class
• Pilferage is eroding supermarket profits in the tobacco category
• The threat of increased tobacco taxes will make tobacco merchandising
and profitability even more difficult
• Consumers are dissatisfied with the tobacco shopping experience in
supermarkets .
Philip Morris suggests that tobacco needs to be perceived as a separate distinct category
within supermarkets, with profitability as a measurement versus merchandising dollars .
Additionally, the category needs in-store ownership by dedicated personnel. Most
importantly, pilferage needs to be addressed in a direct manner .
With these issues in mind, we have developed an alternative for the Supermarket Trade
Class to consider, with the objective of providing maximum choice to the consumer in a
shopper friendly environment, with a minimum of inventory at an acceptable profit level
.
This alternative eliminates pilferage, yet keeps the category visible . The strategy would
be to operate a free standing tobacco "kiosk" profit center in one of two ways :
1. Store dedicated personnel to category
2. Third party operates category, with full financial responsibility ;
store receives rent .
Consider the advantages the in-store kiosk can offer . You satisfy a customer need,
thereby making your store a better shopping option ; pilferage is eliminated . Additionally,
you create a controlled environment that better lends itself to control of sales to minors,
as well as tobacco profitability. If a third party operates the store kiosk, it becomes a
guaranteed risk-free profit center that eliminates costs associated with the category .
