OVERVIEW
The 1997 CART World Series demonstrated the tremendous competition and excitement that currently exists in
open wheel racing in North America . In 1998 the CART World Series will be called the Fed Ex Championship
Series. All the race teams have developed racing packages that can take them to the winner's circle on any
given day . We expect each race in 1998 to be a fight to the finish between the most competitive drivers and
teams in the world. With a completely redesigned chassis, suspension and aerodynamic set-up, expectations
are high for Marlboro Team Penske (MTP) to be on the podium throughout the season . The Marlboro 500
will close out the 1998 series and we look forward to seeing a Marlboro Team Penske driver crowned Series
Champion .
