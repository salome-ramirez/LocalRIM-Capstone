We believe that the duration of Cigatam is to the low
side, particularly taking into consideration the crop
variations, the frequent crop failures, the political
situation, and the difficulties to obtain import
licenses for importation of offshore leaf .
The actual inventory of Flue Cured and Burley tobaccos
is dangerously low . The Sun Cured and Dark tobaccos
are overstocked .
Cigatam will have to absorb, together with La Moderna,
the final Tabamex inventory of tobacco . This inventory
is roughly 7 million kgs of which, Cigatam will take
approximately 3 million kgs . These tobaccos are of
very low quality and as they are composed mainly of
Sun Cured and Dark tobaccos, they will further
exaggerate to overstocked inventory of Cigatam in these
two types .
We were told that the upcoming crop (89/90 crop) is of
average quality, but represents only 50 % of the
volume of a normal crop (25 tons vs . 50 tons) . In
order to face their short tobacco inventory, Cigatam
purchased 670 tons of offshore Flue Cured (not yet in
Mexico), and plans to buy in the coming months 2 .250
tons of Flue Cured strip and 3 .200 tons of Burley
strip .
