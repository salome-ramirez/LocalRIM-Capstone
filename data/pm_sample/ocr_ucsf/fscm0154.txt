Introduction: 
In an effort to respond to questions regarding the appropriate 
light weight reject limit and the maximum rod weight reduction 
achievable while maintaining the integrity and quality of our 
product, the following test plan is submitted for review. 
Objective: 
1. To determine the appropriate cigarette rod light weight 
reject limit, i.e. at what lighter than target cigarette rod 
weight is the quality impacted to the point where the 
product becomes unacceptable and should be rejected. 
2. 
To determine the maximum amount of filler weight that can be 
removed from the target weight cigarettes such as with the 
ATM enabled before quality or machinability "problems" start 
to 
occur and make the cigarette 
undesirable for our 
customers. 
3. 
To determine if there is any significant difference in the 
quality of the cigarettes that are rejected at the light 
weight reject limit (-67 mg) when compared to cigarettes 
that are made at a target weight that corresponds to the 
light weight reject limit (target weight - 67 mg).

c)

cigarettes other than Marlboro, i.e. two different 
blends should be processed on the test maker, Marlboro 
and a second blend.
